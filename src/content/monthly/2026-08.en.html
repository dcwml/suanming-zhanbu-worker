Monthly Horoscope for All 12 Zodiacs — August 2026

This Month at a Glance

August 2026 spans two month pillars: August 1–6 closes out the Yǐ Wèi Goat month, and on August 7, at the Start of Autumn, the Bǐng Shēn Monkey month begins. Metal energy takes command, the heat slowly retreats, and End of Heat arrives on August 23. The month branch Shēn forms a six-harmony with Snake, a trinity with Rat and Dragon, clashes with Tiger and harms Pig; Monkey presides over the month. All dates below are Gregorian.

August Horoscope for All Twelve Signs

Rat

OverallThe Shēn-Zǐ half-trinity water frame brings Metal generating Water: ally luck stands out and matters flow — a fine month to set the tone for the rest of the year.

WealthSupport from elders and superiors comes easily; planning, budgeting and applications all move smoothly.

LoveMore social occasions, and introductions carry real promise; couples can take the next step.

CareerA good month to join new teams or take on collaborative projects; room to advance after mid-month.

HealthMetal and Water feed each other and energy flows; just avoid chill and damp.

AdviceRide the trinity and widen your circle — August is the Rat's patron month.

Ox

OverallThe month branch neither combines nor clashes your Chǒu: a quiet, controllable month — advance at your own pace.

WealthRegular income holds steady while windfalls stay flat; tidy your finances and trim needless spending.

LoveSteady but spark-free — make a few small surprises of your own.

CareerProgress comes from accumulation; do current work solidly and your reputation follows.

HealthAutumn Metal brings dryness: mind airways and keep skin moisturized.

AdviceStay grounded — opportunity will find you in September.

Tiger

OverallYín clashes Shēn head-on: this is a Tai Sui-clash month, and turbulence grows. Seek stability through stillness.

WealthCash flow swings wide — avoid risky investments and large transfers, and read every contract clause.

LoveClashes carry a flavor of change and distance: couples should talk more, and long-distance pairs should meet more.

CareerRole or personnel shifts may surface; defer major decisions to next month if you can.

HealthWatch travel and road safety; guard limbs and sinews against strain.

AdviceIn a clash month, staying still avoids errors — postpone big matters and let stillness lead.

Rabbit

OverallMǎo meets the Shēn month: pressure and opportunity arrive together — busier than usual, but meaningfully so.

WealthSocial and face-saving expenses creep up; set a ceiling in advance. Regular income stays stable.

LoveRomance is quiet; keep workplace relations clear to avoid ambiguous misunderstandings.

CareerSuperiors hand down more tasks; shoulder the pressure and it becomes your stage.

HealthLong sitting strains neck and shoulders; rest your eyes.

AdviceMore effort, more harvest this month — and learn to decline the socializing that leads nowhere.

Dragon

OverallThe Shēn-Zǐ-Chén trinity water frame gathers — ally luck is second to none among the twelve signs. Promote, sign, expand.

WealthBoth regular and side income perform; collaborative projects pay out — lock partnerships in.

LoveSingles may meet someone in collaborative settings; couples should weave each other into future plans.

CareerGood news on promotion or new responsibility may arrive late in the month; put signatures on trinity days.

HealthThe water frame runs strong: mind kidneys and urinary health, and avoid sitting too long.

AdviceAugust is the Dragon's month for action — when opportunity appears, don't hesitate.

Snake

OverallSì-Shēn six-harmony makes you the month's best-aligned sign — though the harmony carries a hidden penalty, so mind the fine print when things double up nicely.

WealthCooperation produces money: sign contracts and form alliances, but keep every clause of every document clear.

LoveWarm romance: singles may meet someone through introductions; couples plan the future together.

CareerSmooth early on; later, be wary of entanglement when acting or guaranteeing on others' behalf.

HealthWatch for flare-ups of old complaints, skin allergies and mouth troubles.

AdviceIn a harmony-with-penalty month, handle contracts and money paperwork with extra care and the luck lasts.

Horse

OverallThe month stem Bǐng mirrors your year's fire and the month branch neither combines nor clashes your Wǔ — largely calm, with impatience as the only real risk.

WealthAverage fortune; peer competition and shared expenses rise, so pace your outgoings.

LoveEmotions run warm and small things spark arguments — one step back opens the sky.

CareerPeers bustle around you; tend your own patch and don't let others' rhythm carry you off.

HealthFire runs high: watch for heart-heat, insomnia and mouth ulcers.

AdviceSlow down and move steadily; September reveals your true standing.

Goat

OverallWèi meets Shēn without clash or combination: a recuperative month for study, tidying up and storing strength.

WealthSpending on learning pays; for investments, wait for next month's timing.

LoveRomance simmers low — tend yourself first, and you'll attract better.

CareerQuiet help surrounds you; do your duties solidly and a turn arrives at month's end.

HealthLate nights and irregular meals upset the stomach — keep to routine.

AdviceAugust's theme is sharpening the blade; no need to rush onto the stage.

Monkey

OverallYou preside over the month: strong presence, many chances — yet Shēn meeting Shēn is also a repetition frame, so outward bustle comes with inward drain.

WealthRoads to money open, but you may run errands for others; think twice before collecting for or guaranteeing anyone.

LoveAttention rises; partnered Monkeys should keep boundaries clear to dodge entanglements.

CareerResponsibility grows in your presiding month — speak little, deliver much, and pressure turns into results.

HealthMind airways and low moods; balance work and rest.

AdviceIn your presiding month, master yourself first and the affairs follow.

Rooster

OverallYǒu meets the Shēn month with Metal doubled: execution is formidable, but hardness snaps — the keyword is restraint.

WealthAction brings income and motion brings gain; just avoid aggressive all-in positions and keep reserves.

LoveYour presence is strong and magnetism rises — but words can cut, so soften your delivery.

CareerLead projects and decide quickly, but don't make enemies by calling out every fault.

HealthExcess Metal dries: mind throat, airways and skin.

AdviceIn a Metal-strong month, turn the edge inward on yourself, not outward at others.

Dog

OverallXū meets the Shēn month smoothly: no storms on the horizon — a month for quick, small steps.

WealthFortune warms, especially late in the month; side projects and part-time income look promising.

LoveRomance improves by degrees — friendships can ripen into more, so gather often.

CareerSeeds sown early in the month bear fruit by the end; submit documents and applications early.

HealthGenerally sound; mind digestion through the seasonal change.

AdviceKeep your own rhythm — your road is the most solid one.

Pig

OverallShēn harms Hài: one of the month's two unsettled signs — watch for scheming people and hidden losses.

WealthMoney leaks: beware honeyed words, and don't lend or guarantee lightly.

LoveRumors breed misunderstandings — ask directly instead of guessing.

CareerGuard your credit and stay out of crossfire; keep important exchanges in writing.

HealthMinor ailments can linger — colds and old complaints deserve rest, not late nights.

AdviceIn a harm month, stay low-key and sparing with words; leave no opening and trouble dissolves on its own.

Monthly Horoscope for All 12 Zodiacs — December 2026

This Month at a Glance

December 2026 spans two month pillars: December 1–6 closes out the Jǐ Hài Pig month, and on December 7, at Major Snow (大雪), the Gēng Zǐ Rat month begins. Water reaches its yearly peak in the deep freeze, and the Winter Solstice (冬至) on December 22 turns the tide — one spark of yang born at the coldest point. The month branch Zǐ forms a six-harmony with Ox, a trinity with Monkey and Dragon, clashes with Horse and harms Goat; Rat presides over the month. All dates below are Gregorian.

December Horoscope for All Twelve Signs

Rat

OverallZǐ presides over the month: Zǐ meeting Zǐ is a repetition frame — presence strong and chances many, but duty weighs heavy; beware inner drain and running in circles.

WealthRoads to money exist, yet face and favors invite spending; peak Water means fast flow — money comes quick and leaves quicker, so bank it while it's there.

LoveAttention rises; partnered Rats should mind boundaries to keep misunderstandings out, and singles should screen for quality — choose carefully, not widely.

CareerA presiding month loads you with tasks — dare to decide, but learn to delegate; don't carry everything alone.

HealthWater peaks for the year: mind the kidneys, bladder and chilled lower back — moderate exercise kindles heat and shakes off the cold.

AdviceSettle yourself first in your presiding month — less dwelling, more doing, and break the loop.

Ox

OverallZǐ-Chǒu six-harmony makes you the month's smoothest sign — cooperation, signings and relationships all trend up, and things get done with half the effort.

WealthHarmony brings money: patrons point the way — good for contracts, partnerships and funding matches, with both incomes carrying warmth.

LoveThe Zǐ-Chǒu union favors steady romance — singles enjoy blooming prospects and should use the auspicious days to commit or get engaged; couples grow closer.

CareerCollaboration flows unusually well — negotiations, signings and presentations all find windows; schedule key moves on harmony days.

HealthWarm and stable overall; mind the deep-winter cold — keep warm and watch cold hands, feet and stiff knees.

AdviceIn a six-harmony month, be proactive — reach out before the moment passes; luck favors the ones who move.

Tiger

OverallYín wood drinks the month's full Water — quietly nourished and quietly backed, a month for building reserves, steady and upward.

WealthThe resource star is on duty: regular income holds, and studying, certifications or new skills convert best into pay.

LoveA nourishing, settled month — couples find daily time together softer and richer, while singles do well enriching themselves first; no rush.

CareerGood for learning, planning and drafting foundations — someone quietly has your back; hold off on hasty expansion.

HealthWood fed by Water in deep frost: mind the liver and chilled joints and tendons — warm up fully before exercise.

AdviceDecember is for growing roots — root deep enough, and spring brings the branches.

Rabbit

OverallMǎo wood is well watered this month — roots moist, air calm; move at your own pace and the month cooperates.

WealthFinances run level, steady like a slow stream; Water rules circulation — spending within means keeps everything smooth.

LoveWater softens wood: couples flow easily with gentle words, while singles find sparks hiding in the everyday — no forcing needed.

CareerA clear head and smooth paperwork — good for reviews, wrap-ups and refining plans ahead of the new year.

HealthFull Water floats the wood: mind liver blood and tired eyes — sleep early to nourish the liver, and watch cold hands and feet.

AdviceRide the nourishment, keep the tempo in your own hands, and cross winter steadily.

Dragon

OverallThe Shēn-Zǐ-Chén trinity water frame forms with Chén as the reservoir — kindred forces converge, patron luck and command both swell, and Dragon stands among December's brightest signs.

WealthTrinity gathers money: partnerships and resource integration bring gains — good for closing big deals and calling in what's owed.

LoveYour social field is strong; singles may meet someone real through industry events and introductions, and couples find shared goals sharpening.

CareerPatrons show up clearly — lead the projects, marshal the resources; consolidation and expansion are both well-timed.

HealthThe frame settles you; in deep winter, mind cold-damp digestion and keep the lower back warm.

AdviceIn a trinity month, consolidate on the rise — store the resources, and gather the people.

Snake

OverallSì meets the Zǐ month: full Water presses Fire — the environment runs cold and costs run up. Bank your warmth, sheathe the flash, and pace yourself.

WealthRegular income holds; go easy on side gains. Full Water against weak Fire hints at leakage — defer the big expenses.

LoveCold water dims the flame: couples should add warmth and company, while singles are better off waiting for a better window.

CareerCompetition and reviews multiply — make the core work solid and decline the scalding new assignments.

HealthWater dousing Fire: mind heart-brain circulation and blood pressure swings — keep warm, keep a regular clock.

AdviceBank your fire inside this cold month — spend less, restore more; at the Winter Solstice one spark of yang is born, and the thaw has its day.

Horse

OverallZǐ clashes Wǔ head-on — and the year's own Wǔ is struck by the same month, a doubled clash: volatility squared. Seek stability through stillness and defer the big calls.

WealthFinances swing wide — no impulsive investing or large guarantees, and read every clause before signing.

LoveClashes bring change: couples should talk more and suspect less, leaving each other buffer room; singles can let it wait.

CareerRoles or direction may reshuffle — postpone major decisions where possible; observe first, then place your move.

HealthWater and Fire collide: watch heart-brain circulation, blood pressure, insomnia and flare-ups — and be extra careful driving and traveling.

AdviceIn a clash month, stability beats speed — closing out your Tai Sui year on a steady note is itself a win.

Goat

OverallZǐ harms Wèi: one of December's most cautious signs — watch schemers, gossip and hidden losses; stay low-key and spare with words.

WealthMoney leaks quietly: avoid casual loans and guarantees, and don't pay for favors with your wallet.

LoveMisunderstandings can wedge in — clarify face to face instead of guessing; singles should weed out the bad apples.

CareerGuard against credit theft and crossfire — keep important exchanges in writing, pick no sides, and just deliver.

HealthHarm months invite relapses and chilled digestion — regular hours through deep winter keep the edge off.

AdviceIn a harm month, sheathe the blade and hold your tongue — gossip dies with silence, and the storm passes.

Monkey

OverallThe Shēn-Zǐ-Chén trinity water frame forms around Shēn — the alliance lifts you, wit and drive both surge, and Monkey ranks among December's luckiest signs.

WealthTrinity gathers money: deals grow through partners — good for negotiations, signings and joint projects, with both incomes picking up.

LoveCharisma peaks; singles may meet someone real in teams and gatherings, and couples find their rapport deepening.

CareerTeam play runs smoothly — organize resources and lead the charge; visibility pays this month.

HealthMetal feeding Water keeps you settled; in deep winter, mind the airways in the cold and stiff joints — bundle up.

AdviceThe frame's strength lies in people — build alliances, borrow the momentum, and get double the result for half the effort.

Rooster

OverallYǒu meets the Zǐ month: Metal sinks beneath the Water and your energy leaks into giving — output is high, returns slow. Lower the expectations and guard rather than charge.

WealthRegular pay holds while side gains stay flat; trade expertise for pay — sit out the speculation and the gambles.

LoveWarmth dips a notch; couples should create small surprises, and singles are better off slow-burning than rushing to define things.

CareerPolish what's already on your desk and contribute from behind — but keep a clear record of your credit.

HealthA draining month breeds fatigue: mind airways and colds — layer up early and keep a regular clock.

AdviceDecember favors sheathing the edge and recharging — spend your energy only on people and causes worth it.

Dog

OverallXū meets the Zǐ month: Earth damming Water makes the wealth star shine, yet full winter Water also strains Earth — opportunity and outlay arrive together; build steadily.

WealthThe wealth door has a path, but Water in flood circulates fast — keep a ledger for every cent in and out, and leave margins on big spends.

LoveSteadfastness outshines sweet talk: couples do well drafting next year's plans together, and sincerity is a single Dog's best card.

CareerDuty on the shoulders with year-end closeout near — tidy up your measurable wins, and the annual review will carry your weight.

HealthEarth straining against Water drains you: mind digestion in the cold and a heavy lower back — warm, regular meals.

AdviceMoney doesn't run; walk it slowly — watch the cash flow and close the year on a steady note.

Pig

OverallHài meets the Zǐ month: kindred water joins in — same element, same season. Energy and spirits rise, and the month rewards moving with the current.

WealthStrong Water can carry wealth: regular income edges up steadily; yet Water rules flow — bank it while it's there.

LoveWater lends wit and warmth: partnered Pigs should speak softly and often, while singles may cross paths with someone in the crowd.

CareerIdeas run and connections open — good for communication, collaboration, and reviving the projects left on ice.

HealthWater peaks: mind the kidneys and chill — sleep early, keep warm, and go easy on cold food and drinks.

AdviceRide the momentum in your element's season, but remember to close the valve — don't let energy and money drift out with the tide.

Monthly Horoscope for All 12 Zodiacs — September 2026

This Month at a Glance

September 2026 spans two month pillars: September 1–6 closes out the Bǐng Shēn Monkey month, and on September 7, at White Dew, the Dīng Yǒu Rooster month begins. Metal energy turns crisp and dry, the cool deepens, and the Autumn Equinox arrives on September 23. The month branch Yǒu forms a six-harmony with Dragon, a trinity with Snake and Ox, clashes with Rabbit and harms Dog; Rooster presides over the month. All dates below are Gregorian.

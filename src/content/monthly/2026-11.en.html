Monthly Horoscope for All 12 Zodiacs — November 2026

This Month at a Glance

November 2026 spans two month pillars: November 1–6 closes out the Wù Xū Dog month, and on November 7, at Start of Winter (立冬), the Jǐ Hài Pig month begins. Water takes command as early winter settles in, and Light Snow (小雪) on November 22 deepens the chill. The month branch Hài forms a six-harmony with Tiger, a trinity with Rabbit and Goat, clashes with Snake and harms Monkey; Pig presides over the month. All dates below are Gregorian.

November Horoscope for All Twelve Signs

Rat

OverallZǐ meets the Hài month: rivers find their water — kindred element joins in, energy and spirits rise, and the month rewards moving with the current.

WealthStrong Water can carry wealth: regular income edges up steadily. Yet Water also means flow — guard against money rushing in and out; bank it while it's there.

LoveWater lends wit and warmth: partnered Rats should speak softly and often, while singles may cross paths with someone in the crowd — social circles hold the spark.

CareerIdeas run fast — a fine month for creative and communication work; peers pitch in, and joint efforts go smoother.

HealthWater peaks: mind the kidneys and chill — sleep early, keep warm, and go easy on cold food and drinks.

AdviceRide the momentum in your element's month, but remember to close the valve — don't let energy and money drift out with the tide.

Ox

OverallChǒu meets the Hài month: Earth damming Water makes the wealth star shine — opportunity comes with strain, so pace yourself and build steadily.

WealthThe wealth star is on duty and money has a door; but winter Water runs full and Earth can slump — leave generous margins on any big outlay.

LoveA pragmatic month: couples find romance in getting life solidly done, while singles do better letting things grow slowly through work connections.

CareerTasks on hand, duties on shoulders — chew through the hard work step by step; the results will speak for themselves.

HealthEarth straining against Water drains reserves: mind digestion in the cold and a heavy lower back — warm, regular meals.

AdviceThe wealth door is open — still, weigh what you carry first; take profit steadily and it stays in the bag.

Tiger

OverallYín-Hài six-harmony makes you the month's smoothest sign — cooperation, relationships and romance all trend up, and things get done with half the effort.

WealthHarmony brings money: patrons point the way — good for contracts, partnerships and resource matches, with both incomes carrying warmth.

LoveYín-Hài union favors romance — singles enjoy blooming prospects and should use the auspicious days to commit or get engaged; couples grow closer.

CareerCollaboration flows unusually well — negotiations, signings and presentations all find windows; schedule key moves on harmony days.

HealthWarm and stable overall; just mind the early-winter cold — keep warm and watch cold hands and feet.

AdviceIn a six-harmony month, be proactive — reach out before the moment passes; luck favors the ones who move.

Rabbit

OverallThe Hài-Mǎo-Wèi trinity wood frame forms — Mǎo gains the alliance, patron luck surges, and Rabbit stands among November's brightest signs.

WealthTrinity gathers money: partnerships and resource integration bring gains — good for closing big deals and long contracts.

LoveCharisma peaks; singles may meet someone real at team events and gatherings, and couples grow closer.

CareerPatrons show up clearly — take the lead on projects, ask for resources, and expansion is well-timed.

HealthThe wood frame stretches and settles you; in winter cold, sleep early to nourish liver blood and rest tired eyes.

AdviceIn a trinity month, press the advantage — your network is November's biggest lever.

Dragon

OverallChén meets the Hài month: winter Water swells, and Chén — the reservoir — is built to store it. A month for consolidation, not adventurism.

WealthFinances run level and hidden: save, budget and stockpile dry powder — impulsive speculation is the month's cardinal sin.

LoveA quiet month: for couples, simply keeping company is the blessing; singles should polish themselves while fate is still brewing.

CareerGuard rather than charge — wrap up current projects cleanly and lay in provisions for the year-end push.

HealthStrong Water turns Earth damp: mind cold-damp digestion and lower-back strain, and avoid sitting long in chilly, damp spots.

AdviceNovember's keyword is storage — a full reservoir finds its day of use.

Snake

OverallSì clashes Hài head-on: a month of clear upheaval — seek stability through stillness and defer the big calls.

WealthFinances swing wide — no impulsive investing or large guarantees, and read every contract line.

LoveClashes bring change: couples should talk more and suspect less, leaving each other buffer room; singles can let it wait.

CareerRoles or projects may reshuffle — postpone major decisions where possible; observe first, then place your move.

HealthWater crashing on Fire: watch heart-brain circulation, blood pressure and old ailments; travel carefully and keep a calm hand on the wheel.

AdviceIn a clash month, stability beats speed — move less, err less, and treat change as information, not threat.

Horse

OverallWǔ meets the Hài month: strong Water presses Fire — a pressure-cooker month. Sheathe the flash, conserve strength, and don't fight every round.

WealthRegular income holds; go easy on side gains. Full Water against weak Fire hints at leakage — defer the big expenses.

LoveRising water dims the flame: couples should manufacture warmth and little rituals, while singles are better off waiting for spring.

CareerRules and reviews multiply — make the in-bounds work impeccable and steer clear of gray areas.

HealthWater dousing Fire: mind heart-brain circulation and cold hands and feet — keep warm, and let movement kindle your yang.

AdviceWinter water banks the fire — store your warmth inside, gather strength, and wait for the wind to turn.

Goat

OverallThe Hài-Mǎo-Wèi trinity wood frame lifts Wèi — allies and team power multiply, an excellent month to expand and build coalitions.

WealthThe frame pools money: joint projects pay handsomely — good for opening new markets.

LoveYour social field is strong; singles won't lack admirers — choose carefully rather than widely, and couples find shared goals sharpening.

CareerTeam play runs smoothly — organize resources and lead the charge; visibility pays this month.

HealthThe wood frame keeps things moving; in winter cold, mind digestion — no cold or raw food on the plate.

AdviceThe trinity's strength lies in people — turn friends into teammates, and teammates into patrons.

Monkey

OverallShēn harms Hài: one of November's most cautious signs — watch schemers, gossip and hidden losses; stay low-key and spare with words.

WealthMoney leaks quietly: avoid casual loans and guarantees, and don't pay for favors with your wallet.

LoveMisunderstandings can wedge in — clarify face to face instead of guessing; singles should weed out the bad apples.

CareerGuard against credit theft and crossfire — keep important exchanges in writing, pick no sides, and just deliver.

HealthHarm months invite relapses and minor respiratory nags — regular hours and steady exercise through early winter keep the edge off.

AdviceIn a harm month, sheathe the blade and hold your tongue — gossip dies with silence, and the storm passes.

Rooster

OverallYǒu meets the Hài month: Metal generates Water and your energy leaks into giving — output is high, returns slow. Lower the expectations and flow steadily.

WealthRegular pay holds while side gains stay flat; social and favor spending grows — spend within means.

LoveWarmth dips a notch; couples should create small surprises, and singles are better off slow-burning than rushing to define things.

CareerA month for supporting roles — contribute from behind, but keep a clear record of your credit.

HealthA draining month breeds fatigue: mind airways and colds — layer up early and keep a regular clock.

AdviceNovember favors guarding and assisting — spend your energy only on people and causes worth it.

Dog

OverallXū meets the Hài month: Earth damming Water makes the wealth star shine, yet full winter Water also strains Earth — behind every opportunity stands a test.

WealthMoney has a way in, but full Water means flow — keep a ledger for every cent in and out; no drifting.

LoveSteadfastness outshines sweet talk: couples do well planning the year ahead together, and sincerity is a single Dog's best card.

CareerDuty on the shoulders and reviews around the corner — tidy up your measurable wins, and year-end will carry your weight.

HealthEarth straining against Water drains you: mind digestion and a chilled lower back — balance work and rest, don't gut it out alone.

AdviceMoney doesn't run; walk it slowly — watch the cash flow and close winter on a steady note.

Pig

OverallHài presides over the month: Hài meeting Hài is a repetition frame — presence strong and chances many, but duty weighs heavy; beware inner drain and running in circles.

WealthRoads to money exist, yet face and favors invite spending — invest only on your own judgment.

LoveAttention rises; partnered Pigs should mind boundaries to keep misunderstandings out, and singles should screen for quality.

CareerA presiding month loads you with tasks — dare to decide, but learn to delegate; don't carry everything alone.

HealthWater peaks: mind the kidneys and chilled lower back — moderate exercise kindles heat and shakes off the cold.

AdviceSettle yourself first in your presiding month — less dwelling, more doing, and break the loop.

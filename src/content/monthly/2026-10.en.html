Monthly Horoscope for All 12 Zodiacs — October 2026

This Month at a Glance

October 2026 spans two month pillars: October 1–7 closes out the Dīng Yǒu Rooster month, and on October 8, at Cold Dew (寒露), the Wù Xū Dog month begins. Dry Earth takes command as deep autumn settles in, the lunar ninth month opens on October 10, and Frost Descent (霜降) on October 23 deepens the chill. The month branch Xū forms a six-harmony with Rabbit, a trinity with Tiger and Horse, clashes with Dragon and harms Rooster; Dog presides over the month. All dates below are Gregorian.

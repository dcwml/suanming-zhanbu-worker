FAQ

Why avoid clashes when picking dates?

A clash means one of the six earthly-branch oppositions (zi-wu, chou-wei, yin-shen, mao-you, chen-xu, si-hai). Days whose branch clashes with your own year branch are traditionally considered unfavorable.

What are Yang Gong taboo days?

Thirteen fixed lunar dates, said to be set by Yang Junsong, regarded as unsuitable for important affairs — e.g. the 13th of the first lunar month. This tool excludes them together with Month-Broken days.

How are Yellow Road lucky days determined?

Twelve celestial officers take turns presiding over days, split into the Yellow Road (auspicious) and Black Road (inauspicious), refined by the Twelve Day Officers and the 28 lunar mansions. Higher-ranked days score better across all three.

Are this tool's results authoritative?

Traditional date-selection schools disagree with each other. This tool applies one transparent rule set; results are for reference only — use your own judgement for important matters.

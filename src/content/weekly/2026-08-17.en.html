This Week at a Glance

In the Bǐng Wǔ Fire-Horse year and Bǐng Shēn Monkey month, this week's day branches run Hài, Zǐ, Chǒu, Yín, Mǎo, Chén, Sì, with Metal and Water energy gathering strength. Wednesday also falls on the seventh day of the seventh lunar month — the Qīxī festival, a traditional day for needlework and wishes, though the almanac itself marks the rest of the day as unsuitable for major matters.

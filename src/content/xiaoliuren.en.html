Xiao Liu Ren Divination

A reading at the snap of your fingers. Following the folk Xiao Liu Ren (Small Six Ren) method, cast a chart from the current lunar month, day and hour — or any three numbers — across the six palaces of Da An, Liu Lian, Su Xi, Chi Kou, Xiao Ji and Kong Wang, with AI interpretations.

Step 1 · Focus

Xiao Liu Ren treasures the exact moment the question forms. Calm your mind, hold your question in focus, and write it down.

Step 2 · Cast

Casting method

The moment you press "Cast" is taken as the oracle: the month is counted over the six palaces, the day continues from the month palace, and the hour settles the final palace.

The three numbers stand for month, day and hour: count the first from Da An, the second from the palace it lands in, and the third settles the final palace.

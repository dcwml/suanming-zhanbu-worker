Hour Omens of Lunar August 2026: The Sole All-Yang Day, Metal Trines and Triple Kui Gang

Lunar August — mid-autumn, the month when metal holds the season's warrant — runs from September 11 to October 9, 2026: twenty-nine days, three hundred and forty-eight double-hours. This essay charts them one by one, recording only the extraordinary: completed trines, directional unions, all-yang bodies and the Kui Gang days — each verdict cast in an imperial astrologer's antique voice, then explained in plain words. Published on the very first day of the month, it is a record written entirely in advance: every configuration below still lies in the future, to be checked day by day as it arrives.

The Month at a Glance

In the year of Bing Wu (the Fire Horse), the month pillar runs Ding You — after Cold Dew on October 8, only the month's last day, October 9, turns to Wu Xu. Across all 348 double-hours —

  • Not one all-yin chart: the year pillar Bing Wu is wholly yang, so an all-yin chart remains structurally impossible this month — a small lesson in how the calendar works;
  • Six all-yang hours — all on a single day: July's seventy-eight all-yang hours give way before the yin month pillar Ding You; only on October 9 (day 29), when the pillar turns Wu Xu, do six all-yang hours emerge (Zi, Yin, Chen, Wu, Shen, Xu) — the whole month's store, gathered into one day;
  • Nineteen grand configurations: eleven trines (five metal, six fire) and eight directional unions (four southern fire, four western metal), with days 11 and 23 each pairing a fire trine at dawn and a metal quarter at dusk, each with its verdict below;
  • Three Kui Gang days: day 5 (Ren Chen), day 11 (Wu Xu) and day 23 (Geng Xu) — three of the four Kui Gang pillars visit this month, with a dedicated section.

The Nineteen Grand Configurations

1st · Day 2, Snake hour (Sep 12, 9–11 a.m.)

Four pillars: 丙午 丁酉 己丑 己巳

Configuration: Snake-Rooster-Ox metal trine

On the second day in the Snake hour, the metal trine convenes with the season's own warrant — one born here judges cleanly and speaks to the point, words like coined metal; the west favors them.

The month's Rooster, the day's Ox and the hour's Snake bind the metal trine whole, and Rooster metal holds August's warrant — autumn metal gathered into a trine, its keenest edge. Ji earth, the day master, seated on the Ox, lets metal polish its brilliance: a mind of exact order and precise speech, with finance, law and the clerk's blade all promising fields.

2nd · Day 3, Dog hour (Sep 13, 7–9 p.m.)

Four pillars: 丙午 丁酉 庚寅 丙戌

Configuration: Canopy Star, Tiger-Horse-Dog fire trine

On the third day in the Dog hour, a metal day-master inside a fire trine — Geng gold tempered in the Dog's storehouse, the Canopy Star in attendance — one born here is stern and upright, honored more with each passing year.

The year's Horse, the day's Tiger and the hour's Dog complete the fire trine; the Dog is the Canopy Star of a Geng day and fire's own storehouse. Geng metal, the day master, born inside a trine of fire: a sword in the furnace — a firm, principled nature, name and standing rising through the middle and later years.

3rd · Day 6, Ox hour (Sep 16, 1–3 a.m.)

Four pillars: 丙午 丁酉 癸巳 癸丑

Configuration: Canopy Star, Snake-Rooster-Ox metal trine

On the sixth day in the Ox hour, the metal trine convenes at midnight beneath the Canopy Star — one born here is quiet, deep in counsel, the edge kept out of sight.

Snake, Rooster and Ox bind the trine whole, and the Ox is the Canopy Star of a Gui day (the Snake-Rooster-Ox trine roofs in the Ox). Gui water, the day master, fed by an entire metal trine — a well that never runs dry: wisdom worn out of sight, still water running deep; scholarship and the healing arts suit.

4th · Day 6, Goat hour (Sep 16, 1–3 p.m.)

Four pillars: 丙午 丁酉 癸巳 己未

Configuration: Snake-Horse-Goat southern union

The same day in the Goat hour, Snake, Horse and Goat assemble the southern quarter — a torch carried against the wind — one born here is round without, square within.

The day's Snake, the year's Horse and the hour's Goat stand in succession, and the fire quarter gathers. A metal trine at midnight, a fire quarter at noon — two configurations in one day. Gui water, the day master, born into the fire quarter: water and fire striking sparks — a stubborn will, a heart set toward daylight though the body walk through shadow.

5th · Day 8, Snake hour (Sep 18, 9–11 a.m.)

Four pillars: 丙午 丁酉 乙未 辛巳

Configuration: Traveling Horse, Snake-Horse-Goat southern union

On the eighth day in the Snake hour, the fire quarter gathers again with the Traveling Horse in the same palace — one born here founds enterprises on far roads and wins rank amid motion; the southeast greatly favors them.

The year's Horse, the day's Goat and the hour's Snake complete the southern quarter; and the Snake is likewise the Traveling Horse of a Yi Wei day (the Pig-Rabbit-Goat trine keeps its horse in the Snake). Fire quarter and horse in one palace: fortune found in passage, an estate founded away from home.

6th · Day 9, Dog hour (Sep 19, 7–9 p.m.)

Four pillars: 丙午 丁酉 丙申 戊戌

Configuration: Monkey-Rooster-Dog western union

On the ninth day in the Dog hour, Monkey, Rooster and Dog string through the west in a Rooster month — one born here speaks with a bell's authority and measures all things by rule; the west favors them.

The day's Monkey, the month's Rooster and the hour's Dog join in unbroken succession, gathered under the season's warrant. Bing fire, the day master, standing before the metal quarter: the sun walking the western lands — an awesome, accomplished air, with law, arms and public office all suitable.

7th · Day 11, Tiger hour (Sep 21, 3–5 a.m.)

Four pillars: 丙午 丁酉 戊戌 甲寅

Configuration: Kui Gang day, Tiger-Horse-Dog fire trine

On the eleventh day in the Tiger hour, Kui Gang commands the day while the fire trine convenes toward dawn — one born here stands unshaken in crisis and breaks ground where others hesitate.

A Wu Xu Kui Gang day: the year's Horse, the day's Dog and the hour's Tiger complete the fire trine. Thick earth bearing a whole trine of fire — Kui Gang's severity given fire's momentum: daring beyond the common, decisive in every office held. All twelve double-hours of this day carry the Kui Gang mark — see the section below.

8th · Day 11, Monkey hour (Sep 21, 3–5 p.m.)

Four pillars: 丙午 丁酉 戊戌 庚申

Configuration: Kui Gang day, Traveling Horse, Monkey-Rooster-Dog western union

The same day in the Monkey hour, Kui Gang above and the metal quarter assembled below, the Traveling Horse attending — one born here holds the frontier with authority, batons of rank conferred on the western marches.

The day's Dog, the month's Rooster and the hour's Monkey complete the western quarter; the Monkey is likewise the Traveling Horse of a Wu day. Within one day a fire trine and a metal quarter stand in turn — gold and iron cast upon a Kui Gang day: military honor and deeds at the border.

9th · Day 14, Snake hour (Sep 24, 9–11 a.m.)

Four pillars: 丙午 丁酉 辛丑 癸巳

Configuration: Snake-Rooster-Ox metal trine

On the fourteenth day in the Snake hour, the metal trine convenes a third time — Xin gold seated on the Ox, its refinement shining at the Snake hour — one born here is a master of fine work, famed by craft.

The third completion of the metal trine (days 2, 6 and 14). Xin gold, the day master, seated on the Ox and fed by it, the trine holding the Rooster month's warrant: a mind of the finest grain, a livelihood built on skill — metalwork, carving and the healing arts all suit.

10th · Day 15, Dog hour (Sep 25, 7–9 p.m.) — Mid-Autumn

Four pillars: 丙午 丁酉 壬寅 庚戌

Configuration: Canopy Star, Tiger-Horse-Dog fire trine

On Mid-Autumn night in the Dog hour, the fire trine convenes beneath a full moon, the Canopy Star in the Dog — one born here has blossoms for the full moon and a ready brush; the late years flourish.

The fifteenth of the eighth month is Mid-Autumn, and the Dog hour is the very time of moon-viewing. The year's Horse, the day's Tiger and the hour's Dog complete the fire trine; the Dog is the Canopy Star of a Ren day. Ren water, the day master, mirroring a trine of fire: the moon printed on ten thousand streams — lucid writing and a harmonious house, with the Canopy Star granting quiet fortune in old age.

11th · Day 18, Ox hour (Sep 28, 1–3 a.m.)

Four pillars: 丙午 丁酉 乙巳 丁丑

Configuration: Canopy Star, Snake-Rooster-Ox metal trine

On the eighteenth day in the Ox hour, the metal trine convenes a fourth time beneath the Canopy Star — one born here keeps counsel close and their talents closer, waiting upon the hour.

The fourth completion of the metal trine (days 2, 6, 14 and 18). Yi wood, the day master, takes the trine's carving as fine timber takes the carpenter's line — strict with the self, a great vessel finished late.

12th · Day 18, Goat hour (Sep 28, 1–3 p.m.)

Four pillars: 丙午 丁酉 乙巳 癸未

Configuration: Snake-Horse-Goat southern union

The same day in the Goat hour, the fire quarter gathers again to stand beside the night's metal trine — one born here tempers hardness with softness and knows when to advance, when to hold.

The same shape as the sixth day: metal trine in the Ox hour, fire quarter in the Goat — two configurations in one day. Yi wood, the day master, flowers into fire, wood and fire shining through each other: an outstanding literary gift joined to an easy worldly grace.

13th · Day 20, Snake hour (Sep 30, 9–11 a.m.)

Four pillars: 丙午 丁酉 丁未 乙巳

Configuration: Traveling Horse, Snake-Horse-Goat southern union

On the twentieth day in the Snake hour, the fire quarter gathers a fourth time with the Traveling Horse attending once more — one born here trades across ten thousand li, name and profit arriving together; the southeast greatly favors them.

The fourth completion of the fire quarter (days 6, 8, 18 and 20). The Snake is likewise the Traveling Horse of a Ding Wei day — the same device as the eighth day: fire quarter and horse in one palace, fortune found in the hurry of the road.

14th · Day 21, Dog hour (Oct 1, 7–9 p.m.)

Four pillars: 丙午 丁酉 戊申 壬戌

Configuration: Monkey-Rooster-Dog western union

On the twenty-first day in the Dog hour, the metal quarter strings together again — thick earth born in a metal month — one born here keeps every promise and grows rich slowly, like a river deepening; the west favors them.

The day's Monkey, the month's Rooster and the hour's Dog join for the third western union (days 9, 11 and 21). Wu earth, the day master, born in a metal month: earth and metal generating each other — honest to the bone, the family estate swelling by degrees.

15th · Day 23, Tiger hour (Oct 3, 3–5 a.m.)

Four pillars: 丙午 丁酉 庚戌 戊寅

Configuration: Kui Gang day, Tiger-Horse-Dog fire trine

On the twenty-third day in the Tiger hour, Kui Gang commands again and the fire trine opens anew — the very figure of the eleventh day — one born here sees honor won twice and carries the family's standard farther.

The same architecture as day 11's Wu Xu: fire trine in the Tiger hour, metal quarter in the Monkey, the branches having wheeled full circle in twelve days. Geng metal, the day master, seated on the Dog's storehouse with a fire trine for the forging: an heir who surpasses the sire, a name weighing heavy in the home county. All twelve double-hours of this day carry the Kui Gang mark — see the section below.

16th · Day 23, Monkey hour (Oct 3, 3–5 p.m.)

Four pillars: 丙午 丁酉 庚戌 甲申

Configuration: Kui Gang day, Traveling Horse, Monkey-Rooster-Dog western union

The same day in the Monkey hour, the metal quarter convenes a fourth time upon a Kui Gang day — one born here keeps a region in order and has rank and insignia conferred upon them.

The fourth western union of the month (days 9, 11, 21 and 23), the Monkey likewise the Traveling Horse of a Geng day. The four branches match the eleventh day's Monkey hour one for one (Horse, Rooster, Dog, Monkey); only the day master has changed, Wu earth giving way to Geng metal — and metal receiving a metal quarter, the edge runs keener still.

17th · Day 26, Snake hour (Oct 6, 9–11 a.m.)

Four pillars: 丙午 丁酉 癸丑 丁巳

Configuration: Heavenly Noble, Snake-Rooster-Ox metal trine

On the twenty-sixth day in the Snake hour, the metal trine convenes a fifth time — the month's fullest count — with the Heavenly Noble in the same palace: one born here is lifted by patrons and takes a seat at court; the west favors them.

The fifth and last completion of the metal trine (days 2, 6, 14, 18 and 26), the most of any configuration this month. For a Gui day the Heavenly Noble sits in the Rabbit and the Snake; the Snake hour is thus at once one branch of the trine and the Noble's own seat — a lifetime raised up by elders, an official road running smooth.

18th · Day 27, Dog hour (Oct 7, 7–9 p.m.)

Four pillars: 丙午 丁酉 甲寅 甲戌

Configuration: Canopy Star, Tiger-Horse-Dog fire trine

On the twenty-seventh day in the Dog hour, two Jia stems stand together above a whole fire trine, the Canopy Star home in the Dog — one born here is timber for the beams, blessed with both fortune and long years.

The year's Horse, the day's Tiger and the hour's Dog complete the fire trine; the Dog is the Canopy Star of a Jia day. Day and hour stems — two Jias in file, twin trees making a forest, flowering into the trine's fire: a commanding presence and deep roots of late-life blessing.

19th · Day 29, Tiger hour (Oct 9, 3–5 a.m.) — Wu Xu month, peak of the month

Four pillars: 丙午 戊戌 丙辰 庚寅

Configuration: all-yang chart, Traveling Horse, Tiger-Horse-Dog fire trine

On the twenty-ninth day in the Tiger hour, an all-yang chart gathers the fire trine with the Traveling Horse attending — of the month's three hundred and forty-eight hours, every last all-yang hour lives on this one day — one born here stands between heaven and earth and lights a whole region.

After Cold Dew (October 8) the month pillar turns Wu Xu — a yang pillar — and it meets a Bing Chen all-yang day in a Bing Wu yang year: the month's only six all-yang hours (Zi, Yin, Chen, Wu, Shen, Xu) all fall on this day. The Tiger hour adds the fire trine and the Traveling Horse in one palace: pure yang at its flood, crowned with fire's blaze — the peak of August. Strength beyond measure; yet the over-rigid snaps. Holding to the middle way makes it last.

Kui Gang: Three Days in One Month

Kui Gang names four day pillars — 庚辰, 庚戌, 壬辰, 戊戌 — of which the classics say: "severe in nature, firm of grip, decisive." This lunar month holds three of the four: September 15 (day 5), a 壬辰 day; September 21 (day 11), a 戊戌 day; and October 3 (day 23), a 庚戌 day. Of the three days, the eleventh and twenty-third run fullest: a fire trine at the Tiger hour and a metal quarter at the Monkey hour, both written up in the main list above (7th, 8th, 15th and 16th); on day 5, the Ren Chen day, the Rat hour joins Yang Blade and General Star while the Rooster hour pairs Peach Blossom with a branch union — each worth a look. Ren water seated on the Dragon, Wu earth on the Dog, Geng metal on the Dog: Kui Gang's severity nourished each time by the storehouse earth beneath it — hence the old books' word for such days, "noble when the luck runs to strength"; worth a moment's reference.

Day-by-Day Quick Reference

One row per day. "All-yang day" marks days whose day pillar is wholly yang: on such days the six double-hours with yang stem and branch (Zi, Yin, Chen, Wu, Shen, Xu) would normally yield all-yang charts — but this month's pillar Ding You is a yin pillar, so even all-yang days produce none, save on October 9 (day 29) when the pillar turns Wu Xu and the six yang hours at last stand all-yang. Hours are given in pinyin (Zi = Rat, Chou = Ox, Yin = Tiger, Mao = Rabbit, Chen = Dragon, Si = Snake, Wu = Horse, Wei = Goat, Shen = Monkey, You = Rooster, Xu = Dog, Hai = Pig); lunar dates as month/day of the lunar calendar (8/1 = first day of the 8th lunar month, 8/29 = the 29th); "—" means the day has none.

A Word on Method

Birth-hour configurations draw on the classical logic of stems, branches and the five elements — a cultural framework ancient observers used to relate time and destiny. The verdicts above are written in an astrologer's antique voice for the pleasure of the tradition; they are not fate. What a life becomes still rests on one's own conduct and circumstance. Read, enjoy, and take from it what encourages you.

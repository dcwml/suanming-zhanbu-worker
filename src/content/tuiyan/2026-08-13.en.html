Hour Omens of Lunar July 2026: All-Yang Charts, Trine Hours and Kui Gang

Lunar July — the "Ghost Month" of folklore — runs from August 13 to September 10, 2026: twenty-nine days, three hundred and forty-eight double-hours. This essay charts them one by one, recording only the extraordinary: completed trines, directional unions, all-yang bodies and the Kui Gang day — each verdict cast in an imperial astrologer's antique voice, then explained in plain words. Published mid-month, it is a record of the whole month: hours already past are offered for hindsight, hours to come as folklore.

The Month at a Glance

In the year of Bing Wu (the Fire Horse), the month pillar runs Bing Shen, then Ding You after White Dew on September 7. Across all 348 double-hours —

  • Not one all-yin chart: the year and month pillars are wholly yang, so an all-yin chart is structurally impossible this month — a small lesson in how the calendar works;
  • Seventy-eight all-yang hours: roughly one hour in five, so they are marked in the quick-reference table rather than written up individually;
  • Twenty grand configurations: nine trines, ten directional unions, plus the five-marker hour of September 3 at 5–7 p.m., each with its verdict below;
  • One Kui Gang day: September 3, a Geng Chen day — all twelve double-hours carry the Kui Gang mark, with a dedicated section.

The Twenty Grand Configurations

1st · Day 1, Snake hour (Aug 13, 9–11 a.m.)

Four pillars: 丙午 丙申 己未 己巳

Configuration: Snake-Horse-Goat southern union

On the first day in the Snake hour, fire and earth gather into the southern quarter — one born here carries a lucid, scholarly mind; the south favors their dwelling, and renown arrives by the brush.

Snake, Horse and Goat stand together, three branches shaping fire into a direction. A Ji Wei day at a Ji Si hour: fire and earth of one breath, each nourishing the other — quick wits joined to quiet refinement, a life built on letters, with the south most favorable.

2nd · Day 3, Dog hour (Aug 15, 7–9 p.m.)

Four pillars: 丙午 丙申 辛酉 戊戌

Configuration: Monkey-Rooster-Dog western union

On the third day in the Dog hour, metal's qi strings through the west — Shen, You and Xu gather into the metal quarter — one born here is decisive to the bone, their keen pen a blade's edge; the northwest favors them.

The month's Monkey, the day's Rooster, the hour's Dog — three branches of the western metal quarter in unbroken succession. Xin metal, the day master, sits on the Rooster and stands on its own ground: a nature firm and decisive, suited to the northwest, with soldiering, law and surgery all promising fields.

3rd · Day 4, Tiger hour (Aug 16, 3–5 a.m.)

Four pillars: 丙午 丙申 壬戌 壬寅

Configuration: all-yang chart, Tiger-Horse-Dog fire trine

On the fourth day in the Tiger hour, stems and branches stand all yang and the fire trine completes — one born here carries a commander's boldness, a minister's reach; the east favors them.

Not one yin character among the eight — pure yang at its peak. Great force of character, and a temper to match; still water would serve it best.

4th · Day 4, Rooster hour (Aug 16, 5–7 p.m.)

Four pillars: 丙午 丙申 壬戌 己酉

Configuration: Monkey-Rooster-Dog western union

The same day in the Rooster hour, the metal quarter assembles whole while Ren water rests upon the Dog — one born here is soft without, steel within, moving only after full deliberation, with wealth gathering in the later years.

Same day as the Tiger-hour item, but this time it is the metal quarter that completes: Monkey, Rooster and Dog all present, the fire trine denied. Within a single day, fire at dawn and metal at dusk — two configurations standing side by side — and the pairing returns on the sixteenth, a Jia Xu day.

5th · Day 6, Dragon hour (Aug 18, 7–9 a.m.)

Four pillars: 丙午 丙申 甲子 戊辰

Configuration: all-yang chart, Canopy Star, Monkey-Rat-Dragon water trine

On the sixth day in the Dragon hour, an all-yang chart gathers the water trine and the Canopy Star stands at the gate — one born here is deep in counsel, aloof and near to the Way; the north favors them.

Monkey, Rat and Dragon bind the water trine, and in a fire year water and fire strike sparks off each other. The Dragon is the Canopy Star — brilliance with a solitary edge, drawn toward religion and the hidden arts. Jia wood flourishes on water: a mind beyond the common run, best employed in the watery north.

6th · Day 8, Dog hour (Aug 20, 7–9 p.m.)

Four pillars: 丙午 丙申 丙寅 戊戌

Configuration: all-yang chart, Canopy Star, Tiger-Horse-Dog fire trine

On the eighth day in the Dog hour, three Bing fires stand revealed above branches wholly fire, the Canopy Star enthroned in the Dog — one born here is open as daylight and holds the seal of office; a name that rings in the south.

The year, month and day stems — three Bing fires in a row — with Tiger, Horse and Dog completing the trine, and the Dog itself the Canopy Star. Bright fire makes full ceremony: a commanding presence and a name that travels far; the fire lands of the south suit them like water suits a fish.

7th · Day 10, Rat hour (Aug 22, 0–1 a.m. early-Zi)

Four pillars: 丙午 丙申 戊辰 壬子

Configuration: all-yang chart, General Star, Monkey-Rat-Dragon water trine

On the tenth day in the Rat hour, an all-yang chart gathers water and the General Star takes its seat in the Rat — one born here may lead armies and minister alike, holding the tally of command; rank and renown lie in the north.

The Monkey-Rat-Dragon water trine convenes at midnight, and the Rat is the General Star — the flourishing branch of a trine is where the General sits, marking a gift for command. Wu earth, the day master, banks the flood: hardness and softness in balance, with achievement most often found in the north.

8th · Day 11, Goat hour (Aug 23, 1–3 p.m.)

Four pillars: 丙午 丙申 己巳 辛未

Configuration: Snake-Horse-Goat southern union

On the eleventh day in the Goat hour, Snake, Horse and Goat breathe in succession and the fire quarter comes again — one born here is warm-hearted and expansive, laying fields and gardens wide; the south favors them.

The day's Snake, the year's Horse, the hour's Goat — the southern fire quarter gathers once more. Ji earth, the day master, is warmed by fire: a generous nature and a flourishing estate, with property in the south especially favored.

9th · Day 13, Snake hour (Aug 25, 9–11 a.m.)

Four pillars: 丙午 丙申 辛未 癸巳

Configuration: Snake-Horse-Goat southern union

On the thirteenth day in the Snake hour, the fire quarter gathers again while Xin metal sits on the Goat — true gold tried in the fire — one born here is steel after a hundred temperings, a great vessel finished late.

The third completion of the fire quarter this month. Xin metal, the day master, born into a place of fire — gold in the furnace: the early years grind, mid-life shapes the vessel, and each setback leaves it harder still.

10th · Day 15, Dog hour (Aug 27, 7–9 p.m.) — Zhongyuan

Four pillars: 丙午 丙申 癸酉 壬戌

Configuration: Monkey-Rooster-Dog western union

On Zhongyuan night, the fifteenth, in the Dog hour, the western metal union completes as water begins to rise — one born in this hour walks between two worlds, gifted in medicine and divination; the west favors them.

The fifteenth of the seventh month is the Zhongyuan Festival, when folk belief says the gates between the seen and unseen stand open. Metal runs clear and water quickens; the old texts read such a chart as a talent for the healing and diviner's arts.

11th · Day 16, Tiger hour (Aug 28, 3–5 a.m.)

Four pillars: 丙午 丙申 甲戌 丙寅

Configuration: all-yang chart, Tiger-Horse-Dog fire trine

On the sixteenth day in the Tiger hour, an all-yang chart meets the fire trine once more — Jia wood towering to the sky, flowering into fire — one born here writes with wings and leads the multitude.

Jia wood, the day master, flowers through three Bing fires — wood and fire shining through each other — marking brilliant writing and the confidence of the crowd. The Tiger is Jia's seat of strength: roots that run deep.

12th · Day 16, Rooster hour (Aug 28, 5–7 p.m.)

Four pillars: 丙午 丙申 甲戌 癸酉

Configuration: Monkey-Rooster-Dog western union

The same day in the Rooster hour, the metal quarter assembles whole and Jia wood stands before metal — one born here is upright beyond bending, exact in law and measure, with an officer's awesome authority.

Dawn and dusk face off across the same day: the fire trine at morning, the metal quarter at evening. The Rooster is Jia wood's Direct Officer, and an officer star backed by a directional union — a career worth watching, and law enforced like a mountain.

13th · Day 18, Dragon hour (Aug 30, 7–9 a.m.)

Four pillars: 丙午 丙申 丙子 壬辰

Configuration: all-yang chart, Canopy Star, Monkey-Rat-Dragon water trine

On the eighteenth day in the Dragon hour, three Bing fires shine upon the water trine — water and fire bringing each other to completion, the Canopy Star veiling the Dragon — one born here joins courage to wisdom, still water running deep.

The Monkey-Rat-Dragon water trine mirrors three Bing stems above: water and fire complete each other without quenching. The Dragon is the Canopy Star — wisdom worn out of sight, the pattern the ancients called great cleverness dressed as simplicity.

14th · Day 20, Dog hour (Sep 1, 7–9 p.m.)

Four pillars: 丙午 丙申 戊寅 壬戌

Configuration: all-yang chart, Canopy Star, Tiger-Horse-Dog fire trine

On the twentieth day in the Dog hour, thick earth bears up the fire trine and the Canopy Star returns to its own walls — one born here is steady as a mountain, blessed over a long stretch, flourishing in the late years.

Wu earth, the day master, carries the fire trine on its back — fire and earth generating each other, foundations of the deepest sort. The Dog is both Canopy Star and fire's storehouse: firmness of faith and a late-life fortune running full.

15th · Day 22, Rat hour (Sep 3, 0–1 a.m. early-Zi)

Four pillars: 丙午 丙申 庚辰 丙子

Configuration: all-yang chart, Kui Gang day, General Star, Monkey-Rat-Dragon water trine

On the twenty-second day in the Rat hour, Kui Gang commands the day while the General Star returns to the Rat — an all-yang chart gathering water, iron and water in one body — one born here decides as swiftly as they command, a talent to rule a region.

A Geng Chen Kui Gang day is decisive enough on its own; add the Monkey-Rat-Dragon trine draining its brilliance into water, with the General Star holding the field, and wisdom rides with courage. All twelve double-hours of this day carry the Kui Gang mark — see the section below.

16th · Day 22, Rooster hour (Sep 3, 5–7 p.m.) — Peak of the Month

Four pillars: 丙午 丙申 庚辰 乙酉

Configuration: Kui Gang day, Yang Blade, Peach Blossom, day-hour stem pairing, day-hour branch union (five markers)

Kui Gang commands the day while Yang Blade and Peach Blossom arrive together, stems pair and branches embrace — iron will and tender heart in one body; a general who is also a romantic; the west favors them.

Five markers converge on this single hour, more than any other in the month: the Kui Gang day pillar, the Yang Blade's edge, the Peach Blossom's charm, and both a stem pairing and a branch union between day and hour. Strength and sweetness in equal measure — wielded well, a scholar-general; indulged, a slave to romance.

17th · Day 23, Goat hour (Sep 4, 1–3 p.m.)

Four pillars: 丙午 丙申 辛巳 乙未

Configuration: Snake-Horse-Goat southern union

On the twenty-third day in the Goat hour, the fire quarter gathers a fourth time this month and Xin metal meets the furnace — one born here is jade worn to perfection, with a name that travels far.

The southern fire quarter completes for the fourth time this month (days 1, 11, 13 and 23). Xin metal sits on the Snake — metal and stone under the hammer — tempering through hardship into great renown.

18th · Day 25, Snake hour (Sep 6, 9–11 a.m.)

Four pillars: 丙午 丙申 癸未 丁巳

Configuration: Heavenly Noble, Traveling Horse, Snake-Horse-Goat southern union

On the twenty-fifth day in the Snake hour, the Noble and the Traveling Horse arrive together and the fire quarter stands whole again — one born here finds patrons on far roads and riches as a great merchant; the southeast greatly favors them.

For a Gui day Tianyi, the Heavenly Noble, sits in the Rabbit and the Snake; the Snake hour is thus at once Noble and Traveling Horse — the day's Goat belongs to the Pig-Rabbit-Goat trine, whose horse waits in the Snake — and on top of this the Snake-Horse-Goat union completes. Noble and horse in one palace: meeting help amid the hurry, founding an estate away from home; commerce and travel suit best.

19th · Day 28, Tiger hour (Sep 9, 3–5 a.m.) — Ding You month

Four pillars: 丙午 丁酉 丙戌 庚寅

Configuration: Tiger-Horse-Dog fire trine

On the twenty-eighth day in the Tiger hour, the month pillar has already turned to Ding You — yet the fire trine still completes — one born here rises from adversity, the harder struck, the braver.

After White Dew the month pillar changes to Ding You and metal holds the season's warrant, yet Tiger, Horse and Dog remain complete — a fire trine born in a metal month is a boat rowed against the stream: a life that refuses defeat, beaten again and again only to fight again, until the great vessel is done.

20th · Day 28, Monkey hour (Sep 9, 3–5 p.m.) — Ding You month

Four pillars: 丙午 丁酉 丙戌 丙申

Configuration: Traveling Horse, Monkey-Rooster-Dog western union

The same day in the Monkey hour, the month pillar Ding You while Monkey, Rooster and Dog complete once more — fire and metal casting a sword — one born here has daring beyond the common and founds where others only settle; the west favors their journey.

The month's Rooster, the day's Dog, the hour's Monkey — the metal quarter gathers with the season's own warrant in a Rooster month; and the Monkey is likewise the Traveling Horse of a Bing day. Fire smelting autumn metal, the very image of sword-casting: a pioneer's talent, with deeds to be made in the west.

Kui Gang: The Day of September 3

And Geng metal, the day master, seated on the Dragon — Kui Gang's severity nourished by the earth that bears it — is why the old books say of such a day, "noble when the luck runs to strength"; worth a moment's reference.

Day-by-Day Quick Reference

One row per day. "All-yang day" marks days whose day pillar is wholly yang: on such days the six double-hours with yang stem and branch (Zi, Yin, Chen, Wu, Shen, Xu) yield all-yang charts. After September 8 the month pillar turns Ding You — a yin pillar — so even all-yang days no longer produce all-yang charts. Hours are given in pinyin (Zi = Rat, Chou = Ox, Yin = Tiger, Mao = Rabbit, Chen = Dragon, Si = Snake, Wu = Horse, Wei = Goat, Shen = Monkey, You = Rooster, Xu = Dog, Hai = Pig); lunar dates as month/day of the lunar calendar (7/1 = first day of the 7th lunar month, 7/29 = the 29th); "—" means the day has none.

A Word on Method

Birth-hour configurations draw on the classical logic of stems, branches and the five elements — a cultural framework ancient observers used to relate time and destiny. The verdicts above are written in an astrologer's antique voice for the pleasure of the tradition; they are not fate. What a life becomes still rests on one's own conduct and circumstance. Read, enjoy, and take from it what encourages you.

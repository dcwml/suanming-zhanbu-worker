Hour Omens of Lunar October 2026: Three Pairs of Northern Waters, Three Trines Together and All-Yang at the Month's End

Lunar October — early winter, when water begins to freeze and the ground begins to lock — runs from November 9 to December 8, 2026: thirty days, three hundred and sixty double-hours. This essay charts them one by one, recording only the extraordinary: completed trines, directional unions, all-yang bodies and the Kui Gang day — each verdict cast in an imperial astrologer's antique voice, then explained in plain words. Published on the very first day of the month, it is a record written entirely in advance: every configuration below still lies in the future, to be checked day by day as it arrives.

The Month at a Glance

In the year of Bing Wu (the Fire Horse), the month pillar runs Ji Hai — Major Snow falls on December 7 and turns the pillar to Geng Zi that very day, the month's last two days belonging to the new warrant. Light Snow falls on November 22. Across all 360 double-hours —

  • Not one all-yin chart: the year pillar Bing Wu is wholly yang, so an all-yin chart remains structurally impossible this month, as in July, August and September — a small lesson in how the calendar works;
  • Six all-yang hours gathered on the last day: the opposite of September's all-yang spread — the month pillar Ji Hai is a yin pillar holding the warrant for twenty-eight days, so none of the fifteen all-yang days (every second day, pillar wholly yang) yields its six all-yang hours; on the day after Major Snow (day 30, December 8), a Bing Chen all-yang day under the yang Geng Zi pillar, the six double-hours Zi, Yin, Chen, Wu, Shen and Xu stand all-yang together — the month's whole allowance of pure yang gathered into its final half-day. July spread them, August gathered them on the day the pillar turned, September spread them and lost them on the last day, October holds them until the very end: read in sequence, the four months show how the all-yang harvest hangs on the single thread of the month pillar's polarity;
  • Twenty grand configurations: six Pig-Rat-Ox northern water unions, five Tiger-Horse-Dog fire trines, four Pig-Rabbit-Goat wood trines, four Snake-Horse-Goat southern fire unions, and one Monkey-Rat-Dragon water trine. The wood trines need no year branch — the month's own Pig sits in the trine by birthright; the water trine waits for Major Snow to turn the month branch to the Rat — as the month's warrant changes, the trines change with it. The six northern unions fall in three mirrored pairs, one per decade: day 2 (Rat day, Ox hour) with day 3 (Ox day, Rat hour), day 14 with day 15, day 26 with day 27 — the month's most exact piece of architecture; days 2 and 3 carry four marks each (Noble, union, stem pairing, branch union), the richest hours of the month;
  • Three Kui Gang days: day 6 (November 14), a Ren Chen day; day 12 (November 20), a Wu Xu day; day 24 (December 2), a Geng Xu day — a decade apart each; the Tiger hours of the latter two are already graded above, while Ren Chen keeps its own section.

The Twenty Grand Configurations

1st · Day 2, Ox hour (Nov 10, 1–3 a.m.) — four marks in one hour

Four pillars: 丙午 己亥 戊子 癸丑

Configuration: Heavenly Noble, Pig-Rat-Ox northern union, day-hour stem pairing, day-hour branch union

On the second day in the Ox hour, the month's first configuration forms: the northern waters assemble with the Noble in the seat, Wu paired with Gui and Rat tied to Ox — one born here has noble waters at the gate and every blessing arriving together.

The month's own Pig takes the north's first seat; the day's Rat and the hour's Ox complete the Pig-Rat-Ox northern quarter — done by the month's second day. The Ox is likewise the Heavenly Noble of a Wu day (Wu and Geng's noble sits in the Ox and the Goat); the day stem Wu pairs with the hour stem Gui, and the day's Rat pairs with the hour's Ox — Noble, union, stem pairing and branch union, four marks in a single hour, the richest configuration of the month. Wu earth, the day master, holding the Noble and the water's flow: steadiness with circulation — finance, storehouses and the making of friends all suit.

2nd · Day 3, Zi hour (Nov 11, 11 p.m.–1 a.m.) — the mirror replayed

Four pillars: 丙午 己亥 己丑 甲子

Configuration: Heavenly Noble, Pig-Rat-Ox northern union, day-hour stem pairing, day-hour branch union

On the third day in the Zi hour, last night's configuration turns and replays — an Ox day's Rat hour, Jia paired with Ji and Ox tied to Rat, the Noble still at the table: one born here sees a blessing twice in a decade, each a reflection of the other.

The day's Ox and the hour's Rat complete the northern quarter again — last night a Rat day's Ox hour, tonight an Ox day's Rat hour, an exact inversion. The Rat is the Heavenly Noble of a Ji day (Yi and Ji's noble sits in the Rat and the Monkey); Jia pairs with Ji, Rat ties to Ox, four marks in one hour once more. Two nights joined, two configurations mirrored — the first of the month's three pairs of northern waters, replayed in each decade that follows.

3rd · Day 4, Dog hour (Nov 12, 7–9 p.m.)

Four pillars: 丙午 己亥 庚寅 丙戌

Configuration: Canopy Star, Tiger-Horse-Dog fire trine

On the fourth day in the Dog hour, the year's Horse, the day's Tiger and the hour's Dog forge the trine at dusk, the Canopy coming home — one born here is sunset smelting gold: talent kept within.

The year's Horse stands fixed all month; the day's Tiger and the hour's Dog complete the Tiger-Horse-Dog fire trine — the Dog being the trine's Canopy Star and fire's own storehouse. Geng metal, the day master, entering the furnace at sundown: tempering that ends in mastery, art stored in quiet places — craft and letters suit.

4th · Day 5, Goat hour (Nov 13, 1–3 p.m.)

Four pillars: 丙午 己亥 辛卯 乙未

Configuration: Canopy Star, Pig-Rabbit-Goat wood trine

On the fifth day in the Goat hour, the month's own Pig takes its seat and Rabbit and Goat complete the wood trine, the Canopy beside — one born here is a kindly tree by living water: nurture made second nature.

The wood trine's three branches are Pig, Rabbit and Goat — the month branch itself is a member, holding a seat by birthright; only the day's Rabbit and the hour's Goat need supply. October's peculiar ease: September's fire trine waited on the year's Horse, October's wood trine is born of the month itself. The Goat is the trine's Canopy Star besides. Xin metal, the day master, over the wood trine like a fine blade carving timber: subtlety that unravels difficulty — connoisseurship, physic and fine work suit.

5th · Day 7, Goat hour (Nov 15, 1–3 p.m.)

Four pillars: 丙午 己亥 癸巳 己未

Configuration: Snake-Horse-Goat southern union

On the seventh day in the Goat hour, the year's Horse, the day's Snake and the hour's Goat string the southern quarter — one born here governs toward the light, a cold spring facing the sun.

The day's Snake, the year's Horse and the hour's Goat — the southern fire quarter stands complete. Gui water, the day master, above the fire quarter like a spring mirroring the sun: warmth kept in a cool nature, softness steering the hard — letters, messages and southern ventures suit.

6th · Day 9, Rabbit hour (Nov 17, 5–7 a.m.)

Four pillars: 丙午 己亥 乙未 己卯

Configuration: General Star, Pig-Rabbit-Goat wood trine

On the ninth day in the Rabbit hour, the month's Pig leads the trine and the Rabbit star holds the center — one born here commands with method, the morning light in charge.

The month's Pig, the day's Goat and the hour's Rabbit form the Pig-Rabbit-Goat wood trine; the Rabbit sits at the center of the three — the trine's General Star. Yi wood, the day master, seated on the Star with its own trine behind: sure judgment at the council table, the clearest decisions made before noon — leading and lecturing hours favor.

7th · Day 9, Snake hour (Nov 17, 9–11 a.m.)

Four pillars: 丙午 己亥 乙未 辛巳

Configuration: Traveling Horse, Snake-Horse-Goat southern union

The same day in the Snake hour, the southern quarter assembles with the Traveling Horse at its post — one born here decides by morning and profits by motion.

The day's Goat, the year's Horse and the hour's Snake complete the southern quarter; the Snake is likewise the Goat day's Traveling Horse (the Pig-Rabbit-Goat trine keeps its horse in the Snake). Two double-hours from the morning's wood-trine General Star — Star at dawn, horse and fire quarter at noon, wood flowing into fire: inner counsel and outer motion together; journeys and new undertakings favor.

8th · Day 12, Tiger hour (Nov 20, 3–5 a.m.) — Kui Gang commands the day

Four pillars: 丙午 己亥 戊戌 甲寅

Configuration: Kui Gang day, Tiger-Horse-Dog fire trine

On the twelfth day in the Tiger hour, Kui Gang commands the day and the fire trine meets the dawn — one born here issues orders like a mountain: up early, deciding at once.

A Wu Xu Kui Gang day; the year's Horse, the day's Dog and the hour's Tiger complete the fire trine. Kui Gang's severity given the trine's light: daring and brightness together — arms, law and engineering suit. All twelve double-hours of this day carry the Kui Gang mark — see the section below.

9th · Day 14, Ox hour (Nov 22, 1–3 a.m.) — Light Snow turns the season

Four pillars: 丙午 己亥 庚子 丁丑

Configuration: Heavenly Noble, Pig-Rat-Ox northern union, day-hour branch union

On the fourteenth day in the Ox hour, as Light Snow turns the season the second pair of northern waters opens, the Noble still seated on the Ox — one born here finds help in quiet places while the weather changes.

Light Snow falls this very day, winter deepening; the day's Rat, the month's Pig and the hour's Ox complete the northern quarter — the same device as day 2, a full decade on. The Ox is the Heavenly Noble of a Geng day, with the Rat-Ox union tying it fast. Geng metal, the day master, clarified by the quarter's waters and backed by the Noble: metal and water tempering each other — plans that mature after long thought.

10th · Day 15, Zi hour (Nov 23, 11 p.m.–1 a.m.)

Four pillars: 丙午 己亥 辛丑 戊子

Configuration: Pig-Rat-Ox northern union, day-hour branch union

On the fifteenth day in the Zi hour, Ox day's Rat hour makes the mirrored pair and the northern waters stand whole — one born here turns emptiness to substance, thinking clearest in the still night.

The day's Ox and the hour's Rat invert into the union again, pairing with day 14 as day 3 paired with day 2. The Rat-Ox union ties the quarter together. Xin metal, the day master, over the waters: white metal, clear water — a lucid mind, night the hour of best counsel.

11th · Day 16, Dog hour (Nov 24, 7–9 p.m.)

Four pillars: 丙午 己亥 壬寅 庚戌

Configuration: Canopy Star, Tiger-Horse-Dog fire trine

On the sixteenth day in the Dog hour, the trine forms at dusk with the Canopy coming home, Ren water presiding — one born here is clever water steering fire: measure in haste and in ease.

The year's Horse, the day's Tiger and the hour's Dog complete the trine; the Dog is the Canopy Star, fire's storehouse. Ren water, the day master, above the fire: water and fire each to its office — composure through heat and cold alike, a talent for command and adjudication.

12th · Day 17, Goat hour (Nov 25, 1–3 p.m.)

Four pillars: 丙午 己亥 癸卯 己未

Configuration: Canopy Star, Pig-Rabbit-Goat wood trine

On the seventeenth day in the Goat hour, the wood trine stands again with the Canopy beside it — one born here gathers a kindly name, nurture deepening by the day.

The month's Pig, the day's Rabbit and the hour's Goat — three branches of the wood trine. The Goat is the trine's Canopy Star. Gui water, the day master, spent into the trine: well-water feeding thought — a warm pen and a teacher's patience.

Four pillars: 丙午 己亥 乙巳 癸未

Configuration: Snake-Horse-Goat southern union

On the nineteenth day in the Goat hour, the southern quarter strings its fires as a new decade opens — one born here shines without dazzling, better with every turn.

The day's Snake, the year's Horse and the hour's Goat — the southern fire quarter whole. Yi wood, the day master, flowering into the quarter: wood and fire shining through each other — a talent that shows outward, a voice that carries further each year.

14th · Day 21, Rabbit hour (Nov 29, 5–7 a.m.)

Four pillars: 丙午 己亥 丁未 癸卯

Configuration: General Star, Pig-Rabbit-Goat wood trine

On the twenty-first day in the Rabbit hour, the trine's General Star takes the morning watch — one born here gives orders that hold the middle: the day's plan made by dawn.

The month's Pig, the day's Goat and the hour's Rabbit form the wood trine, the Rabbit its General Star. Ding fire, the day master, fed by the trine and steered by the Star: decisions made in the morning, ranks kept in order.

15th · Day 21, Snake hour (Nov 29, 9–11 a.m.)

Four pillars: 丙午 己亥 丁未 乙巳

Configuration: Traveling Horse, Snake-Horse-Goat southern union

The same day in the Snake hour, the quarter assembles and the Traveling Horse quickens — one born here gathers by morning and gains by going.

The day's Goat, the year's Horse and the hour's Snake — the southern quarter whole; the Snake the Goat day's Traveling Horse. The same device as day 9: General Star at dawn, horse and quarter at noon, two configurations in sequence — settle first, then move, and the moving pays.

On the twenty-fourth day in the Tiger hour, Kui Gang sits again and the fire trine meets the dawn — one born here cuts clean and shines: victory at the first stride.

On the twenty-eighth day in the Dog hour, the Ji Hai month's last evening and the trine forms at dusk, the Canopy coming home — one born here gathers the light as the old month closes.

20th · Day 30, Monkey hour (Dec 8, 3–5 p.m.) — after Major Snow: the crowning hour

Four pillars: 丙午 庚子 丙辰 丙申

Configuration: all-yang chart, Monkey-Rat-Dragon water trine

On the thirtieth day in the Monkey hour, the day after Major Snow, an all-yang body and a water trine stand together — one born here is yang gathered at dusk with wise water running through: the month's crowning entry.

Major Snow fell yesterday and the month pillar turned to Geng Zi, a yang pillar — the all-yang chart at last attainable. This day is Bing Chen, all-yang itself: Bing above, Geng below, Bing again at day and hour — every stem and branch yang, so the six double-hours Zi, Yin, Chen, Wu, Shen and Xu all stand all-yang, and this is the grandest of them: the day's Dragon, the new month's Rat and the hour's Monkey complete the Monkey-Rat-Dragon water trine, the new warrant supplying its own seat. Bing fire, the day master, wholly yang above a water trine: brightness and wisdom in one frame, motion and stillness both assured — the closing flourish of thirty days and three hundred and sixty double-hours.

Kui Gang: Three Days in Company

Kui Gang names four day pillars — 庚辰, 庚戌, 壬辰, 戊戌 — of which the classics say: "severe in nature, firm of grip, decisive." This lunar month holds three of the four: day 6 (November 14), a Ren Chen day; day 12 (November 20), a Wu Xu day; day 24 (December 2), a Geng Xu day — arriving a decade apart. The two Xu days wear one face: the Ox hour's Noble, the Tiger hour's fire trine, the Rabbit hour's blossom, the noon General Star, the Goat hour's Noble, the Monkey hour's horse, the Dog hour's Canopy — auspicious marks mirroring each other across twelve double-hours; only Wu Xu adds its Yang Blade beside the noon Star and ties a second pairing at the Pig hour, while Geng Xu moves blade and pairing to the Rooster hour. Severity ringed by auspicious marks — hence the old books' word, "noble when the luck runs to strength"; worth a moment's reference.

Day-by-Day Quick Reference

One row per day. "All-yang day" marks days whose day pillar is wholly yang: on such days the six double-hours with yang stem and branch (Zi, Yin, Chen, Wu, Shen, Xu) would stand all-yang. This month's pillar Ji Hai is a yin pillar, so for twenty-eight days no all-yang day yields its hours; only on December 8 (day 30), the day after Major Snow turns the pillar to the yang Geng Zi, does the Bing Chen all-yang day produce all six — the month's six all-yang hours gathered on its final day, the exact reverse of September's half-month spread lost on its last day. Hours are given in pinyin (Zi = Rat, Chou = Ox, Yin = Tiger, Mao = Rabbit, Chen = Dragon, Si = Snake, Wu = Horse, Wei = Goat, Shen = Monkey, You = Rooster, Xu = Dog, Hai = Pig); lunar dates as month/day of the lunar calendar (10/1 = first day of the 10th lunar month, 10/30 = the 30th); "—" means the day has none.

A Word on Method

Birth-hour configurations draw on the classical logic of stems, branches and the five elements — a cultural framework ancient observers used to relate time and destiny. The verdicts above are written in an astrologer's antique voice for the pleasure of the tradition; they are not fate. What a life becomes still rests on one's own conduct and circumstance. Read, enjoy, and take from it what encourages you.

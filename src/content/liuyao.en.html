I Ching Hexagram Casting

Prepare three coins, focus your mind, cast six times, and receive an I Ching hexagram reading.

Step 1 · Preparation

Prepare three identical coins. Calm your mind and focus on what you wish to ask.

Casting rules: toss all three coins together; if a coin lands on its edge, redo that toss; record each result, six tosses in total (from the bottom line to the top).

Step 2 · Record Tosses

Convention: inscribed/heads side = Yin, other side = Yang.

Today is Jǐ Wèi 己未 — the stem Jǐ is yin earth, the moist soil of fields and gardens, soft and nourishing to all things; the branch Wèi is dry earth, and also the tomb-storehouse of wood, as summer qi recedes and autumn qi gathers in. Jǐ earth rests upon Wèi earth, kindred helping kindred, deep-rooted and firmly founded; the Nayin is again Heavenly Fire, the sun warmly nurturing the thick earth — a picture of stillness and storage. The day deity is Yùtáng 玉堂, an auspicious spirit of the Yellow Path, clear and noble, fitting for offerings and prayers; Mǔcāng 母仓 governs nurture and accumulation — so the favorable list holds sacrifice and meeting relatives and friends alongside the earthworks of breaking ground, building dikes, digging ponds and plugging holes. Yet the fierce spirits Yuèshà 月煞, Yuèxū 月虚, Xuèzhī 血支, Tiānzéi 天贼, Wǔxū 五虚 and Bāzhuān 八专 preside together: Yuèshà governs obstruction, Xuèzhī governs blood and injury, Tiānzéi governs theft and loss, Wǔxū and Bāzhuān govern emptiness and dissipation — so consecration, traveling, construction, raising beams, moving into a house, installing doors, installing a stove and cutting garments are all placed on the unfavorable list. Today is also the first day of the seventh lunar month — in folk custom, the opening of the Ghost Gate — and matters of storage and concealment such as encoffining, moving the coffin, breaking ground for burial and burial are especially fitting, in keeping with the way of honoring the dead. The qi of this day is centered on stillness, on storage and on reverence: walk carefully and humbly, and do not move in grand style.

Zodiac Fortune · Goat

Today's branch is Wèi (未), which corresponds to the Goat — today's featured zodiac. Those born in a Goat year sit squarely on the day's branch and hold the duty, with Jīntáng 金堂 and Yùtáng 玉堂 presiding over the day, bringing the joy of noble documents — official paperwork, contracts and examinations may hear good news, and negotiations and conversations will find ready trust: a fine day for Goats to meet relatives and friends, discuss matters and pay visits. For fortune, the Wealth God dwells due North and the Fortune God likewise — seek wealth to the north; the Joy God is in the Northeast, and matters of celebration may face that way. Mind three cautions: first, today clashes with the Ox and the Sha lies West — avoid direct confrontation with those born in an Ox year, and postpone long journeys westward; second, traveling, moving into a house, raising beams and installing a stove are all unfavorable — postpone relocation, entering a new home and major house renovations; breaking ground, building dikes and digging ponds are favorable, yet construction and raising beams are not — Goats with building projects should start on foundations and waterworks today, and wait for another day to raise beams and set doors; third, today falls on the first day of the seventh lunar month, the day the Ghost Gate opens in folk custom — Goats should join ancestor worship with sincerity, keep words and deeds circumspect, and avoid coming home late at night. In health, earth qi is heavy and dryness is rising, and spleen and stomach easily stagnate — Goats should eat lightly today and avoid overindulging in cold, raw foods, guarding against damp stagnation and bloating.

Folklore

Today is the first day of the seventh lunar month — in folk tradition, the day the Ghost Gate opens. In traditional belief, from this day the gates of the underworld gradually open, and wandering souls with no descendants to offer them sacrifice return to the human world to receive offerings; the month peaks at the Zhōngyuán 中元 Festival on the fifteenth of the seventh month, when the Earth Official of the Three Officials examines the registers and pardons sins, and closes again at the month's end. Folk custom during this month centers on ancestor worship, universal salvation and almsgiving — blessings for the living, offerings for the dead; the Yúlánpén 盂兰盆 tale of Maudgalyayana rescuing his mother is likewise a story of delivering departed kin in this very month. Look back at today's almanac: sacrifice heads the favorable list, and encoffining, moving the coffin, breaking ground for burial and burial all stand in a row — the ancient art of date-selection has indeed given this first day of the seventh month over to matters of storage and of reverently seeing off the dead. The so-called Ghost Month is not meant to frighten: its heart is reverence for ancestors, remembrance of the departed, and acting with due measure — worship sincerely, keep words and deeds restrained, and wander little at night during this month. That is the wisdom the old almanacs hand down: when the gates of the underworld open, what the human world ought to cultivate is its own heart.

Metaphysics Insight

The Yellow Path's procession of six now reaches its fourth — the Heavenly Virtue. The day before yesterday spoke of the Heavenly Virtue Union: the virtue is like a high official who cannot call in person every day, and the arrival of the union-spirit is like an envoy sent ahead with word; today it is the principal himself holding court — among the twelve sky-spirits the Heavenly Virtue bears another name, Treasured Light, the radiance of precious things, and today the spirit column writes Heavenly Virtue while the auspicious roster writes Treasured Light: one name in two places, one and the same star — virtue in person, a degree stronger than the envoy sent before. What is the Heavenly Virtue? The grace of heaven itself. The ancients read the way of heaven thus: the four seasons proceed and the hundred things are born — heaven's virtue lies in nurturing all things; landed in the calendar, this becomes the day the Heavenly Virtue presides: blessings gather, the malign spirits lie hidden — the old books held that where the virtue stands, the hostile spirits fall back of themselves. Hence today's almanac shows a striking sight: marriage and bed-setting stand in the same favored column as encoffining and burial — one affair the beginning of a life, the other its homecoming; on ordinary days the two keep far apart, yet on a Heavenly Virtue day they may share one day: the joy of beginning and the peace of returning both lie within the care of heaven's nurturing virtue — grace makes no distinctions, and so both the start and the finish stand blessed. Look again at the roster's Crowing and Barking — the image of the golden rooster's crow and the jade dog's bark: the ancients held that a burial ground hearing rooster and dog bodes gathered vitality and rest for the departed, a spirit reserved for interment alone; and today the day's branch sits exactly at Yǒu, the Rooster — the golden rooster in its right place, its crow answering the hour, the Virtue shining above and the crow answering below: the almanac's punctiliousness assigns even a rooster's crow its seat. Four of the Yellow Path six are now told: the Azure Dragon for sprouting, the Bright Hall for command, the Golden Casket for the treasury, the Heavenly Virtue for nurturing — beginning and homecoming alike committed to grace; two remain, the Jade Hall and the Commander of Destiny, their turn to come.

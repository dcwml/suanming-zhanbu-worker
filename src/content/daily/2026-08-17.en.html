Today is Guǐ Hài 癸亥 — the stem Guǐ is yin water, the water of rain, dew and mountain springs, subtle and penetrating; the branch Hài is the very home where water flourishes, so stem and branch are both water and the water qi gathers in full. The Nayin, Great Sea Water, adds to the flood, and with the month still in Shēn metal begetting yet more water, the waters run boundless. Yet the presiding deity is Gōuchén 勾陈 of the Black Path, governing delay and dispute, and the inauspicious spirits Tiāngāng 天罡, Sǐshén 死神, Yuèhài 月害, Yóuhuò 游祸, Wǔxū 五虚 and Chóngrì 重日 arrive together — the day branch Hài is precisely harmed by the month branch Shēn, hence the name Month Harm — so the unfavorable list runs long: marriage, litigation, treating illness, purchasing property, making beams, praying for blessings, burial, planting, felling trees and installing gates should all be approached with care. Fortunately the auspicious spirits Tiāndé 天德, Sìxiàng 四相, Xiāngrì 相日 and Pǔhù 普护 uphold the day — Tiāndé turns misfortune into fortune and Pǔhù shelters all things — so sacrifice, bathing, decorating walls, leveling roads and installing a stove remain unimpeded. In sum, the qi of this day favors sweeping and setting in order, bathing to cleanse the body, and sacrifice to settle the heart, but not great undertakings, marriages or the purchase of property; whatever can wait may wait for a fairer day.

Zodiac Fortune · Pig

Today's branch is Hài (亥), which corresponds to the Pig — today's featured zodiac. Those born in a Pig year sit squarely on the day's own branch, and so receive today's qi most directly: stem and branch both water, the water qi at its fullest, with Gōuchén 勾陈 of the Black Path presiding and Yuèhài 月害, Chóngrì 重日 and Wǔxū 五虚 arriving alongside — today's fortune calls for stillness rather than motion, and mending rather than launching. Bathing and cleansing, sacrifice and prayer, sweeping and tidying are all favorable; if you settle your mind, put your home in order, read and reflect, the day suits you well. Mind three cautions: first, today clashes with the Snake and the Sha lies West — avoid journeys westward, and deal gently with those born in a Snake year; second, with Chóngrì and Wǔxū together, weddings, signing contracts, purchasing property and heavy investments may fall through or turn around — better to wait for a fairer day; third, the Month Harm falls on Hài itself, so in partnerships and joint ventures beware of gossip and meddling tongues — put important agreements in writing and confirm the details. For wealth, the Wealth God stands in the due South, the Joy God in the Southeast and the Fortune God in the Northeast — if you must be out and about, these are the directions to favor. In health, Guǐ Hài water runs high as autumn cools — Pigs would do well to keep warm and ward off chill; those with weaker kidneys, bladder or lower back should avoid late nights and overwork, and eat warm, plain foods.

Folklore

Today's presiding deity is Gōuchén 勾陈, one of the six spirits of the Black Path. Gōuchén is first of all a star name — in ancient astronomy it sits within the Purple Forbidden Enclosure beside the North Star, and the ancients revered it as a star of the palace and the army, commanding war and conflict, its image weighty, solemn and slow to move. Date-selection borrowed the name into its system of presiding spirits: Qīnglóng 青龙, Míngtáng 明堂, Jīnkuì 金匮, Tiāndé 天德, Yùtáng 玉堂 and Sīmìng 司命 form the six spirits of the Yellow Path, while Tiānxíng 天刑, Zhūquè 朱雀, Báihǔ 白虎, Tiānláo 天牢, Xuánwǔ 玄武 and Gōuchén 勾陈 form the six of the Black Path — the twelve taking turns to preside over the days in endless rotation. Yellow Path days carry a clear and luminous qi in which most things may proceed; Black Path days carry a dense and sluggish qi in which undertakings meet resistance. When Gōuchén presides, the qi lingers and clings, and its image is dispute, delay and entanglement — hence Gōuchén days bode ill for marriage, litigation and great construction: lawsuits begun on such days are most prone to drag on unresolved, and marriages risk quarrels that refuse to settle. Yet the old masters left room for maneuver: a Black Path day is not ill for everything. Sacrifice, bathing, sweeping the house and setting things in order are matters of cleansing body and mind and gathering oneself in reflection, and they do not run against the Black Path qi — which is why today, for all its long unfavorable list, still welcomes bathing, sweeping, sacrifice and prayer. There are no wholly auspicious days, only undertakings suited to each day; the wise are not bound by the calendar — and that, precisely, is the flexibility at the heart of traditional date-selection.

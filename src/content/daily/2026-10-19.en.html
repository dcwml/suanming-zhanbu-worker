Metaphysics Insight

Today's favorable column holds seven entries, and six of them circle one single matter — encoffining, breaking earth, opening the grave vault, burial, removing and donning mourning garments, all links of the funeral's one chain. The key that admits this whole funeral stands at the foot of the auspicious list: Míngfèiduì, the "Crowing and Barking Pair." Míngwū and Míngfèiduì are the almanac's dedicated pair of burial days: the Crowing-and-Barking day presides over burial, and its companion stands a step beside it, favorable for breaking earth and opening the vault alike. The name takes its image from the plainest of things — a rooster's crow, a dog's bark, the sounds of human hearths. The ancients said of burial, "to bury is to hide away": a child hides the parent's form in the earth, wishing only quiet for the resting place. And folk tradition tells that when burial falls on a crowing-and-barking day, it is as if the golden rooster crowed above and the jade dog barked without — and where that sound reaches, the restless shades give way: the departed gain the peace of returning to the soil, the living gain the full rite of seeing them off. Here sound is not clamor; sound is a keeping watch. Why do Yellow Path days come often, while true burial days come hard? Because the taboos around burial are the most tangled of any matter — the Doubled Mourning, the Returning Day, the Plague of Earth, too many to outrun; only when the virtue spirits attend and the Crowing and Barking holds the day do the sha withdraw, and only then dare the almanac set the word "burial" into its favorable column. Today is exactly such a day: Heaven's Virtue and the Month's Virtue standing guard, the fire trine bracing from either side, the Crowing and Barking Pair opening the road, the Director of Destinies pressing the Yellow Path over all of it — hence this column of six linked matters. A cold-looking line of entries, and yet among the most humane in the book: the sixty Jiazi turn without pause, and the old almanac has managed to put even "farewell" in its proper place.

Gēng Chén follows Jǐ Mǎo: yesterday the yin stem of earth closed the City Wall Earth couplet, today the yang stem of metal opens a new chapter — Gēng is the hardness of yang metal, seated upon the damp earth of Chén, and earth begets metal, so the metal takes root; the Na Yin is White Wax Metal, metal fresh from the first smelting, firm of substance but not yet full of breath. The pivot of the whole day turns on one word: clash. The day's Chén stands against the month's Xū — Chén striking Xū makes this a Month Breaker day, the day branch breaking against the month's command, the breath of the hour collapsing at the point of collision; what breaks disperses, and what disperses is spent — the Great Depletion follows the Breaker as the old rules decree. Hence opening a business is avoided (wealth bleeds away) and marriages are avoided (no roof holds firm on a breaking day); Wǎngwáng, the Departure Doom, sits in the column — journeys and taking office are both unwise; the Nine Voids keep the watch — opening granaries and taking in wealth is set aside; the Four Strikes attend — moving earth and distant service both stand down. Yesterday's closing line promised "tomorrow the Azure Dragon sets its seal again" — and today the Azure Dragon keeps the day, the six of the Yellow Path beginning a fresh round, with seven auspicious spirits in the roster: the Heavenly Grace, the Mother's Granary, the Lunar Grace, the No Obstructor, the Benefactor of Heirs, the Resolving Spirit, and the Azure Dragon — a lineup not to be dismissed. Yet the Month Breaker holds power, and even the first of the Yellow Path can only guard one use for the word "break": the Heavenly Grace grants remission of old faults, the Lunar Grace bestows fresh favor, the Resolving Spirit dissolves old afflictions — three spirits, one voice, all pointing at a single work: pardoning old faults, dispersing old grudges, clearing away old stains. So the favored list of six is a breaker's litany through and through: worship to confess, bathing to cleanse, dissolution to untangle old ties, demolishing a house and razing walls to tear down the old — tearing down on a breaking day is riding the hour, and "all other matters set aside" draws the boundary around it all. Start of Winter draws near (Nov 7): autumn metal about to fade, winter water gathering to hide — a breaking day made to clear out what has gathered and settle what has been owed; when the old is cleared the new opens of itself. Clash the Dog, the Sha lies South: Chén and Xū stand opposed — keep clear of contention with Dog people, and let southern journeys take a slower step.

Zodiac Fortune · Dragon 龙

Today's earthly branch is Chén, which corresponds to the Dragon — making the Dragon the protagonist of the day, and Dragon people keep a natal day that falls under the Month Breaker: Chén strikes Xū, and the branch struck is their own natal branch, so of the twelve days this one stirs their field most — affairs breed reversals, and the great matters of opening a business, signing contracts, pledging betrothal and marrying are all better set aside today; pressing forward invites depletion, for the Great Depletion and the Nine Voids stand in the column and the surest way to lose wealth is to reach for it. The good news is that the Azure Dragon keeps the Yellow Path over this very day, with the Resolving Spirit and the Heavenly Grace at hand — and the fortune of Dragon people today hides in one word: clearing. Settle the debts, return the borrowed, tear out and tidy what has lingered, mend the quarrel and dissolve the grudge — whatever belongs to "clearing away the old," done with the current, carries the Azure Dragon's nobility with it; the old things and old accounts long put off at home meet their one-stroke settlement today. The Mother's Granary keeps the day: worship and prayers, honoring one's elders — blessings for the family's long life fall on open ears. The Wealth God stands Due East: small money may be tended, great money stays untouched; investment and the opening of granaries wait for another day. Clash the Dog, the Sha lies South: avoid quarrels and partnerships with Dog people, and let southern journeys go carefully. Gēng metal answers the lungs and large intestine, Chén earth the stomach and spleen — autumn dryness lingers and damp earth commands; mind the breath and keep the food clean. Bathing is favored — a hot bath to wash off the dust is precisely the spirit of the day: clear the old.

Metaphysics Insight

Yesterday the Black Path closed its six; today the Azure Dragon sets its seal — the Yellow Path opens a fresh round, and the twelve begin to walk again. Yet the true protagonist of today is not the Dragon at all, but the name that heads the inauspicious column: the Month Breaker. The month's command is the branch that reigns; the day branch that stands against it is called broken. The month Wù Xū meets the day Gēng Chén — Chén strikes Xū, and the breaking day is made. The old calendars say it plainly: "the Month Breaker is the day the month's command strikes"; on a breaking day the breath disperses, and what disperses is wealth spent — so the Great Depletion arrives on the Breaker's heels, and the old rule reads the day as "nothing accomplished, wealth drained": great matters keep away. The Chén–Xū clash carries a name of its own: Xū and Hài are the Heaven's Net, Chén and Sì the Earth's Snare — Chén striking Xū is net striking snare, a collapse of the day's breath beyond the ordinary. And yet the breaking day is not all ill — the almanac leaves a few works to be done on it: worship, bathing, dissolving old ties, demolishing a house, razing walls, with all other matters set aside. The reason lies in one word: riding the current. The breath of heaven and earth breaks on this day; the old order comes apart — tear down with it, and the collapsing force works for you: razing the ruined wall, clearing the gathered grime, dissolving the old grudge, settling the old account, all borrow the hour. Build against it, and every new work stands on collapsing ground: opening a business, founding a marriage, setting out for office — nothing escapes the breaking breath; the Departure Doom is precisely the warning that to go is to perish: journeys and appointments alike keep indoors. This is why the Compendium of Calendars and Records lists for the breaking day exactly the works of demolishing houses and razing walls, seeking medicine and treating illness — breaking against breaking, poison drawing out poison. Five days hence comes Start of Winter: the autumn harvest is in, the winter store awaits — sweep the granary clean, settle what is owed, clear the old on this breaking day, and the new opens after Start of Winter of itself. Fortune was never more than a question of with the current or against it; there is no absolutely lucky day and no absolutely unlucky one — and that is the dialectic the almanac has kept hidden for a thousand years.

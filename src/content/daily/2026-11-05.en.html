Guǐ Wèi follows Rén Wǔ: yesterday the yang stem of water opened anew, today the yin stem of water flows on — Guǐ is the water of dew and rain, seated upon the earth of Wèi, and Wèi conceals Dīng: Dīng is the Indirect Wealth of Guǐ water, the wealth star tucked away in the storehouse, answering the "collecting wealth" set down in the favored list; and Rén Wǔ with Guǐ Wèi stand as a paired couplet sharing one Na Yin — Willow Wood — two days playing a single willow tune. Yesterday the Heavenly Punisher took the floor; today the Vermilion Bird relieves it — the second of the Black Path six, the spirit that governs tongues and quarrels, keeps the watch. Yet the auspicious list stands fivefold — the Heavenly Grace, the Mother's Granary, the Four Ministers, the No Bride Unclaimed and the Jade Eaves — ranged five against five, and the light of the blessings is enough to hold the floor, which is why the favored list dares to set down collecting wealth. Worship rides on the Heavenly Grace — the grace of heaven bestowing, a fine day for honoring ancestors, offering prayers and giving thanks; the Mother's Granary governs granary and kitchen, and making the stove is that spirit's very trade; capturing and hunting — after Frost's Descent the hunting season stands open, and the land hunt meets no hindrance — only the Water-Dragon Toucher stands in the list, so crossing rivers and going by boat ask a different day. The four avoided matters run one line: groundbreaking and breaking earth for burial are both works of axe and spade, and burial is of the same household — with Yuèxíng, the Moon's Punisher, on duty, the works of the edged tools stand aside; opening a business is avoided because the Five Voids leave the breath of wealth thin — a business founded on a void day stands on an unfilled base. Start of Winter follows the day after (Nov 7): autumn metal all but spent, winter water soon to arrive — Guǐ water comes into season in a breath, and keeping still while gathering wealth suits the storing breath of the season. Clash the Ox, the Sha lies West: Chǒu and Wèi stand opposed — keep clear of contention and partnership with Ox people, and let western journeys take a slower step.

Metaphysics Insight

Yesterday the Heavenly Punisher took the floor; today the Vermilion Bird relieves it — the round of the twelve spirits reaches its fourth seat, and the second of the Black Path six (Tiānxíng, Zhūquè, Bái Hǔ, Tiānláo, Xuánwǔ, Gōuchén) steps forward in person. By pedigree the Vermilion Bird is far older than the round of the twelve itself: it stands with the Azure Dragon, the White Tiger and the Black Tortoise as one of the Four Symbols, the collective name of the seven southern mansions — Wells, Ghost, Willow, Star, Extended Net, Wings and Axletrace linked across the sky, in which the ancients saw a bird spreading its wings: the Willow its beak, the Extended Net its crop, the Wings its pinions — vermilion in hue, of the nature of fire, garrisoning the south, answering summer and the trigram Lǐ. Four beasts hold the four palaces: the Azure Dragon winds in the east, the White Tiger crouches in the west, the Black Tortoise coils turtle and snake in the north, while the southern mansions alone take the form of a bird — the ancients read the sky as a map of quarters, and the twenty-eight mansions were the fourfold atlas hung upon heaven. Yet this spirit of the south, entered into the roster of the almanac, drew the Black Path's commission: the Vermilion Bird governs tongues and quarrels, papers and disputes — among the six spirits of liùyáo divination it keeps charge of documents, messages, wrangling and litigation, and every signed contract and letter exchanged falls within its office; hence the folk saying "when the Vermilion Bird opens its beak, words turn to strife." Yesterday's Tiānxíng was the minister of justice, whose wound lies in axe and blade; today's Zhūquè is the messenger at the gate, whose wound lies in lips and tongue — each Black Path spirit keeps its own office, and each day has its own way of stepping around it: on a Punisher day avoid the edged tools, on a Vermilion Bird day avoid quarrels, sign with care and argue less. The season, too, stands on today's side: the Vermilion Bird is the fire god of southern summer, and now Frost's Descent has passed with Start of Winter but two days off — the breath of fire winds down as the season of water approaches; the bird of summer arrived at this turn of the year flies with a lowered flame, and with the Heavenly Grace, the Mother's Granary, the Four Ministers, the No Bride Unclaimed and the Jade Eaves ranged fivefold, the light of the blessings is enough to hold the floor. A Black Path day has never been a verdict of ill: know the office each spirit keeps, avoid its edge and use its opening — this is the plain meaning of the almanac's four characters, "seek the auspicious, shun the harmful."

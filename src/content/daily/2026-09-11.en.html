No undertakings advisable

On Wù Zǐ day, the day stem Wù (Earth) sits atop Zǐ (Water), where Earth conquers Water to produce the wealth star; yet Zǐ harbors Guǐ Water, making Water strong and Earth weak, so one must guard against the predicament of too much wealth overwhelming the body. White Dew has just passed and Yǒu Metal commands the season; Metal produces Water, so Zǐ Water flourishes with formidable momentum. Though Wù Earth is thick and heavy, it cannot avoid being soaked through. Today Sīmìng of the Yellow Path presides, governing health, longevity and household peace, joined by Shídé, Yángdé, Mínrì and Yùyǔ — four auspicious spirits gathering together, creating abundant fortunate qi. Yet the taboo column lists only "no undertakings advisable," which seems an absolute prohibition; in truth, five inauspicious spirits — Hékuí, Sǐshén, Tiānlì, Zhìsǐ and Wǎngwáng — surround the day, and Sīmìng's auspicious influence mainly favors "conservation" rather than "advancement." Thus sacrifice, bathing, decorating walls and leveling roads — matters of purification and repair — are favored, while all else is best avoided. The overall tone is "quiet conservation and purification": major undertakings, new ventures, long journeys and contract signing should all be avoided, while minor repairs and spiritual cleansing carry no harm.

Zodiac Fortune · Rat 鼠

Today's earthly branch is Zǐ, which corresponds to the Rat — making the Rat the zodiac protagonist of the day. On this Wù Zǐ day, the Rat sits in its own position, bringing mutual support; yet Wù Earth presses down from above, creating an Earth-conquers-Water pattern. The Rat's energy runs strong today, but external pressure and depletion must be guarded against. Today favors sacrifice, bathing, decorating walls and leveling roads; in career matters it is better to hold than to attack — signing contracts, negotiations and launching new projects are best postponed. Home projects such as decorating walls and leveling roads are acceptable, but large-scale construction, moving house or relocating are taboo. Sīmìng presides, ensuring household peace and physical well-being; this is a suitable day for quiet reflection and internal organization. Shídé and Yángdé together keep petty people at bay and reduce quarrels and disputes. Yet Hékuí and Sǐshén together warn against old ailments recurring or unexpected minor injuries; Wǎngwáng is present, so long journeys, visiting the sick and attending funerals should be avoided. The Wealth God resides in the Due North, offering modest prospects for conserving wealth in that direction. Today clashes with the Horse and brings sha to the South — those born in the Year of the Horse should exercise caution and avoid heading south.

Metaphysics Insight

Thunder Fire (霹雳火) is one of the Na Yin Five Elements, corresponding to the Wù Zǐ and Jǐ Chǒu days; its image is drawn from lightning and thunder — erupting suddenly with irresistible force. The San Ming Tong Hui states: "Thunder Fire is a thread of light, a command from the highest heaven; it has the momentum of golden serpents and the charge of armored cavalry," describing its fierce intensity and awe-inspiring power. This is no ordinary candle flame; it is born from the communion of heaven and earth and the clash of yin and yang, hence its nature is fierce and its momentum sudden, governing transformation, surprise and sudden nobility. In the Wù Zǐ day pillar, Wù Earth is the riverbank and Zǐ Water is the river; placing water atop earth would normally suggest flooding, yet the Na Yin Thunder Fire hidden within achieves the harmonious interaction of water and fire, turning danger into mutual support. Those born under Thunder Fire tend to be resolute in character and decisive in action, with the ability to turn misfortune into blessing and find life in desperate situations; yet they must also guard against the clash of water and fire, lest excessive hardness lead to fracture. Today, as Thunder Fire presides over the day, though the fire burns fiercely, White Dew has just passed and Metal is strong while Water thrives; the fire qi is restrained by water and earth and cannot spread unchecked — this perfectly embodies the image of "the hidden dragon does not act": build momentum and await the right moment, rather than advancing rashly or acting recklessly.

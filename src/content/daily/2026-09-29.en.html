All else contracts: Xián Chí stirs romance and scandal, so marriage steps aside; Jiǔ Kǎn and Jiǔ Jiāo are unkind to timber and hearth, so roofing, beams and coffin-making all wait.

Today's earthly branch is Wǔ, which corresponds to the Horse — making the Horse the zodiac protagonist of the day. The Horse presides on its own branch while year and day pillars double the Bǐng fire, one current running through heaven and earth: Horse people are brimming with vigor and drive today, and the word "capturing" in the favored column is the footnote — finish the pending, tie up the loose ends, draw in the nets, all in one sweep. Yet blazing fire turns restless, and with fire striking metal the wealth is bright but the spirits are heavy: keep your edge sheathed. Sacrifice and capping stand in the favored column: honor the ancestors, quiet the mind, set robes and appearance in order, and formal occasions will carry their own composure. Fire runs high in the body — mind the heart, the eyes and your sleep; drink more water and go light on spice. The day clashes with the Rat and the Sha lies North: those born in the Year of the Rat should keep a low profile in all matters, avoid journeys toward the North, and refrain from quarrels with Horses.

Metaphysics Insight

Today's presiding spirit is Jīn Kuì, the Gold Chest — an auspicious spirit among the Twelve Officers who rotate through the days. The ancients set twelve spirits in rotation — the Azure Dragon (Qīnglóng), Bright Hall (Míngtáng), Heavenly Punishment (Tiānxíng), Vermilion Bird (Zhūquè), Gold Chest (Jīn Kuì), Treasured Light (Bǎoguāng), White Tiger (Báihǔ), Jade Hall (Yùtáng), Heavenly Prison (Tiānláo), Dark Warrior (Xuánwǔ), Siming (司命) and Gouchen (勾陈); six of them — the Azure Dragon, Bright Hall, Gold Chest, Treasured Light, Jade Hall and Siming — dwell on the Yellow Path, the remaining six on the Black, and from this comes the very phrase "auspicious day of the Yellow Path." The Gold Chest's image is the treasury of heaven: a treasury stores, and so it presides over blessed protection and abundant reserves — on its days one offers sacrifices and prays, gathers wealth into store, honoring the single word "keep." Curiously, a Yellow Path day is not good for everything: though the Gold Chest holds today, Tiān Zéi and Dà Bài narrow the favored column to three gates — sacrifice, capping and capturing — with no other matters undertaken. The almanac's favored and taboo columns were never the verdict of one spirit alone, but a weighed judgment reached by the Yellow and Black Paths and every auspicious and inauspicious spirit checking one another; grasp that weighing, and you have truly learned to read the almanac.

Folklore

Among today's auspicious spirits is "Chúshén 除神", one of the traditional "Twelve Day Officers" (Jiàn, Chú, Mǎn, Píng, Dìng, Zhí, Pò, Wēi, Chéng, Shōu, Kāi, Bì). These twelve officers rotate daily based on the relationship between the day branch and the month branch, each carrying its own theme — Jiàn for initiating, Chú for clearing away, Mǎn for fulfillment, Shōu for gathering in. The Chinese almanac (黄历) has used this framework for over a thousand years to determine daily auspices. Today's officer Chú aligns with the recommended activities of cleansing, sweeping, and repairing walls — the imagery of clearing out the old to make way for the new.

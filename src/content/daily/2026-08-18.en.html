Today is Jiǎ Zǐ 甲子 — the stem Jiǎ is yang wood, the timber of ridgepoles and beams, upright and quick with growth; the branch Zǐ is the very home where water flourishes, water begetting wood, and with the month still in Shēn metal feeding the waters, wood drinks deep and stands vigorous. The Nayin, Gold in the Sea — gold sunk to the sea floor, its treasure not yet revealed — mirrors well the image of Jiǎ wood hidden within Zǐ water. The presiding deity is Qīnglóng 青龙 of the Yellow Path, and the auspicious spirits Tiān'ēn 天恩, Shíyáng 时阳, Mínrì 民日, Sānhé 三合, Línrì 临日, Shíyīn 时阴 and Fúshēng 福生 all gather to uphold the day, while only Sǐqì 死气 lurks among the inauspicious — the auspicious qi stands at its height. Month branch Shēn and day branch Zǐ meet moreover in partial Three Harmony (the Shēn–Zǐ–Chén water frame), so harmonious qi converges: today favors marriage, moving into a house, relocating, travel, trading and signing contracts, construction and breaking ground, all the way down to burial and encoffining — nearly all matters may proceed, and only Daoist rites, opening for business, opening the granary, installing a stove and building ships are set apart. In sum, the qi of this day is clear and vigorous, harmonious and auspicious — truly a lucky day for great undertakings, and whatever you have long been planning may well be carried forward today.

Zodiac Fortune · Rat

Today's branch is Zǐ (子), which corresponds to the Rat — today's featured zodiac. Those born in a Rat year sit squarely on the day's own branch and so receive today's qi most directly: Jiǎ wood is nourished by Zǐ water, Qīnglóng 青龙 of the Yellow Path presides, and eight auspicious spirits — Sānhé 三合, Tiān'ēn 天恩 and Fúshēng 福生 among them — uphold the day, so the Rat's fortune today is broadly excellent and full of vigor. At work, the day favors trading and signing contracts: negotiations, agreements and partnerships may all bear fruit, and anyone long weighing a move, construction or renovation may act within today. Wealth favors steady advance and exchange, but not grand openings, opening the granary or heavy spending — the unfavorable list warns of scattered qi, so build quietly and hold your ground. In relationships, Sānhé governs union and good matches: an excellent day to broker introductions, settle engagements and bring people together. Mind one caution: today clashes with the Horse and the Sha lies South — avoid journeys southward, and settle any dispute with those born in a Horse year gently. The Joy God and Wealth God both stand in the Northeast and the Fortune God in the due North — favor these directions when you go out about business. In health, Zǐ water runs high as autumn cools: Rats should keep warm and ward off chill, and those with weaker kidneys, bladder or ears should avoid late nights and overwork, eating warm and plain.

Folklore

Today's auspicious spirits include Sānhé 三合 — the Three Harmony — one of the most sociable stars in the almanac's spirit system. It grows from the twelve stages of life by which the five elements wax and wane across the branches: Shēn is water's birth, Zǐ its fullness and Chén its storage, and when the three branches Shēn, Zǐ and Chén meet, the qi of water runs its whole course from birth to fullness to ingathering — hence "Three Harmony". By the same reckoning Hài–Mǎo–Wèi frames wood, Yín–Wǔ–Xū frames fire and Sì–Yǒu–Chǒu frames metal, four harmonious groupings in all. Today is a Zǐ day in a Shēn month, so month and day meet in partial harmony — birth and fullness joined — and Sānhé arrives to uphold the day: its qi is gentle, and undertakings find willing partners. Tradition holds Three Harmony days favorable for marriages and covenants, negotiations, matchmaking and everything governed by union, and folk date-selection has long prized them as wedding days. Sānhé travels far in destiny studies too: three harmonies among the four pillars speak of friendly ties and ready helpers, and a Three Harmony hour is said to carry undertakings smoothly to completion. The Xiéjì Biànfāng Shū counts Sānhé among the auspicious spirits to be sought, and today, if any matter calls for cooperation, negotiation or a signed agreement, it may well ride this harmonious qi to success.

Metaphysics Insight

The Three-Harmony (Sānhé) triad is a concept of great importance in both destiny analysis and auspicious date selection. It refers to the formation of a complete elemental bureau when three of the twelve earthly branches come together. There are four triads: Shēn-Zǐ-Chén forming Water, Hài-Mǎo-Wèi forming Wood, Yín-Wǔ-Xū forming Fire, and Sì-Yǒu-Chǒu forming Metal. The Three Harmony takes its pattern from "growth, peak and storage" — using the Metal triad as an example: Sì is the birth (growth) stage of Metal, Yǒu is its imperial prosperity (peak), and Chǒu is its grave treasury (storage). Joined end to end like an unbroken ring, they complete the full cycle of Metal's qi. Today, on the Jǐ Chǒu day, Chǒu is the treasury of Metal; coinciding with the Dīng Yǒu month (where Yǒu is Metal's peak), the Sì-Yǒu-Chǒu Metal triad is fully formed — what is called "Sānhé present at the day." The San Ming Tong Hui states: "The Three Harmony means each of the five elements attains one of birth, prosperity and storage; like a human person having soul, spirit and body, the three unite to form the whole," speaking to its complete power and flowing qi. When selecting an auspicious day, a Sānhé day governs smooth outcomes and easily achieved goals, especially favoring matters requiring "combined effort" — partnerships, marriages, home building and burials. Today, though Gōuchén of the Black Path would normally make this an inauspicious day, the combined presence of Sānhé and other auspicious spirits transforms ill fortune into good, with all twenty-four matters favorable — a vivid demonstration of the power of the Three Harmony.

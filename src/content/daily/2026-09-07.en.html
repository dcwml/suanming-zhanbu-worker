Metaphysics Insight

White Dew (Bái Lù 白露) is the fifteenth of the twenty-four solar terms and the third of autumn — and today is the very day it arrives. As the ancient text Yueling Qishi'er Hou Ji Jie states: "In the eighth month festival... yin qi grows heavier, and dew condenses into white." After White Dew, the sun's direct rays shift southward, bringing greater temperature differences between day and night in the Northern Hemisphere, with morning moisture forming white droplets on plants. In Five Elements theory, White Dew marks the transition to Geng Metal's dominance: Metal peaks, Wood is restrained, Fire declines, and Water begins to regenerate. Those with weak Wood or Fire in their birth charts should conserve energy and keep a low profile after this term, while those with strong Metal and Water elements see their fortunes open up and may seize the momentum. Folk wisdom says "After White Dew, bare no more skin," reminding people to dress warmly — a practice aligned with Chinese medicine's principle of nurturing yin through autumn and winter.

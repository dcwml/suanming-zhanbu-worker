On a Guǐ Mǎo day, Guǐ is yin water — the water of dew and gentle rain; Mǎo is yin wood, the wood of flowers and fruiting trees — Guǐ water seated on Mǎo, water giving birth to wood: fine rain nourishing blossom, a look of generation held inward. Where yesterday's Rén Yín was the great river and the great tree, today is the light rain and the flowering branch — yang yielded to yin, and the day's force drawn finer. The Na Yin is Goldfoil Metal 金箔金 again — Rén Yín and Guǐ Mǎo share it: metal at its thinnest, laid as foil upon the face of vessels, its luster outwardly shown; the account was given yesterday, and today that foil-bound luster stays one more day, painted with an even finer brush. The day god arrives at Xuánwǔ 玄武, the Black Tortoise, one of the Black Path baleful ones: the Black Tortoise is the water god of the north, governing thieves, shadow and deceit — matters done in the dark. Yet today the favorable list runs to twenty-two matters, the fullest of late — marriage, proposing, betrothal pledges, offerings, prayers, taking in wealth, trade, contracts, relocation, setting the bed: nearly every matter of gladness stands favorable, and the secret again lies in the auspicious spirits. Seven stand in rank, and the first is the Three Harmonies 三合 — today's Mǎo day and the Hài month branch form the Hài–Mǎo half-union, two of the three Hài–Mǎo–Wèi Wood frame: the Three Harmonies govern accord that becomes a frame, and a frame binds faster than a handshake — the Six are a clasping of hands, the Three a sworn alliance — hence trade, contracts, marriage and betrothal all stand favorable. "Bùjiāng" 不将 and Wǔhé 五合 attend — yin and yáng unopposed, accord in all affairs — hence marriage, proposing, betrothal pledges and setting the bed stand together in one column: the most complete wedding list of recent days. Yīndé 阴德, the Hidden Virtue, governs the merit of quiet devotion — hence offerings, fasting rites, prayers, consecrating icons and installing incense stand favorable; Mínrì 民日, the People's Day, governs the common affairs of the folk — hence travel, adding household members and relocation stand favorable; Míngfèiduì 鸣吠对 marks the dog's answering bark and the open road, and is likewise the burial spirit — hence burial, removing and completing mourning attire stand favorable; Shíyīn 时阴 attends — the aid of the yin hour, fitting this day of yin water on yin wood. Only two baleful ones stand in list, and the avoided column holds only four matters, the briefest of late: Sǐqì 死气, the Death's Breath, governs the withering air — the qi of growth runs short, hence planting stands avoided: what is set in the ground will not take. Yuánwǔ 元武, the Black Tortoise, governs the unclear — hence lawsuits stand avoided, where tongues are hard to untangle and deceits easy to suffer; acquiring property stands avoided, where the deed may hide a dark loss; digging a well stands avoided, where hidden veins below must not be lightly stirred: all four born of the words "shadow" and "withering." Clash the Rooster, the Sha lies West: Mǎo and Yǒu stand opposed — with Rooster people today, guard against contention above all and go slow on partnership; western journeys take a slower step. The Black Tortoise holds the day, yet the Three Harmonies command the season; today's measure is this: what is done in the open hall may join and conclude — what is done in the shadow keeps away.

Zodiac Fortune · Rabbit 兔

Today's earthly branch is Mǎo, which corresponds to the Rabbit — making the Rabbit the protagonist of the day: it is the Rabbit's natal day, with the Mǎo hours at dawn on its watch; better still, today's Mǎo day forms the half-union with the Hài month branch, two of the Hài–Mǎo–Wèi Wood frame — the Rabbit's natal day is again one "joined with the month's command," the lucky qi unbroken. The Three Harmonies attend, with "Bùjiāng" and the Five Unions beside: in love, this is the Rabbit's great day of late — marriage, proposing and betrothal pledges all stand favorable, and with "Bùjiāng" holding the day — yin and yáng unopposed — registration, engagement, a formal proposal and setting the bed may all go forward with a free hand; for the unattached, the Three Harmonies govern introductions that become a frame: a friend's matchmaking or an elder's referral works best today, and the one you meet may enter the circle — not a passing romance, but the kind who meets the family. In career, the Three Harmonies govern partnership that binds: trade, contracts and taking in wealth stand favorable — sign, join ventures, form teams, settle terms; an agreement fixed today holds fast. Travel stands favorable, and business trips and visits all go smoothly. The Wealth God sits South, so the southern quarter favors talking money; the Joy God sits Southeast, so take guests and old friends to the southeastern side. The Black Tortoise in the list offers one caution: today favors open frames, not shadowed dealings — whatever rests on a spoken word should go onto the page, and "inside tips" go unheard; acquiring property stands avoided, so hold off on deeds and deposits. Relocation and setting the bed stand favorable: moving house, changing the bed, arranging a new place — all in season; breaking ground and repairs stand favorable, and small works may go ahead. In the body, Guǐ water answers to the kidneys and bladder, Mǎo wood to the liver and gallbladder — after Minor Snow the water runs cold: lie early to nourish the liver, and drink warm water to guard the kidneys. The Mǎo hours, five to seven in the morning, are the Rabbit's own watch: a cup of warm water at rising and a slow stretch of the limbs takes the day's qi as it comes. The avoided list's planting and well-digging hardly touch the Rabbit — save one: lawsuits and quarrels. Today keep clear of disputes and the courtroom; even a just cause is hard to argue clear on a shadowed day — one step back is the better part. Clash the Rooster, Sha West: Mǎo and Yǒu stand opposed — with Rooster people, guard against partnership and contention, and western journeys take a slower step.

Metaphysics Insight

Yesterday spoke of the Six Harmonies; today speaks of the Three Harmonies 三合 — the twelve branches' other system of alliance. Where the Six join two by two, the Three join three by three: Hài, Mǎo and Wèi combine into the Wood frame; Yín, Wǔ and Xù into the Fire frame; Sì, Yǒu and Chǒu into the Metal frame; Shēn, Zǐ and Chén into the Water frame — four frames covering all twelve branches, each holding three branches of birth, flourishing and the tomb, after the life-path of an element's qi. Take wood: wood is born at Hài — the qi at its first breath, like a child in arms; it flourishes at Mǎo — the qi at its fullest, like one in the prime of life; and it is laid to rest at Wèi — the qi gathered home, like one buried in peace. The three stations Hài–Mǎo–Wèi string one whole life of wood's qi into a single line, and so the Three Harmonies bind deeper than the Six: the Six are a handshake, the Three a blood oath — a frame once formed does not scatter. In the discipline of choosing days, the Three Harmonies govern matters of "framing": partnership, marriage alliance, societies and contracts — whatever binds for the long term does best on a Three Harmonies day, taking its firmness from the frame. Today, Mǎo day in a Hài month, is a half-frame — two branches only, yet holding the greater part of the Wood frame's air; hence the almanac ranks the Three Harmonies first among today's auspicious spirits, and trade, contracts, marriage and betrothal all draw their strength from it. The old books of fate, weighing matches, looked to the Three Harmonies beside the Six: Tiger, Horse and Dog of one hearth (Yín–Wǔ–Xù); Pig, Rabbit and Goat of one frame (Hài–Mǎo–Wèi) — the folk phrase "three-harmony marriage" 三合婚 names exactly the three signs of one frame: temperaments attuned, mutual aid in every undertaking, a match the old registers also called of the first rank. Sweeter still is the contrast the Three Harmonies make with today's baleful spirit: the Black Tortoise governs shadow, the Three Harmonies govern the open — shadow is one person hidden in a room; the open is three people gathered in a crowd. Hence today's line between favorable and avoided is unusually clean: matters of entering a frame — signing, pledging, joining — all favorable; matters of the dark — lawsuits, the hidden flaw in a deed, the stirrings beneath a well — all avoided. The subtlety of the Three Harmonies lies in the one word "frame": no single tree makes a forest, and no single branch makes a frame — on a day of frames, enter one, and help the work of many to its end.

The first day after yesterday's Frost's Descent: the air stern and congealing, deep autumn opening the gate of winter's store. Xīn metal sits upon Wèi earth: Xīn is the pearl-and-jade metal, gentle and reserved; Wèi is dry earth, and with the Xū month's earth in command, thick soil births metal and the day's lord stands on solid ground. Rarest of all, the Union with the Heaven's Virtue and the Union with the Moon's Virtue arrive on the same day — in the Xū month the Heaven's Virtue falls on Bǐng, and so does the Moon's Virtue, and Bǐng unites with Xīn: today's day stem Xīn is precisely the union-partner of both virtues, as when a noble lord, unable to attend in person, has already sent his envoy ahead. The Mother's Granary favors worship and gathering-in, with Bùjiàng and the Jade Eaves lending support. Yet the Vermilion Bird of the Black Path holds the day, star of quarrels and disputes on duty, with Hékuí, the Month's Punishment, the Five Emptinesses and the Earth's Pouch standing about: the Five Emptinesses leave the wealth qi drifting and ungathered, hence business openings are avoided while collecting wealth alone is favored — money may be received in, not spent out on new ventures; the Earth's Pouch seals the earth's breath, so breaking ground, opening ponds and building privies are all pressed down; with the Month's Punishment and the Vermilion Bird together, grand weddings too are set aside. Below, the branches pair union with punishment — Wǔ and Wèi joined in six-harmony, Wèi clamped by the Xū month in the triple punishment — union and punishment coexisting, mirroring the spirits' mixed ledger: today favors calm and gathering-in — worship, meeting friends, relocation, receiving wealth, capping and grooming to set one's appearance right — while grand openings and grand celebrations are better left for another day.

In wealth, keep rather than venture: collecting wealth stands favored while opening a business stands avoided, the Five Emptinesses on duty leaving wealth qi thin and drifting — chasing in accounts receivable and long-owed sums is well timed today, while investment, expansion and grand openings are not; the Wealth God sits Due East — take the east's advantage in negotiations and collections. Relocation and meeting friends and kin both stand favored: moving into a new home, calling on old friends, gatherings and reunions all proceed smoothly under the two virtues' guard. Only the Vermilion Bird and the Month's Punishment share the watch, and quarrels rise easiest of all — hold back three parts in speech, read documents and contracts twice before signing, and quarrel not over idle airs. The day clashes with the Ox and the Sha lies West: keep clear of contention with Ox people and entrust them no heavy charge, and let journeys or moves toward the West wait. After Frost's Descent the autumn air grows sterner still, and Goat people today are like warm jade in its case — dim the gleam, gather in the light, and make wealth by way of harmony.

Metaphysics Insight

Today is the fifteenth of the ninth lunar month — the night of the full moon — a fitting evening to speak of a pair of top-grade auspicious spirits in the day's ledger: the Union with the Heaven's Virtue and the Union with the Moon's Virtue. The almanac matches virtue-spirits to the month: the Heaven's Virtue, the grace of heaven; the Moon's Virtue, the kindness of the moon — each fixed by its own rhyme. The Heaven's Virtue rhyme goes "in the ninth, Bǐng; in the tenth, Yǐ" — so in the ninth month, the Xū month, the Heaven's Virtue falls on Bǐng; the Moon's Virtue is taken by triple-harmony: "in Yín, Wǔ and Xū months, on Bǐng" — so the Moon's Virtue in the Xū month also falls on Bǐng. The virtues themselves are already auspicious of the first order, and Bǐng unites with Xīn — whenever the day's stem meets their union-partner, the day is said to bear "union with the virtue": today, Xīn Wèi, whose stem Xīn is precisely the union-partner of the Xū month's Bǐng virtue, both unions arrive from a single source — a double-virtue day, rare stock in a date-selector's eyes. What does "union" mean? The ancients' figure is charming: the virtue-spirits are like noble officials who cannot attend in person every day; when the union-partner arrives, it is as if the lord has sent his envoy ahead with word — the virtue's force is somewhat lessened, yet the blessing remains, and above all it can dissolve the crowd of lesser maligns: the Canon of Times and Taboos, Examined and Harmonized, says that where the virtue-unions stand, "the many maligns submit of themselves." Why then are marriage rites and business openings still avoided today? Because the auspicious spirits and the Black Path keep separate ledgers: the virtue-unions govern "whence the blessing comes," while the Black Path's Vermilion Bird governs "what manner of day this is" — on a day pressed under the Black Path, the ceremonies and courtesies of grand celebrations are prone to wrangles and setbacks, and date-selection must weigh the two together: spirits strong enough, the day itself unoffending — only then is the whole auspicious. Herein lies the almanac's subtlety: fortune and misfortune are never a single label, but a chessboard of spirits checking and balancing one another. Today's board: the double virtues holding the base, the Vermilion Bird setting the limit — favor gathering-in, decline grand ventures, and each fits the position it holds.

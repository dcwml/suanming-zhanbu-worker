Jǐ Mǎo follows Wù Yín: yesterday the yang stem of earth opened the City Wall Earth couplet, today the yin stem closes it — Wù and Jǐ are earth both, commanding in the earth-ripened Xū month, shoulder to shoulder, two days of earth unbroken. Brightest of all is the meeting of day and month: the day's Mǎo and the month's Xū stand in Six Harmony, and the auspicious spirit Six Harmony keeps the roster true to its name; Mǎo and Xū moreover combine into fire, with the year's Wǔ flame for the lighting — fire and earth begetting one another, the whole frame in harmony. Gōuchén, the Hook Array, keeps the Black Path — and with it the six of the Black Path (the Heavenly Punishment, the Vermilion Bird, the White Tiger, the Heavenly Prison, the Black Tortoise, the Hook Array) have all taken the stage, the closing seat falling on the day when the auspicious spirits stand thickest: the Heavenly Grace, Six Harmony, Sacred Heart and Five Unions hold the watch fourfold, and the six inauspicious names, small spirits mostly, cannot press them down. Set against yesterday it reads best of all: yesterday the Yellow Path's Fate Master yet wrote "all other matters set aside" under the Month Loathing's watch; today the Black Path's Hook Array opens nearly everything — the line between yellow and black lies not in the name, but in the pairing of spirits. The favored list opens wide at twenty-two entries, the whole suite of marriage among them: weddings, betrothal gifts, betrothal pledges, adopting a son-in-law, with moving residence, entering a new home, setting the bed and burial all favored — Six Harmony joins what is sundered, the Sacred Heart delights in ceremony, the Heavenly Grace grants remission; and Xiánchí, the Pool of Sweetness, carries a study of its own: for the Yín-Wǔ-Xū years the Pool sits at Mǎo, so the day branch is the Peach Blossom of the year's Wǔ — peach blossom meeting Six Harmony, romance closes its bonds most easily, though the Pool's first meaning warns of wine and excess; let the wedding cups be measured. The Little Spender counts small leaks — keep the gifts of the season within means. The four avoided entries all turn on moving earth: opening a business, breaking ground, digging wells, fitting coffin wood — on a City Wall Earth day the earth is raised for ramparts, not dug for wells; and the Five Voids mark emptiness — an unfilled granary does not open for trade. Clash the Rooster, the Sha lies West: Mǎo and Yǒu stand opposed — keep clear of contention with Rooster people, and let western journeys take a slower step.

Today's earthly branch is Mǎo, which corresponds to the Rabbit — making the Rabbit the protagonist of the day, and Rabbit people keep a natal day that the month itself comes to join: the month's Xū stands in Six Harmony with the day's Mǎo, and the spirit of that harmony holds the watch. A natal day met by union is the roundest of the twelve — patrons lend their arms, affinities close their circles. The favored list opens for Rabbit people nearly in full: marriages, betrothal gifts, betrothal pledges, adopting a son-in-law — the single Rabbit may confess, meet and propose today, for the Peach Blossom of the Pool of Sweetness shines on the day branch and affinities settle easiest; the paired may pledge and wed — a good hour not to be let slip. Moving residence, entering a new home, setting the bed, laying foundations and raising pillars are all favored — relocate, furnish, break ground with an easy mind. Worship, prayers, consecration and installing incense — prayers for elders' health made today carry weight. Two cautions: the Little Spender counts small leaks — keep seasonal giving within means and buy no bargains that buy trouble; the Great Hour and Great Ruin stand in the same column — no grand investments, no signing of large contracts today: the work of getting wealth folds in, the work of joining people opens wide. The Hook Array tangles what is left vague: though affairs run smooth, read every clause of every document before the brush falls — on a Hook Array day, ambiguity is the one enemy. Clash the Rooster, the Sha lies West: keep clear of contention with Rooster people, enter no partnership with them today, and let western journeys take a slower step. Yin Earth answers the stomach and Mǎo wood the liver — deep autumn fires the liver quick to temper; keep it gentle, eat regularly, sleep early — on a day of union, harmony itself is the nourishment.

Metaphysics Insight

Yesterday the Fate Master closed the Yellow Path's six; today the Hook Array closes the Black Path's — with Gōuchén 勾陈, all six of the Black Path (the Heavenly Punishment, the Vermilion Bird, the White Tiger, the Heavenly Prison, the Black Tortoise, the Hook Array) have taken the stage, the twelve have walked a full round, and tomorrow the Azure Dragon sets its seal again. The one who arrives last is the one born highest: the Hook Array began as a star official — the Book of Jin's Treatise on Astronomy sets six stars of Gōuchén in the Purple Forbidden Enclosure, four of them curved like a hook and arrayed in linked ranks, hence the name: gōu the hook, chén the array — six stars linked in a ring about the pole star itself. Daoist tradition raised the star to the Emperor of the Hook Array, the Exalted Celestial Sovereign — second of the Four Sovereigns, who assists the Jade Emperor in holding the two poles of south and north and the three powers of heaven, earth and man, marshals the host of stars, and keeps the wars of the human world. The Purple Forbidden Enclosure is the palace of heaven, and the Hook Array is the marshal of its guard. Yet when this sovereign steps down onto the almanac's stage of spirits, he keeps the most mundane of ledgers: fields, entanglements, delays — among the six spirits of divination the Hook Array means land disputes, snarled affairs and things that slow without resolving; the character gōu is a curved hook, the thing that catches and will not let go, so on a Hook Array day one signs nothing vague and enters no tangles. The curious part is that today's remedy stands written in the auspicious column: the Hook Array "hooks," and Six Harmony "joins" — one draws matters into tangles, the other draws parties into accord; hook and harmony share the day, and under four auspicious spirits the Hook Array's tangle turns into contracts read clause by clause and betrothals of two families closed round and whole. Yellow Path and Black Path are, in the end, only the names of shifts on a roster: yesterday the Yellow Path's Fate Master met the Month Loathing and everything folded in; today the Black Path's Hook Array meets Six Harmony and nearly everything opens. Fortune never sat in the name — it sits in the pairing; and that is the lesson a thousand years of almanacs have been spelling out.

Today is Rén Xū 壬戌 — the stem Rén is yang water, the water of rivers, lakes and seas, vast and all-embracing; the branch Xū is the storehouse of fire, standing firm as a dike. Xū earth does restrain Rén water, yet it is precisely what gives the great waters their banks, and the Nayin, Great Sea Water, adds further depth and breadth; with the month still in Shēn metal and metal begetting water, the water veins run unbroken — a day whose qi favors unblocking, connecting and setting things in motion. The presiding deity Sīmìng 司命 is an auspicious spirit of the Yellow Path, and with Yuèdé 月德, Yuè'ēn 月恩, Shǒurì 守日, Fúdé 福德, Yángdé 阳德 and Mǔcāng 母仓 arriving alongside, the favorable list is remarkably broad: sending betrothal gifts, sealing covenants, moving into a house, relocating, construction, raising beams, setting beds, planting, acquiring livestock, installing machinery and setting up looms are all favorable, and consecration, travel, removal, installing incense, moving household shrines, dismantling and meeting relatives and friends are no less so. Yet the inauspicious spirits Yànduì 厌对, Zhāoyáo 招摇, Tiāngǒu 天狗 and Jiǔkōng 九空 stand in the way — Yànduì bodes ill for unions, Jiǔkōng for amassing wealth and property, Tiāngǒu for hunting and matters concerning dogs, and Zhāoyáo for ostentatious displays — so installing a stove, breaking ground, breaking ground for graves, burial, praying for blessings, conducting funerals, felling trees and giving thanks to the earth are all placed on the unfavorable list. In sum, the qi of this day favors unblocking and connecting, moving into a new home and sealing agreements, but not large purchases of property; ground-breaking and burial should wait, and while betrothal and covenants are welcome today, a formal wedding may fare better on another chosen day.

Zodiac Fortune · Dog

Today's branch is Xū (戌), which corresponds to the Dog — today's featured zodiac. Those born in a Dog year sit squarely on the day's own branch, with Sīmìng 司命 of the Yellow Path presiding and auspicious spirits Yuèdé 月德, Yuè'ēn 月恩, Shǒurì 守日 and Fúdé 福德 attending alongside — overall fortune is highly auspicious and open: engagement (sending betrothal gifts, sealing covenants), moving into a new home (moving in, relocating), renovating the house (construction, raising beams, setting beds), travel, planting, acquiring livestock and meeting relatives and friends may all be set in motion; both the Joy God and the Wealth God stand in the due South and the Fortune God in the Southeast, so seek fortune, conduct business and head out primarily toward the south. Mind three cautions: first, today clashes with the Dragon and the Sha lies North — leave room in dealings with those born in a Dragon year, and postpone journeys northward; second, the inauspicious spirit Tiāngǒu 天狗 presides, and the Heavenly Dog bodes ill for dogs — Dogs would do well today to beware of bites and scratches from pets and livestock, and to avoid hunting and the like; third, Jiǔkōng 九空 bodes ill for amassing wealth and property — today favors planning and starting things, not large purchases, heavy investments or stockpiling goods. In health, Xū is dry earth meeting autumn dryness — Dogs may feel their spleen, stomach and skin run out of balance; eat light and moistening foods, drink plenty of water, and retire early to nourish your qi.

Folklore

Today's presiding deity is Sīmìng 司命 — a name of very ancient lineage. The "Nine Songs" of the Chuci (楚辞) devote two chapters to it, Dà Sīmìng 大司命 and Shǎo Sīmìng 少司命: the ancients revered Sīmìng as a celestial spirit holding authority over human lifespans, birth and death — Dà Sīmìng commanding the span of life and death, Shǎo Sīmìng blessing the union of man and woman and the gift of children. Later date-selection brought Sīmìng into the company of the six auspicious spirits of the Yellow Path — Qīnglóng 青龙, Míngtáng 明堂, Jīnkuì 金匮, Tiāndé 天德, Yùtáng 玉堂 and Sīmìng 司命 — who take turns presiding over the days in rotation with the spirits of the Black Path; a day on which Sīmìng presides is called a Sīmìng Yellow Path day, its qi clear and luminous, most suited to beginning new undertakings, sealing covenants, welcoming what is new and setting many matters in motion — which is why Sīmìng days so often carry betrothal, covenants, moving into a house and relocating on their favorable list. Today is precisely such a day, and with Yuèdé and Yuè'ēn attending alongside, the favorable list is broad and the unfavorable one short. Even setting spirits aside, this old habit of choosing a good day to begin what matters is itself a ritual of life: behind the search for an auspicious day lies the wish to treat important things with due solemnity. Seeking fortune and avoiding harm, as the old almanacs teach, rests half in the calendar and half in the heart.

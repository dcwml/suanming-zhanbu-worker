Today is Dīng Sì 丁巳 — the stem Dīng is yin fire, the flame of lamp and candle, fine yet bright; the branch Sì is the birthplace of fire, stem and branch of one kind, the fire qi at full strength. With Bǐng fire standing in both the year and month pillars, though metal qi begins to rule after the Start of Autumn, today's fire remains generous. The day god is Tiāndé 天德 — an auspicious day — with Yuèdé Hé 月德合 attending, while Liùhé 六合 governs harmony and completion, Wǔfù 五富 governs abundance, Yào'ān 要安 governs peace and Bǎoguāng 宝光 governs radiance, so sacrifice, consecration, removal, trading, signing contracts and collecting wealth are all on the favorable list. Yet Jiéshà 劫煞 governs loss, Hékūi 河魁 governs blockage, Dìnáng 地囊 governs enclosure and Chóngrì 重日 governs repetition — so breaking ground, breaking earth, marriage, moving in, moving house and travel are all best postponed. The virtue spirits hold the fierce ones down and turn danger into fortune: today's momentum favors pushing business forward and gathering wealth in, not launching great undertakings.

Zodiac Fortune · Snake

Today's branch is Sì (巳), which corresponds to the Snake — today's featured zodiac. Those born in a Snake year sit squarely on the day's branch and hold the duty, with Tiāndé 天德 presiding, Yuèdé Hé 月德合 attending, and Liùhé 六合 and Wǔfù 五富 beside them — a clear and favorable day. Career-wise, trading, signing contracts and collecting wealth are all on today's favorable list, so Snakes running affairs or striking deals have a good day to negotiate agreements and settle accounts — work long deliberated may be locked in today. For fortune, the Wealth God dwells Southwest, the Joy God due South and the Fortune God Southeast — seek wealth to the southwest, seek peace and blessing to the southeast. But mind three avoidances: first, moving into a house and moving are both unfavorable today, so Snakes with a relocation planned should pick another day; second, travel is unfavorable and the Sha lies East, so long journeys, especially eastbound ones, should be delayed if possible; third, the clash falls on the Pig — avoid direct confrontation with those born in a Pig year, and yielding a step will carry you through. Marriage is also unfavorable today, so Snakes weighing a wedding or engagement should choose a better day. In health, with fire qi at its peak, guard against a rising heart fire and a short temper — eat light, keep calm, and all will be at ease.

Folklore

Today's day god is Tiāndé 天德, and Yuèdé Hé 月德合 stands among the auspicious spirits — both are what almanac masters call the virtue spirits. Yesterday we spoke of Jīnkuì 金匮 as one of the six auspicious spirits of the Yellow Path — Tiāndé belongs to that same company. Dé (virtue) means to obtain, and also to bestow favor: Tiāndé governs auspicious signs and stands among the most revered spirits for dissolving misfortune, while Yuèdé 月德 and Yuèdé Hé govern calm and celebration, nourishing with the seasons. When the two virtue spirits attend one day together, the ancients called it Virtue Harmony — "two virtues assisting the day, and a hundred fierce spirits are dissolved." Look at today's list of inauspicious spirits — Hékūi 河魁, Jiéshà 劫煞, Dìnáng 地囊, Chóngrì 重日 — none few — yet the almanac still judges today auspicious, precisely because the virtue spirits hold authority and keep the fierceness of the rest in check. Here lies the old wisdom in weighing fortune against misfortune: never read a single auspicious or inauspicious spirit alone, but see which spirits dominate the day's whole configuration, whether the auspicious qi can hold its own, and whether the inauspicious qi meets its counterweight. Virtue spirits, of course, do not bestow blessings out of nothing — they excel at dissolving and guiding, not at forcing matters through. So on a Virtue Harmony day, business and gathering wealth prosper, while breaking ground and marriage still deserve a wide berth — auspiciousness has its bounds, and avoidance has its bounds. This is the sequel to yesterday's line: each spirit governs its own domain, applied to affairs to hit the mark.

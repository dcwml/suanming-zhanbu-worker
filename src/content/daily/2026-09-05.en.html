Today is Rén Wǔ 壬午 — the stem Rén is yang water, like the water of great rivers, flowing ceaselessly; the branch Wǔ is yang fire, like the blazing sun, bright and intense. Water restrains fire, so stem and branch counter each other, carrying the imagery of "water and fire at war"; yet today both Yuèdé and Tiāndé attend together — Yuèdé is the virtue of the moon, governing yin, secrecy and inner blessing; Tiāndé is the virtue of the sun, governing yang, openness and public blessing. With both virtues present, yin and yang harmonize, and the water-fire restraint instead becomes a "water and fire bringing success" pattern. Ten auspicious spirits attend together: Yuèdé and Tiāndé turn misfortune into fortune; Yuè'ēn governs heavenly grace; Sìxiàng governs peace through the four seasons; Tiānmǎ governs favorable long journeys; Shíyáng governs the rising of yang qi; Shēngqì governs the growth of all things; Bùjiāng governs harmonious marriage; Yùyǔ governs celestial clarity; and Míngfèi governs livestock prosperity — the auspicious qi is truly abundant, rarely seen in recent days. Twenty-three matters are favored, covering marriage, pledging alliance, opening a market, collecting wealth, construction, travel, breaking ground, opening a grave and more — almost all matters are suitable. Only five are avoided: lighting fires, moving into a house, building a roof, installing a door and burial — Báihǔ governs punishment and bloodshed, Zāishà governs disaster and Tiānhuǒ governs fire calamity, so installing doors, building roofs and moving in are best postponed to avoid provoking fire sha. End of Heat has passed and White Dew approaches; autumn metal grows strong and Rén water is nourished. The harmonizing power of Tiāndé and Yuèdé complements autumn metal's solemn gathering. For marriage, opening a market, construction and travel, borrowing this seasonal momentum and the blessing of both virtues brings smooth success in all matters.

Zodiac Fortune · Horse

Today's branch is Wǔ (午), which corresponds to the Horse 马 — today's featured zodiac. Those born in a Horse year sit squarely on the day's own branch — a Horse meeting a Wǔ day is like a fine steed returning to its stable, stem and branch sharing the same qi, naturally endowed with the auspice of "success upon arrival." Yet Rén Wǔ is a day of water-fire conflict: the stem Rén water restrains the branch Wǔ fire, so Horses today should guard against outward smoothness masking inner strain — outward affairs are mostly auspicious thanks to the many auspicious spirits, yet inner energy may be somewhat depleted. Marriage, pledging alliance, accepting betrothal, opening a market, collecting wealth, construction, breaking ground, travel and meeting friends are favored; with Tiāndé and Yuèdé presiding to turn misfortune into fortune and Tiānmǎ governing favorable long journeys, marriage, signing, wealth-seeking and travel proceed quite smoothly today. Lighting fires, moving in, building roofs, installing doors and burial are avoided — Báihǔ, Zāishà and Tiānhuǒ attend together, so installing doors and moving in may provoke fire sha, while burial and ground-breaking easily attract disaster. The day clashes with the Rat and the Sha lies North: yield a step to those born in a Rat year, and travel northward should be cautious. The Joy God stands in the South, so seeking joy may face south; the Wealth God stands in the South, so business pursuits may face south; the Fortune God stands in the Southeast, so prayers for the household may face southeast. In health, Rén water governs the bladder and kidneys, while Wǔ fire governs the heart and small intestine; water restrains fire yet both virtues resolve it, so Horses should guard against kidney coldness and excessive heart fire, favoring warming the kidneys and clearing the heart while avoiding late nights, nurturing body and mind through the harmonizing virtue of Tiāndé and Yuèdé.

Folklore

Among today's auspicious spirits, Yuèdé and Tiāndé attend together, so today's reading is about the lore and meaning of "Tiāndé and Yuèdé in Union." Tiāndé is the foremost of Yellow Path auspicious spirits, governing the transformation of misfortune into fortune and danger into safety; Yuèdé is the virtue of the moon, governing yin, secrecy and the pardoning of sins and deliverance from calamity. On days when both attend together, it is called "Tiāndé and Yuèdé in Union" — yin and yang harmonize, hardness and softness complement each other, all disasters disperse and all matters are greatly auspicious. The Xieji Bianfang Shu records: "When Tiāndé and Yuèdé shine together, their auspiciousness is boundless." In the almanac's spirit system, Tiāndé belongs to yang, like the sun at midday, governing open and upright blessing; Yuèdé belongs to yin, like moonlight upon the earth, governing hidden and gentle blessing. A day with Tiāndé alone can resolve all misfortune; a month with Yuèdé alone can dissolve half of calamity; when both attend together, all inauspicious spirits are eliminated and auspicious qi is doubled. Today Báihǔ presides, originally governing punishment, bloodshed and disaster, yet with Tiāndé and Yuèdé together, Báihǔ's ferocity is transformed by both virtues into the auspice of "White Tiger Carrying a Letter" — ancient tradition holds that when the white tiger carries a letter, it brings good news and written blessings. Together with Yuè'ēn, Sìxiàng, Tiānmǎ, Shíyáng, Shēngqì, Bùjiāng, Yùyǔ and Míngfèi, the auspicious qi is truly abundant. End of Heat has passed and White Dew approaches; autumn metal grows strong and Rén water is nourished. The harmonizing power of Tiāndé and Yuèdé complements autumn metal's solemn gathering. For marriage, opening a market, construction and travel, borrowing this seasonal momentum and the blessing of both virtues brings doubled results with half the effort.

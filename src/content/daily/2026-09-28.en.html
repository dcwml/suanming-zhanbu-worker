Metaphysics Insight

Heading today's auspicious column is Yuè Déhé — the Month Virtue in harmony. The date-selection masters derive the month's virtue spirit from the month branch's Three Harmony frame: in Yín–Wǔ–Xū months, the fire frame, the virtue rests on Bǐng; in Shēn–Zǐ–Chén months, the water frame, on Rén; in Hài–Mǎo–Wèi months, the wood frame, on Jiǎ; in Sì–Yǒu–Chǒu months, the metal frame, on Gēng — this is the Month Virtue. The stem that joins the Month Virtue's stem gives the Month Virtue in harmony: Bǐng joins Xīn, Rén joins Dīng, Jiǎ joins Jǐ, Gēng joins Yǐ. The Celestial Treasure Almanac says: "The Month Virtue is the virtue spirit of the month; where it presides, all evils dissolve; on the day it holds, a hundred blessings gather." The harmony form carries that virtue in a gentler, more yielding key, presiding over hidden patronage and quiet protection — the Month Virtue stands openly at your back, while the Month Virtue in harmony props you from the shadows. In this Dīng Yǒu month the Month Virtue rests on Gēng, and Yǐ joins Gēng — and today's pillar is precisely Yǐ Sì: the stem Yǐ answers the Month Virtue in harmony, while the branch Sì half-joins the month's Yǒu in the Three Harmony frame, stem and branch both chiming with the month command — hence marriage heading the favored column with business and trade beside it. With the Month Virtue in harmony understood, marriage, betrothal gifts and betrothal agreements standing beside opening a business, trading and signing contracts shows itself no polite stock phrase, but a day of virtue joined, deduced by the concord of stems, branches and month command.

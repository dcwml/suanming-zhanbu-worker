Today is Jǐ Sì 己巳 — the stem Jǐ is yin earth, the soft and moist earth of fields and gardens, yielding and sustaining; the branch Sì is yin fire with fire held within, so fire begets earth, stem and branch generating one another, the earth qi warm and mild. The Nayin is Great Forest Wood, paired in one sound with yesterday's Wù Chén 戊辰 — since yesterday's reading already unfolded the image of the great forest, today one need only press forward with the steadiness of earth and nourish all things with the vitality of wood. The presiding deity Tiāndé 天德 is a Yellow Path god, a virtue-star overhead that turns misfortune into fortune; with it stand Tiānyuàn 天愿, granting wishes fulfilled; Liùhé 六合, presiding over union and achievement; Wǔfù 五富, presiding over wealth and plenty; Yào'ān 要安, presiding over peace and steadiness; and Bǎoguāng 宝光, a protecting radiance — five auspicious spirits assembled, so the favorable list runs to eighteen items, from marriage and sacrifice to setting beds and relocating, from breaking ground, erecting pillars and raising beams to trading and signing contracts, nothing amiss. The seven bans fall mostly on funerals and heavy labor — funeral processions, burial, making beams, felling trees, building bridges and acquiring livestock are all best avoided; and travel, too, stands on the banned side, while among the inauspicious spirits Chóngrì 重日 presides over repetition and going in circles, Hékūi 河魁 over disputes and Jiéshà 劫煞 over robbery and harm — so journeys today should be slow rather than rushed, near rather than far, and litigation and quarrels shunned altogether. Today also marks End of Heat itself: the summer heat ends here, Metal qi gathers strength and the White Dew draws near — from this day on, heaven and earth turn from spreading out to gathering in, and our affairs should follow suit, advancing steadily and storing up.

Zodiac Fortune · Snake

Today's branch is Sì (巳), which corresponds to the Snake — today's featured zodiac. Those born in a Snake year sit squarely on the day's own branch, receiving today's qi in direct accord, and with Tiāndé 天德 of the Yellow Path on duty and five auspicious spirits shining upon them, this is one of those rare days when advancing pays off and whatever one sets out to do gets done. At work, trading, signing contracts and meeting relatives and friends all stand on the favorable list, and Liùhé 六合 presides over union and achievement — negotiating partnerships, sealing agreements, launching new plans or asking someone to broker a match all move with the wind today; those in business will do well to schedule contract signings precisely for this day, for documents and covenants are especially favored. In wealth, Wǔfù 五富 presides over plenty, and both the Wealth God and the Fortune God dwell in the due North — favor the north when seeking money and doing business; the Joy God stands in the Northeast, a fine direction for anything joyous. In love, marriage heads the favorable list — Snakes with a wedding in mind may hold the ceremony or settle the engagement today, and praying for offspring is likewise favored, a good day indeed for couples hoping for a child. Mind, though, that today clashes with the Pig and the Sha lies East: yield a step to those born in a Pig year, keep important matters away from the due east, and since travel stands among the bans, long journeys are better left for another day. In health, today is End of Heat itself — the summer heat retreats and autumn dryness begins to rise; the Snake belongs to fire, answering to the heart, so at this turn of the season sleep early and rise early, keep the mind calm, eat lightly and add a few moistening foods, that the heart's qi be not spent.

Folklore

Today is End of Heat (Chǔshǔ 处暑) itself. The twenty-four solar terms share out the qi of the year among them, and End of Heat stands fourteenth of the twenty-four, the second term of autumn — the word Chǔ (处) means "to stop", and here the summer heat stops at last. The ancients divided End of Heat into three pentads: in the first, hawks begin offering up birds; in the second, heaven and earth begin to turn severe; in the third, the ripened grain is gathered in — falcons start hunting and storing food, a solemn air spreads over the world, and the grain in the fields ripens for harvest. In the calendar, End of Heat is not a point where the month pillar changes — the Shēn month began at Start of Autumn, as told in an earlier reading — yet it is the signal that the heat is truly leaving the stage: Fire retreats, Metal advances, yin qi grows, and from this day on the mornings and evenings begin to carry a coolness. The folk customs of End of Heat mostly bid summer farewell and greet autumn: in the Jiangsu and Zhejiang regions people say "send off a duck on End of Heat", eating duck on this day, for duck is cooling and moistening, nourishing yin and easing dryness, just right for the autumn air; folk also set river lanterns afloat on this day, lamps drifting on the water to honor the departed and pray for peace — one of the important rites of the seventh lunar month. As for solar terms and the almanac, earlier readings have said it: the terms set the month pillars and govern the waxing and waning of the qi; and the particular lesson of End of Heat lies in the word "gather" — the heat spent, all things ripen and are gathered in, and so should our affairs advance steadily and draw their gains together.

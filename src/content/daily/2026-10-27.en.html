Jiǎ wood sits upon Xū earth: with Frost's Descent past and the earth of the Xū month wielding power in deep autumn, Xū is the vault of Jiǎ wood's indirect wealth — yet autumn's wood stands bare of leaf and branch, heavy the earth and light the trunk, a day for rooting deep and gathering strength rather than for unfurling and stirring. The Na Yin is Fire on the Mountaintop — Jiǎ Xū and Yǐ Hài form yet another mated pair in the sexagenary cycle, and today is its first day: yesterday Guǐ Yǒu sheathed the Sword's Edge, today Jiǎ Xū raises the beacon fire, one blaze across two days burning upon the mountaintop — fire in the heavens, lighting what is far and not what is near. The White Tiger holds the watch today, a Black Path day — the White Tiger is the emblem of the west's metal essence, presiding over blades, mourning garments and bloodshed, which is why burial, funeral processions and breaking earth for the grave stand in a full avoided row: its nature answered exactly. Yet within the malign lies its own remedy: the three auspicious spirits each keep their post — the Mother's Granary, the star-lore's rich earth, carrying all things and nourishing the root; the Keeping Day, a day of abiding, favoring what is held over what is scattered; the Post Horse, the spirit of galloping motion, hastening journeys and removals — three spirits in concert, and the tiger's menace slackens: travel and building may both go forward. The earth-spirits in power must be read closely: the Month's Establishing means the day branch Xū meets the month branch Xū, the month's own authority enthroned, the earth's qi at its fullest; the Lesser Hour and the Earthly Bureau are of the earth-spirit kindred — on a day when earth reigns its vanguard is not to be assailed, hence breaking ground, grave-digging, cutting channels, planting and felling trees all stand avoided: the season's earth already commands, and to dig is to wound its breath; though the Mother's Granary presides over planting and nurture, the weight of the Month's Establishing and the Earthly Bureau presses harder — weighed together, planting yields to keeping the soil. On wealth the Keeping Day has its lesson: releasing wealth and opening granaries are avoided — a keeping day gathers rather than scatters, and the treasury stays shut today. Above, Chén clashes Xū — clash the Dragon, the Sha lies North: mind the northern undertakings of earthworks, long journeys and building.

Metaphysics Insight

The Yellow Path's procession paused yesterday — the Azure Dragon, the Bright Hall, the Golden Casket and the Heavenly Virtue having taken four seats, with the Jade Hall and the Commander of Destiny still to come; today the Black Path takes the floor instead: the White Tiger holds the watch. Of the twelve sky-spirits, six ride the Yellow Path and six the Black, like the two wings of an ancient court — the Yellow Path's six presiding over gladness and growth, the Black Path's six over punishment and arms, and the White Tiger is the most famed name on the Black Path's roster. To speak of the White Tiger, first untangle its two identities. The first is written in the sky: the ancients divided the stars along the ecliptic into four great emblems — the Azure Dragon's seven mansions of the east, the White Tiger's seven mansions of the west, the Vermilion Bird's seven mansions of the south and the Dark Warrior's seven mansions of the north — four emblems guarding the four quarters, each keeping a season: the Dragon springs, the Bird summers, the Tiger autumns and the Warrior winters. The western seven — Kuí, Lóu, Wèi, Mǎo, Bì, Zī, Shēn — link into the shape of a tiger, posted on the western frontier and ruling autumn's season of severing metal, hence the White Tiger. Now Frost's Descent is past and Start of Winter not yet come — deep autumn exactly: lift your eyes south after nightfall and the Tiger's seven mansions are riding the sky; and today the day's branch sits on the White Tiger of the Black Path — sky-lore and calendar meeting head-on in one deep-autumn Xū day. The second identity belongs to the calendar: the twelve sky-spirits borrowed the four emblems' names into timekeeping, and the White Tiger entered the Black Path, presiding over blades, mourning garments and bloodshed — in the old almanacs on a White Tiger day marriage went unasked, journeys were weighed twice, and even the magistrates mostly shunned it for sentencing. But a malign spirit is never one-faced: the White Tiger's menace lies in severing, and severing is the very nature of autumn's qi — frost fells the grasses and the ten thousand things gather in; within the cutting lies the storing, and within the storing, life. The ancients did not build the calendar to make men cower at an ill name, but to know each spirit's temper and step around its edge: today the White Tiger presides, so burial and grave-breaking give way — yet with the Mother's Granary thickening life, the Keeping Day holding fast and the Post Horse galloping, three auspicious names stand in the roster and travel and building may proceed as ever. The true art of seeking fortune and shunning harm was never to bar one's door, but to read the weather and choose the road. Tomorrow the Jade Hall arrives and the Yellow Path's civil wing reopens — but that is a tale for the coming day.

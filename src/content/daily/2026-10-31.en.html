The answer heads the inauspicious roster: the Month Loathing holds the watch. In the Xū month the loathing sits at Yín, and today's Yín day is precisely that spirit's seat — the Loathing is the "yin construction," where yin and yang do not meet: on its day worship and offerings, moving residence, marriage and far travel are set apart. Hence the avoided list names worship (favored yesterday, avoided today — the virtues stood down, the loathing took the watch, and the page of sacrifice turns), entering a new home (of the moving kind) and opening for trade (the comings and goings of yang affairs); with Gūchén, the Solitary Star, on the roster, weddings and gatherings keep their distance too. The Loathing's hard qi is not spent on festive yang affairs, yet may be spent on drawing the net: pressing claims, chasing what was set aside, closing accounts, ferreting out what slipped — today suits the work of pulling the net in, not the work of opening wide. Metaphysics Insight

Yesterday the Black Tortoise of the Black Path came hair-loosed to the hall; today the Fate Master keeps the Yellow Path — of the Yellow Path's six (the Azure Dragon, the Bright Hall, the Golden Vault, the Heavenly Virtue, the Jade Hall and Sīmìng 司命), the Heavenly Virtue's Treasured Light and the Jade Hall have had their tellings of late, and today comes the one whose folk identity is the most down-to-earth of them all: Sīmìng — the Stove God. Sīmìng's credentials are very old. The Nine Songs of the Chuci carry whole hymns to the Grand Master of Fate and the Young Master of Fate: in Qu Yuan's Chu, the god who holds the span of human life — "the lot of human fate is fixed; what parting and meeting can be arranged?" — seated high in the clouds, awesome beyond reach. After the Han, that heaven-seated god descended step by step into the kitchens of the people: the Book of the Later Han tells how Yin Zifang, at his morning fire on the La day, met the Stove God face to face and rose to great wealth, and the cult of the stove flourished on his tale; the Daoist canon titled him "the Fate Master of the Eastern Kitchen," and in the south of China folk speech still calls the Stove God "Lord Siming" to this day. From the Grand Master holding the spans of all lives to the kitchen lord holding one household's fortunes, the office moved from the heavens to the hearth — but the work stayed the same: recording good and ill, reporting on schedule, and fixing reward and penalty by the words brought up. The Stove God's most famous duty is the year-end report: the In Search of the Supernatural of the Jin says he "ascends to heaven on the last day of the moon to tell the sins of the household" — later settled into the Stove Festival of the twenty-third and twenty-fourth of the twelfth month, with malt candy to seal the lips and the famous couplet: "Speak good things when you ascend; keep peace for us when you return." The day-choosers took Sīmìng up onto the Yellow Path alongside the Azure Dragon and the Jade Hall — yet with the Yellow Path on watch today and the Month Loathing holding power, matters fold in, and one work of the Stove God's own remains open to all: go home, light the stove, eat well. Curiously, today's Na Yin is City Wall Earth: the wall's earth shields a city, the stove's fire shields a home — one without, one within, a matched pair of guardians. Deep autumn runs out and Start of Winter waits a week off: the colder the sky, the more precious the household fire.

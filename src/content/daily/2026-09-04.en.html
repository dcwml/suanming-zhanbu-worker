Folklore

Today's presiding deity is Tiāndé, so today's reading is about the lore and meaning of Heavenly Virtue. Tiāndé is one of the twelve heavenly deities of the almanac, belonging to the Yellow Path of auspicious spirits, and stands as the foremost of all auspicious spirits, governing the transformation of misfortune into fortune, danger into safety, and the pardoning of sins and deliverance from calamity. The name Tiāndé originates in ancient astronomy and ritual: the ancients believed that the Way of Heaven possessed virtue, moving ceaselessly, and on days when the star of Tiāndé shone, heaven sent down virtue and all disasters dispersed. The Xieji Bianfang Shu records: "Tiāndé is the virtue and blessing of heaven; in whatever direction it governs, construction, marriage, taking office and seeking wealth are all greatly auspicious." In the almanac's spirit system, on any day when Tiāndé presides, no matter how fierce the inauspicious spirits, all can be resolved — hence the saying "Tiāndé can resolve all misfortune." Tiāndé stands in relation to Yuèdé: Tiāndé is the virtue of the sun, governing yang, openness and public blessing; Yuèdé is the virtue of the moon, governing yin, secrecy and inner blessing. When Tiāndé and Yuèdé attend together, it is called "Tiāndé and Yuèdé in Union," greatly auspicious for all matters, with hidden noble help and no calamity daring to approach. Today Tiāndé presides, and Tiān'ēn, Liùhé, Wǔfù, Yào'ān and Bǎoguāng attend together — the auspicious qi is truly abundant. More than ten days have passed since End of Heat, White Dew approaches, autumn metal grows strong and Xīn metal is in season; Tiāndé's upright yang qi complements autumn metal's solemn gathering.

Folklore

Today's presiding deity is Jīnkuì, so today's reading is about the lore and meaning of the Golden Coffer. Jīnkuì is one of the twelve heavenly deities of the almanac, belonging to the Yellow Path of auspicious spirits, governing collection, preciousness, documents and treasuries. The name Jīnkuì originates in the copper cabinets used by ancient emperors to store important documents, edicts and treasures — adorned with gold and fashioned as a coffer, taking the meaning of "golden resonance and jade vibration, the coffer that holds the world." Historical records note that the Han palace maintained a "golden coffer and stone chamber" to store national maps, secret archives and precious treasures, and later generations used Jīnkuì as a metaphor for a place of safekeeping. In the almanac's spirit system, on any day when Jīnkuì presides, collection, signing contracts, trading, moving into a house and marriage are favored — anything related to treasury, contracts and peaceful dwelling receives its protection. Jīnkuì stands in opposition to Qīnglóng: Qīnglóng is the wood of the east, governing growth, creation and advancement; Jīnkuì is the metal of the west, governing collection, preservation and stability. Wood generates fire, fire generates earth, earth generates metal — the growth of Qīnglóng ultimately returns to the collection of Jīnkuì, one opening and one closing, the very principle of heaven and earth's cycle. The ancients said: "On a Jīnkuì day, store rather than scatter, defend rather than attack." Today twenty matters are favored, including marriage, moving into a house, opening a market, trading and signing contracts, perfectly matching Jīnkuì's imagery of "collection and stability"; while funerals and visiting the sick are avoided, using the inauspiciousness of Yuèyàn and Dìhuǒ to escape yin gloom. After End of Heat and before White Dew, autumn metal grows strong and Jīnkuì's metal qi flourishes in season; anything seeking wealth, contracts or peaceful dwelling can borrow this seasonal momentum for doubled results.

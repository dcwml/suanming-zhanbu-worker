On a Xīn Hài day, Xīn metal sits upon Hài water — metal and water mirror each other in clear purity; the Na Yin is Metal of the Hairpin and Bracelet, fine metal quenched in autumn water, lustrous and reserved. The Travel Horse presides over movement, with Heavenly Grace, the Fortune Virtue and the Spirit of Peace lending support — hence travel, relocation, kindling the stove fire and raising roof beams all stand favored, and opening a business, trading and signing contracts may proceed with the momentum. Yet the Dark Warrior holds the Black Path and the tone of the day runs dim: the Four Exhaustions and Five Emptinesses drain wealth thin, the Great Sha threatens harm, and the Repeating Day and Heavy Day warn that ill deeds may redouble — so weddings, burial, groundbreaking and bridge building are all set aside today.

Metaphysics Insight

The most travel-friendly name in today's auspicious column is the Travel Horse (Yì Mǎ) — the spirit of movement, transfer and change. Its derivation runs on the clash position of each trine: the trine Shēn-Zǐ-Chén stations its horse at Yín, Yín-Wǔ-Xū at Shēn, Sì-Yǒu-Chǒu at Hài, and Hài-Mǎo-Wèi at Sì. This month is built on Yǒu, which belongs to the Sì-Yǒu-Chǒu metal trine, so the horse is stabled at Hài — and today, a Xīn Hài day, is precisely a day the Travel Horse holds. The ancients named it after the post-station horses that carried couriers and merchants along the roads; it governs journeys, relocations, new postings and changes of every kind, and travelers of old — examination candidates and merchants alike — would deliberately choose a Travel Horse day to set out, that their movement might "achieve merit through motion". Interestingly, today's inauspicious column holds the Four Exhaustions and the Five Emptinesses — the horse's very opposites, spirits of depletion that counsel stillness against scattering. One urges you to move, the others warn you not to leak; so the almanac charts a middle course: travel, opening a business and trading may all proceed, but whatever wealth comes in should be gathered in promptly rather than spread thin. Such fine calibration between motion and restraint is where the old almanac shows its subtlety.

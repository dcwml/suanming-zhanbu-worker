On Rén Chén day, the day stem Rén (Water) is the water of great rivers, sitting upon Chén (Earth) — and Chén is the storage vault of Water, so Rén takes root in its own vault, its momentum deep and grounded. The month pillar Dīng Yǒu arrives with Yǒu Metal commanding the season after White Dew, generating Water, while Dīng Fire, the direct-wealth star, stands revealed on the month stem, giving wealth its source. Today's rarest quality lies in the Six Harmonies between the day branch Chén and the month branch Yǒu — heaven and earth in accord, their qi intermingling — hence the auspicious spirit Liùhé presides, governing harmonious unions and successful completion. Bùjiàng, the foremost auspicious spirit of marriage, also attends; Mǔcāng governs nurturing and planting, Sìxiàng governs construction and launching work, and Jìng'ān governs tranquil sacrifice. With five auspicious spirits gathered, the "Yi" column is exceptionally abundant — marriage, betrothal gifts, engagement pledges, moving into a house, relocation, setting the bed, renovation, groundbreaking, planting and acquiring livestock are all favorable, and breaking ground, burial and encoffining also receive their support. Yet Tiānxíng of the Black Path presides, governing punishments and disputes, with Yuèshà, Yuèxū and Sìjī arriving together; hence the taboo on opening market, digging wells, cutting canals, building bridges and building boats — all undertakings of founding commerce or cutting into earth and water. On a day of abundant Water, stirring waterworks further invites the peril of overflowing, which the almanac specially avoids. Overall, today offers abundant harmony but no commerce: forming a marriage union, relocating and settling a home are highly auspicious, while opening for business and major transactions should await a better day.

Today's earthly branch is Chén, which corresponds to the Dragon — making the Dragon the zodiac protagonist of the day. Those born in the Year of the Dragon preside over the day, and Chén forms the Six Harmonies with the month branch Yǒu, the qi of heaven and earth aligning with their natal branch — a rare day of harmony bringing noble person support and smooth relations. Matters of union — marriage pledges, partnership negotiations, relocation and settling a home — are all favorable to advance; with both the Joy God and Wealth God in Due South, heading south to talk business or seek wealth yields twice the result for half the effort. Yet Tiānxíng of the Black Path also presides, governing punishments and disputes: Dragons should watch their words and deeds today, read every clause before signing documents, and never ink a contract hastily out of personal favor, lest disputes follow. With Mǔcāng presiding, tending to the family's daily meals and one's own recuperation is also favored. The "Ji" column leads with opening market: merchants should avoid grand openings today, as well as large transactions and major business decisions — hold steady instead. Metaphysics Insight

Liùhé (Six Harmonies) is the gentlest set of relationships among the twelve earthly branches: Zǐ pairs with Chǒu, Yín with Hài, Mǎo with Xū, Chén with Yǒu, Sì with Shēn, and Wǔ with Wèi — two by two, like the mutual embrace of yin and yang, hence the name. Unlike the head-on collision of a clash, the harmonies describe attraction and fusion of qi. Today, with Chén as the day branch and Yǒu as the month branch, Chén and Yǒu stand in exact harmony; the classics say Chén-Yǒu combines and transforms into Metal — Earth bears Metal, Metal generates Water (today's Rén Water), one link interlocking with the next, the qi channels flowing freely. Hence the almanac honors Liùhé as an auspicious spirit governing harmonious union and successful completion, lending its power to marriage, alliances, contracts and moving house. Bùjiàng, listed alongside it, is an auspicious spirit dedicated to wedding dates, arising from the doctrine of "neither yin nor yang jiāng" — the old geomancers matched the yin-yang nature of the chosen day against the month branch, holding that "the yang jiāng harms the groom, the yin jiāng harms the bride, both together harm the couple; bùjiàng alone brings fortune to both." Whenever Bùjiàng presided, almanacs unfailingly marked the day favorable for marriage. Today Liùhé and Bùjiàng arrive together, which is why the "Yi" column is led by marriage, betrothal gifts and engagement pledges — a rare day in the year for forming a happy union. The ancients said "in harmony, both sides profit"; in the art of date selection, those words took concrete form as a day when Chén and Yǒu embrace.

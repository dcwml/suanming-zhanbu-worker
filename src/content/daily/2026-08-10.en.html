Today is Bǐng Chén 丙辰 — the stem Bǐng is the fire of the sun, the branch Chén is earth and also the storehouse of water; fire generates earth, stem and branch producing each other, the qi smooth and warm. With Bǐng in the year and month pillars as well, three Bǐng fires stand in a row, and though Start of Autumn has passed and metal qi begins to rule, today's fire remains generous. The auspicious spirits gather in force: Sānhé 三合 governs harmony and completion, Tiānxǐ 天喜 governs celebration, Tiānyī 天医 governs healing, Xùshì 续世 governs long life and descendants, and Mǔcāng 母仓 governs nurture — while the day god Jīnkuì 金匮 presides over storing and gathering, so marriage, moving into a house, opening business and signing contracts are all favored, and the avoidance list is short. Only Yuèyàn 月厌 and Dìhuǒ 地火 rule contrary and stagnant qi, and Xuèjì 血忌 warns especially against drawing blood — so planting and stove-building should wait, and acupuncture and travel are best avoided. Whatever wedding, move or opening has long been prepared, today's momentum favors bringing it home.

Folklore

Today's day god is Jīnkuì 金匮 — and Jīnkuì also stands among today's auspicious spirits — one of the six auspicious spirits of the Yellow Path. In traditional almanac lore, twelve day spirits take turns presiding: Qīnglóng 青龙, Míngtáng 明堂, Jīnkuì 金匮, Tiāndé 天德, Yùtáng 玉堂 and Sīmìng 司命 form the six auspicious spirits of the Yellow Path, and the days they rule are called Yellow Path lucky days. Kuì means a cabinet for keeping things; in ancient times the state's weightiest documents and treasures were stored in "gold cabinets and stone chambers" — this is where the spirit takes its name, governing storage, accumulation and safekeeping. So when Jīnkuì presides, almanac masters deem it especially favorable for matters of receiving and holding: signing contracts, collecting wealth, storing grain, placing valuables — all answer to its image. Today's favorable list includes opening business, trading, signing contracts, moving into a house and setting up beds — matters of laying foundations and settling in place, answering exactly to Jīnkuì's storing nature. With Tiānyī 天医 attending at the same time, old practice also held such days fitting for prayers of blessing and longevity. One thing to remember: Jīnkuì governs gathering in, not giving out — receiving wealth is favored today, but large expenditures or lending to others still deserve a second thought. This is how the spirits are read: each governs its own domain, applied to affairs to hit the mark.

Metaphysics Insight

Tomorrow marks White Dew (Bái Lù 白露), the 15th of the 24 solar terms and the third of autumn. It signals the end of early autumn and the beginning of mid-autumn. As the ancient text *Yueling Qishi'er Hou Ji Jie* states: "In the eighth month festival... yin qi grows heavier, and dew condenses into white." As temperatures drop, moisture in the night air condenses into glistening white dewdrops — hence the name. In Five Elements theory, after White Dew, Metal qi reaches its peak, Wood qi is restrained, and Water qi begins to regenerate. Those with weak Wood in their birth charts often experience more ups and downs during the two weeks following White Dew; it is wise to keep a low profile and conserve energy. Conversely, those with strong Metal and Water elements see their fortunes improve and can seize opportunities. Folk wisdom says "After White Dew, bare no more skin," reminding people to add clothing in the morning and evening — a tradition aligned with Chinese medicine's principle of "harvesting in autumn and storing in winter."

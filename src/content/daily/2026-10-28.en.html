Metaphysics Insight

Yesterday the White Tiger of the Black Path left the floor, and today the Jade Hall of the Yellow Path takes its seal — with this, five of the Yellow Path's six have taken their seats (the Azure Dragon, the Bright Hall, the Golden Casket, the Heavenly Virtue and now the Jade Hall), with the Commander of Destiny alone to come; and in the roster of the twelve sky-spirits, the Black Path's White Tiger is followed at once by the Yellow Path's Jade Hall — after the malign, the auspicious; when the black is spent, the yellow returns: the very rhythm of yin and yang rising and falling. And of all the names in this celestial rotation, the Jade Hall has the grandest résumé in the world of letters. The first register belongs to the calendar: the Jade Hall is one of the Yellow Path six, presiding over documents, glad tidings and the help of patrons — in the old art of choosing days, submitting memorials and offering counsel, sitting for examinations, taking a master or entering school, drawing up contracts and setting pen to paper were often laid upon a Jade Hall day, for its noble air of letters carries what is sought to its destination; in today's favored list worship and consecrating stand in view, and though the old almanacs kept no column for paperwork, on a day with the Jade Hall shining, signing and submitting find their very hour. The second register belongs to the human world: the "Hall of Jade" traveled from the palaces of heaven down into the groves of learning — the Han palace of Weiyang held a Jade Hall chamber where men of letters awaited the imperial summons, named alongside the Golden Horse Gate, whence the phrase "Golden Horse and Jade Hall"; and in the Chunhua years of the Northern Song, Emperor Taizong wrote the four characters "Yùtáng zhī Shǔ" — "The Studio of the Jade Hall" — in flying-white script and bestowed them upon the Imperial Academy, and from then on "Jade Hall" became the Academy's cherished other name, with a scholar's lifelong glory to have "once entered the Jade Hall": the heavenly Jade Hall is the mansion of immortal officials, the earthly Jade Hall the wellspring of literature — one name straddling heaven and the human world, and holding the word "noble" in both. On this deep-autumn Hài day with the Jade Hall keeping the watch and the Pig standing on its natal ground — documents and audiences, reverence and rest, all belong to today; as for the day the Commander of Destiny comes to close the Yellow Path's roster — that is a tale for a few days hence.

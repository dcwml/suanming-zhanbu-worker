On a Rén Yín day, Rén is yang water — the water of rivers on the run; Yín is yang wood, the wood of the tiger crouched in mountain forests — Rén water seated on Yín, and Yín hides jiǎ wood within: water moistens and wood grows, a picture of generation and circulation, of wealth channels running free. The Na Yin is Goldfoil Metal 金箔金 — Rén Yín and Guǐ Mǎo share it: metal at its thinnest, laid as foil upon the face of vessels, its luster outwardly shown, complete only when it has something to cling to — like the thin veneer of respectability on a shopfront, handsome yet needing substance beneath to hold it. The day god arrives at the Heavenly Cage 天牢, one of the Black Path baleful ones: the Heavenly Cage is the spirit of the prison, governing confinement and obstruction. Yet today the favorable list runs to seventeen matters — opening a business, taking in wealth, trade, contracts, breaking ground, relocation, entering a new home: nearly every matter of "movement" stands favorable, and the secret lies wholly in the auspicious spirits. Nine stand in rank, and the first of them is the Six Harmonies 六合 — today's Yín day and the Hài month branch form exactly the Yín–Hài pairing: the month commander and the day branch in combination, the qi of heaven and earth joining, and the Black Path's "cage" more than half unfastened by that one union; the Six Harmonies govern accord and concluded deals, hence trade and contracts stand favorable. Wǔfù 五富, the Five Abundances, governs overflowing wealth, hence taking in wealth and shipping goods stand favorable; Tiānyuàn 天愿, the Heaven's Wish, governs the fulfillment of hopes; Wǔhé 五合, the Five Unions, governs accord in all affairs; Jīntáng 金堂, the Golden Hall, governs the peace of the house — hence relocation, entering a new home and installing incense stand favorable. "Bùjiāng" 不将 is the very highest auspicious spirit for choosing wedding days — yin and yáng unopposed, nothing thwarted; Míngfèiduì 鸣吠对 marks the dog's answering bark and the open road, hence travel stands favorable; Shídé 时德 and Xiàngrì 相日 attend — the virtue of the seasons and the mutual aid of yin and yáng, every matter falling due. Six baleful ones stand in list: Sǐshén 死神, the Death Spirit, governs the breath of dying, and Fùrì 复日, the Repeated Day, governs what is doubled — hence the whole string of funerary and netherworld matters, burial, funeral rites, tending graves, building a temple, thanking the earth, together with visiting the sick before the sickbed, all stand avoided; Yóuhuò 游祸, the Wandering Calamity, governs calamity that roams without fixed seat, and Wǔxū 五虚, the Five Voids, governs what is hollow and wasteful — hence works of raising and craft stand avoided as well: building the stove, felling trees, making roof beams. Yín is ground where wood's qi runs strong and Hài is wood's birth-womb; the wood command holds the season, and to fell it or beam it wounds the living qi. Clash the Monkey, the Sha lies North: Yín and Shēn stand opposed — with Monkey people today, guard against contention above all and go slow on partnership; northern journeys take a slower step. The Heavenly Cage holds the day and the Six Harmonies stand at its head; today's measure is this: with accord in the lead, nearly all matters stand favorable — only the funerary and the netherworld, and the raising of wood upon it, keep respectfully apart.

Zodiac Fortune · Tiger 虎

Today's earthly branch is Yín, which corresponds to the Tiger — making the Tiger the protagonist of the day: it is the Tiger's natal day, with the Yín hours before dawn on its watch; better still, today's Yín day forms the Six Harmonies with the Hài month branch, and the Tiger's natal day is one "joined with the month's command," doubling the lucky qi. The Six Harmonies attend, with the Heaven's Wish, the Five Abundances and the Five Unions ranked beside: in career, today is the most favorable day of late for "movement" — opening a business, trade and concluding contracts all stand favorable, so sign, negotiate, launch and take orders with a free hand; partnership above all: the Six Harmonies govern accord, and a new partner or a joint venture settled today makes the luckiest of agreements; those in post find talks, presentations and business trips all smooth, for travel too stands favorable. In wealth, the Five Abundances attend and the Wealth God sits South: taking in wealth and shipping goods stand favorable — collecting payments, settling accounts, shipping stock, gathering what is owed all go well; the southern quarter favors talking money, and the Joy God sits in the South as well, so close a deal and catch up with an old friend in one breath; with the Five Voids in the list, let income and outgo keep their measure: hold a little in reserve against large spending, and speculative moves still wait. In love, "Bùjiāng" holds the day — yin and yáng unopposed, most auspicious for marriage: registration, engagement, a formal proposal may all use today; meeting family and friends stands favorable, and for the unattached a dinner on the southern side of town is worth the trip — under the light of the Six Harmonies, luck in love comes mostly from old acquaintances. Relocation, entering a new home and installing incense stand favorable: moving house, settling into a new place, setting the incense burner in its place — all in season; breaking ground stands favorable, and small repairs may go ahead. In the body, Rén water answers to the kidneys and bladder, Yín wood to the liver and gallbladder — after Minor Snow the water is cold and the wood frozen: lie early to nourish the liver's blood, and drink warm water often to guard the kidney's qi; warping and weaving stands favorable, so a round of stretching or a long soak for the feet suits the day exactly. The funerary matters of the avoided list hardly touch the Tiger — save one: visiting the sick, which may wait a day or two. Clash the Monkey, Sha North: Yín and Shēn stand opposed — with Monkey people, guard against partnership and contention, and northern journeys take a slower step.

Metaphysics Insight

The first auspicious spirit in today's list is the Six Harmonies 六合 — not a loose word of good cheer, but a precise system of "pairings" within the twelve earthly branches. Taken two by two they join into exactly six pairs: Zǐ with Chǒu, Yín with Hài, Mǎo with Xū, Chén with Yǒu, Sì with Shēn, Wǔ with Wèi — hence the name, the Six Harmonies. Whence do they come? The old account takes its image from "sun and moon sharing one mansion" — the mansion the sun traverses and the station where the moon overtakes it meet six times in a year, and the ancients mapped that sky onto the branches, and the six pairs followed. In the discipline of choosing days, the Six Harmonies are the supreme lucky spirit of the month: on any day the Six Harmonies hold, accord, concluded deals and marriage all stand favorable — that trade, contracts and meeting family and friends stand favorable in today's almanac rests on exactly this ground. Nor is the harmony blind: within each pairing there is a distinction of generation and conquest. Yín and Hài join and Hài water generates Yín wood — a union "with generation within," the tighter joined the stronger it grows; Zǐ and Chǒu join and Chǒu earth conquers Zǐ water — a union "with conquest within," holding its measure of restraint. Today's Yín–Hài union is of the former kind: the month branch's Hài water feeds the day branch's Yín wood, water moistening and wood flourishing, and so today's "joining" is exceptionally clean — a partnership joined with generation, a contract concluded as a living agreement. The old books of fate, weighing matches by zodiac sign, looked first to the Six Harmonies as well: Tiger with Pig, Rabbit with Dog, Dragon with Rooster — the folk phrase "harmony marriage" 六合婚 comes from here, and a Tiger matched with a Pig was called in the old registers a marriage of the first rank. In today's almanac the Heavenly Cage of the Black Path would lock things fast, yet the Six Harmonies, the Five Abundances, the Heaven's Wish and the rest of the nine unlatch it layer by layer, leaving only the funerary and netherworld matters avoided — proof that the almanac's judgment is never a single veto, but a board game in which lucky and baleful spirits contend and their weight is reckoned together. The subtlety of the Six Harmonies lies in the one word "join": heaven and earth have their joinings, human affairs have theirs — on a day of joining, help another's good work to its end, and see your own through.

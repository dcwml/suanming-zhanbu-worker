Today's earthly branch is Yǒu, which corresponds to the Rooster — making the Rooster the zodiac protagonist of the day. Yǒu is the purest vein of metal among the twelve branches, and with autumn metal holding command and the Jade Hall of the Yellow Path shining above, Rooster people should find their bearing bright and their minds clear: travel runs especially smooth, and distant errands, official journeys and visits meet helpers along the way. With the Official's Day and the Six Instruments alongside, reports, presentations, interviews and negotiations are all scenes that reward composure — measured manners and proper courtesy readily win the favor of elders and superiors; the Benefit of Posterity shelters matters concerning elders and the young at home. The wealth star sits North: earned income holds steady, and prudence befits financial moves — yet with the Month Builder and Earth Treasury presiding, no major building: hold off on renovations, groundbreaking and moving house; with the Month Loathing and its Pair at hand, romance easily breeds quarrels — proposals and engagements would do better on another day, and gatherings with kin are best kept simple. The day clashes with the Rabbit and the Sha lies East: those born in the Year of the Rabbit should keep a low profile in all matters, avoid journeys toward the East, and refrain from quarrels with Roosters.

Metaphysics Insight

Today's stamp of "auspicious" comes from the Jade Hall — the folk phrase "a Yellow Path day" stands on a complete rotating system of twelve path spirits. The ancients anchored the Azure Dragon by the month branch and counted through the twelve stations, giving the Azure Dragon, the Bright Hall, Heavenly Punishment, the Vermilion Bird, the Gold Chest, Heavenly Virtue (also called Treasured Light), the White Tiger, the Jade Hall, the Heavenly Prison, the Dark Warrior, the Commander of Life and Hook and Argument, one presiding each day in turn: the seats of the Azure Dragon, Bright Hall, Gold Chest, Heavenly Virtue, Jade Hall and Commander of Life are the "Yellow Path", favoring gladness and smooth going, while those of Heavenly Punishment, Vermilion Bird, White Tiger, Heavenly Prison, Dark Warrior and Hook and Argument are the "Black Path", marking friction and setback — the old saying that on Yellow Path days matters may proceed, and on Black Path days nothing should be undertaken, descends from exactly this. The Jade Hall is the noble spirit among the six Yellow Path seats, its image the favor of patrons and the flourishing of documents and letters; the old almanacs commend its day for meeting the honored, setting the bed, entering school and felicitous gatherings, so with the Official's Day and the Six Instruments beside it, public business and travel both receive its benefit. Yet the Yellow Path is no all-clear pass: today the Jade Hall shines, but the Month Loathing and its Pair stand in the inauspicious column, and weddings remain forbidden all the same. The old almanac never reads a single spirit alone — first the path spirit sets the frame, then the officers and every lesser spirit sift the ledger: good fortune hiding harm, harm hiding good fortune — that layered weighing is precisely the date-selector's art of steering between them.

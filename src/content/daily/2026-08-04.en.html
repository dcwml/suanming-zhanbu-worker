Folklore

Today's ruling day spirit is Qīnglóng (青龙), the Azure Dragon, first of the six auspicious "Yellow Path" deities. Traditional date-selection (择日) assigns one of twelve day spirits to each day based on the relationship between the month branch and the day branch: Qīnglóng, Míngtáng, Jīnkuì, Tiāndé, Yùtáng, and Sīmìng form the six auspicious spirits, while Tiānxíng, Zhūquè, Báihǔ, Tiānláo, Xuánwǔ, and Gōuchén form the six inauspicious ones. Qīnglóng heads the auspicious six, belongs to the Wood element, and presides over joy and good fortune — folk tradition holds that "when the Azure Dragon rules the day, all matters are auspicious." Yet that blessing sets only the overall tone: specific activities must still be checked against the day's own lists. Today, even with Qīnglóng on duty, marriage and ground-breaking remain taboo — a fine example of how the almanac layers its judgments.

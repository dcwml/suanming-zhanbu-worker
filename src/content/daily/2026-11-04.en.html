Yesterday the Hall of Light kept the Yellow Path with the double virtues shining; today the round of the twelve spirits reaches its third seat — Tiānxíng, the Heavenly Punisher, first of the Black Path six, takes over after two Yellow Path days. Yet open the almanac and the favored list stands full at thirteen matters, and the subtlety lies in the count: the Moon's Void, the Heavenly Grace, the Four Ministers, the Triple Harmony, the Heavenly Joy, the Heavenly Physician, the Heavenly Granary, the No Bride Unclaimed, the Peace of Mind and the Crying Hound — ten auspicious spirits hold the watch while Tiānxíng stands alone; the almanac has never judged a day by a single spirit but weighs the stars together — many blessings pressing on one punisher make a good day, and a lone punisher cannot outshine a hall full of light. Each spirit answers its own works: the Heavenly Joy governs celebration and the No Bride Unclaimed is the master list of weddings — marriages, betrothal pledges and presenting gifts stand three in a row; yesterday the Blood Taboo held the floor and the wedding was set aside while the pledge went first, today the obstacle withdraws and marriage returns to the favored list — the very landing point of "wed on a luckier day." The Crying Hound makes a day fit for breaking ground and burial, and worship and gathering with kin ride on the Heavenly Grace; opening a business, drawing up contracts, travel, moving residence and entering a new home draw on the fire trio and the paired wealth and officer — the Wǔ of the day half-joins the Xū of the month in the Yín–Wǔ–Xū fire trio, and the Triple Harmony's name matches its deed. Each of the five avoided matters has its reason: raising the ridgepole, roofing a house and setting the frame horses are works upon a dwelling; consecration opens the eyes of a newly made deity's image; fitting coffin wood brings timber to its final form — all five are the trades of axe, saw and chisel, and Tiānxíng governs punishment and the wound of blades, so they are set aside. Three days remain until Start of Winter (Nov 7): autumn metal about to fade, winter water soon to come — Rén water comes into season, and travel and trade ride the rising tide. Clash the Rat, the Sha lies North: Zǐ and Wǔ stand opposed — keep clear of contention with Rat people, and let northern journeys take a slower step.

Metaphysics Insight

Yesterday the Hall of Light kept its second day on the Yellow Path; today the round of the twelve spirits reaches its third seat — Tiānxíng, the Heavenly Punisher, the first of the Black Path six (Tiānxíng, Zhūquè, Bái Hǔ, Tiānláo, Xuánwǔ, Gōuchén) to take the floor in person. The name says it plainly: "xíng" is the punishment of penalties — in the old reading it governs judgment and execution, lawsuits and official charges, the wound of blades and the shed of blood, and every matter of tribunals and edged tools falls within its charge. It stands directly after the two great Yellow Path spirits, the Azure Dragon and the Hall of Light, and the order has not changed in a thousand years — after two Yellow Path days a Black Path day must follow, just as in the court of old, when the hall of audience had concluded its business, the hall of justice took its turn: the ancients read the heavens as a court in session, the Azure Dragon the sovereign's retinue, the Hall of Light the chamber of counsel, and Tiānxíng the minister of punishments. The season makes it more telling still: after Frost's Descent and before Start of Winter is precisely the season of the old "executions after autumn" — spring bears birth, summer growth, autumn the harvest and winter the store, and the axe of the law waited for autumn's end, when the stern breath of the season moved of itself; that Tiānxíng keeps its watch exactly in this window — spirit and season answering one another. And yet the favored list of the day offers a second lesson: with the lone Punisher on duty, the list of favored matters still runs to thirteen — the almanac has never judged a day by one spirit but weighs the stars in concert: with the Triple Harmony, the Heavenly Joy, the Heavenly Physician, the Crying Hound and the No Bride Unclaimed among ten auspicious names, a lone punisher cannot outweigh a hall full of light — and so "a Black Path day is not always an ill day": when blessings outnumber the punishers the day reads good, when punishers crowd out the blessings it reads ill — this is the almanac's arithmetic of "good stars checking the bad." On a Tiānxíng day, three refrains are enough: raise no lawsuits, enter no quarrels, lift no axe or chisel — keep peace as the first virtue, and the punisher's breath dissolves of itself.

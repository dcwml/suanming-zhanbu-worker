Start of Autumn has passed and Metal qi now rules, cutting Wood — today the way forward is to stand firm, and to act with reverence.

Zodiac Fortune · Rabbit

Today's branch is Mǎo (卯), which corresponds to the Rabbit — today's featured zodiac. Those born in a Rabbit year sit squarely on the day's branch, a position of authority in itself — except that today's inauspicious spirits assemble in force: Tiānlì 天吏, Zhìsǐ 致死, Sìfèi 四废 and Wǔxū 五虚 stand in a ring, and Zhūquè 朱雀 rules inauspicious, so even as the featured native the Rabbit's qi runs to gathering in rather than pressing forward. Career-wise, launching, signing or expanding is all out of step today; tidying current affairs, clearing backlog and giving what must end a proper ending, on the contrary, is precisely in tune with the day's qi. Wǔhé 五合 brings human harmony and quiet help from benefactors — if old business needs mediation or old friction needs smoothing, let today's spirit of union work slowly and gently. Financially, the Wealth God dwells Northeast, yet Tiānlì and Zhìsǐ warn of losses: small takings are fine, while major investments, loans and guarantees should wait. In relationships, heed "clash Rooster, Sha East": avoid disputes with those born in a Rooster year and long journeys toward the east; Zhūquè governs gossip and quarrels of the tongue, so words today should be measured and rumors left untrusted. After Start of Autumn, Metal qi presses down on Wood — Rabbits should mind liver and temper alike, resting early and keeping quiet.

Folklore

Among today's auspicious spirits stands Wǔhé 五合 — one of the most personable stars in the Chinese almanac. Its name comes from the Five Combinations of the heavenly stems: among the ten stems, five pairs join in union — Jiǎ with Jǐ to form Earth, Yǐ with Gēng to form Metal, Bǐng with Xīn to form Water, Dīng with Rén to form Wood, Wù with Guǐ to form Fire. "Union" (hé 合) means harmony, matching and mutual affinity, so when Wǔhé attends a day, almanac masters deem it favorable for meetings, negotiations and matchmaking — which is why this spirit so often marks the chosen dates of weddings and engagements. Curiously, today's own pillars carry union within them: among the stems, Yǐ joins with Gēng, and among the branches, Mǎo joins with Xū. Yet even a star of union has its limits — Wǔhé attends today, and still the almanac reads "all other matters best avoided", because Tiānlì 天吏, Zhìsǐ 致死 and Sìfèi 四废 hold the field and the inauspicious tide outweighs the auspicious. Each auspicious spirit governs its own domain and has its own bounds — which is precisely how the almanac should be read: auspicious spirits, malefic spirits and the favorable/avoidance lists considered together, never one lucky star taken as license to act. All Wǔhé can accomplish today is a gentle closing, not a forceful opening.

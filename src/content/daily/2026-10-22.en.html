Yesterday the Azure Dragon opened the lecture on the Yellow Path; today the Bright Hall takes the next shift. Jǐ earth sits upon Sì — Jǐ is the garden earth, and the Bǐng fire hidden within Sì is precisely its seal star, a vein of fire-and-earth warmth running through the killing crispness of the Xū month's metal; and the Na Yin, Wood of the Great Forest, reaches its second day — the forest grown full, ready to shelter human affairs. The Bright Hall of the Yellow Path cherishes noble helpers and favors meeting the great, while Yīndé urges quiet kindness and Xùshì urges the continuing line — so all thirteen favored matters gather under one word, nurture: worship to honor the forebears, prayer for an heir to continue the generations, new members to swell the household, friends and kin to warm the heart; doors installed and the bed set to settle the home, wealth collected and stored to feed the purse; and spinning, livestock, hunting, releasing water and harvesting honey are all the quiet farm work of the ninth month — deep autumn, the honey ripe, the season of gathering in. The avoided column, seven matters long, is one approach to Heaven and one grand rite after another: Yóuhuò, the Wandering Misfortune, drifts from place to place, so prayers and fasts hold off their devotions; the Repeated Day answers to today's own Sì branch — yang ends at Sì, yin ends at Hài, and what ends must begin again, hence "repeated" — with old rule above all forbidding burial and breaking ground to be done twice over, and Xuèjì forbidding the drawing of blood beside it, so all funeral matters stand aside; and betrothal gifts, the concluded engagement, marriage rites and moving into a new home — the four great ceremonies — give way with them. Tomorrow Frost's Descent crosses the mark, the last term of autumn, and the cold will gather strength day by day — with the auspicious spirit in the hall, gather in early and gather close, and let nothing new be opened.

Zodiac Fortune · Snake 蛇

Today's earthly branch is Sì, which corresponds to the Snake — making the Snake the zodiac protagonist of the day. Snake people stand on their own natal ground today, with the Bright Hall of the Yellow Path shining overhead — and the Bright Hall is the star of noble helpers, favoring audiences with the great: calling on elders, paying respects to persons of standing, interviews, any meeting of consequence — today the word spoken wins the kindly eye, and a call in person is worth a thousand written pleas. Wealth is the day's brightest line: collecting wealth stands favored and the Wealth God sits Due North — collecting debts, storing funds and taking stock of savings all run smooth; only Tiānzéi, the Heavenly Thief, stands among the inauspicious — settle every exchange of goods and money early in the day, carry nothing heavy of value by night, read every transfer and contract twice, and guard against deceit as much as theft. In company, meeting friends and kin and admitting new members to the household both stand favored: gather a few old companions for a small reunion, or make ready for a new face joining the family — all in season; and with Yīndé in the list, lend a hand in secret and expect nothing back — the blessing returns of itself. For health, mind the Blood Taboo: blood draws, acupuncture and tattoos — anything that draws blood — wait for another day; Frost's Descent draws near, so add a layer and keep moist, and do not toy with the autumn chill. The day clashes with the Pig and the Sha lies East: keep clear of contention with Pig people and entrust them no heavy charge, and let long journeys or moves toward the East wait. Marriage rites, betrothal gifts, moving home and burial — every grand ceremony gives way today: the snake coils at its den and stills its body — the Bright Hall's nobility is spent on meeting people and keeping what is won, not on opening what is new.

Metaphysics Insight

Yesterday the Azure Dragon opened the lecture on the six Yellow Path spirits; today the Bright Hall follows in step — so let the roster continue. The six Yellow Path spirits — Qīnglóng the Azure Dragon, Míngtáng the Bright Hall, Jīnkuì the Golden Casket, Bǎoguāng the Treasured Light, Yùtáng the Jade Hall and Sīmìng the Director of Destinies — are all named for the most honored images of the human world: the Azure Dragon is the quintessence of the Eastern sky's dragon, while the Bright Hall is the palace where the Son of Heaven governed in state. The institution reaches back to the Rites of Zhou; when the King of Xí asked Mencius, one sentence settled its purpose — "the Bright Hall is the hall of the true king": there he proclaimed government and teaching, received the feudal lords, and sacrificed to the ancestors. The Ballad of Mùlán — "returning to see the Son of Heaven, the Son of Heaven sat in the Bright Hall" — carried the great hall into every household on the wings of the song. In the fourth year of Chuígǒng, Wǔ Zétiān tore down the Qiányuán Hall to raise her Bright Hall over a hundred chi high, naming it the Palace of Myriad Images and Divine Splendor — lifting the ritual building to its grandest page in history. The date-selectors named the second shift of the Yellow Path after the most honored hall of the human world to capture its very image — upright, square, bright and open: the old almanac-praise reads, the Bright Hall is the star of noble helpers, the star of bright assistance — favoring audiences with the great, favoring all going forth, with every undertaking brought to completion. And the marvel is that the Bright Hall stands in the sky as well: the Book of Jin's Treatise on Astronomy records three Bright Hall stars southwest of the Tàiwēi Enclosure, the very image of "the palace where the Son of Heaven governs" — one hall on earth, three stars in heaven, and the rites of the human world join straight onto the star-offices of the sky. Today the Bright Hall holds the day, on the very eve of Frost's Descent — autumn nears its end and the hall stays bright: on a day that favors audiences with the great, see the people who should be seen and renew the ties that should be renewed — one word exchanged in the hall outdoes ten visits paid afterward.

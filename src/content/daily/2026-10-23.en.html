The Frost's Descent promised yesterday crosses the mark today — the last term of autumn, when the air grows stern and thickens and dew congeals into frost. Gēng metal sits upon Wǔ: Gēng is the axe-metal, Wǔ the fire of the furnace, and the Dīng fire hidden within Wǔ is the very furnace-flame that tempers Gēng into a vessel — with Xū month earth thriving to birth metal, the Metal qi of Frost's Descent stands at full strength; and the Na Yin, Roadside Earth, is the open and level road that bears the traveler on. Tiānxíng of the Black Path holds the day, a lone punisher on duty that would counsel restraint; yet by chance eight auspicious spirits arrive together — the Moon's Grace, the Triple Harmony, Heavenly Joy, the Heavenly Physician, the Heavenly Granary, Bùjiàng, Yào'ān and Míngfèi — for Wǔ and the Xū month half-unite into a frame, and numbers outweigh the lone malign: the favored column therefore swings wide. Bùjiàng and Heavenly Joy preside over marriage, the Triple Harmony and the Heavenly Granary over business openings, trade, contracts and plaques, while Míngfèi and the Triple Harmony permit breaking earth for graves and raising tombstones — the red affairs and the white alike find their footing. The six avoided matters each keep their measure: visiting the sick avoids the punisher's air of wounds and blood; felling trees, laying foundations, making beams and building roofs are all works of wood and stirring earth, and with the trees yellowing and falling under a stern and gathering air, construction runs against the season; buying livestock, too, may wait. On this day that closes autumn, the twenty-seven favored matters are best dispatched by daylight — after Frost's Descent the days shorten, each one shorter than the last, and great affairs favor the early over the late.

Metaphysics Insight

Today Frost's Descent crosses the mark — the final solar term of autumn — so let us speak of the most easily misunderstood name in the twenty-four terms. Read literally, "Frost's Descent" suggests frost falling from heaven — yet frost never falls from the sky. In the night the ground, the plants and the tiles and stones radiate their heat away and grow cold, and the water vapor near the surface, meeting that chill, skips the liquid stage and crystallizes straight into ice upon leaf and eave — that is frost. The ancients, seeing every branch furred white, took it to descend like rain and snow, hence the word "descent"; the Explanations of the Seventy-Two Pentads states it precisely: "In the middle month of the ninth, the air grows stern and thickens, and the dew congeals into frost" — dew condenses into beads, frost crystallizes into gems; a single character apart, and the cold has deepened a full layer. The three pentads of Frost's Descent: first, the jackals offer up their prey, laying the beasts they catch in rows as if in sacrifice before they eat; second, the plants yellow and shed, the autumn leaves taking leave of the mountain; third, the hibernating creatures all bow their heads, entering their burrows, neither eating nor stirring, quietly awaiting winter's store — heaven and earth, from top to bottom, gather themselves in together. In folk custom Frost's Descent is the gate of tonics — the proverb holds that toning up at Frost's Descent outdoes toning up at the Start of Winter; in Fújiàn and Táiwān families eat persimmons on the day, for "a ripe persimmon at Frost's Descent keeps the runny nose away"; and in Guǎngxī the Zhuàng people keep their annual Frost's Descent Festival, celebrating the harvest and thanking the gods. As for the almanac: the solar terms are fixed by the sun's ecliptic longitude, and Frost's Descent is longitude two hundred ten degrees; the almanac's months heed not the new moons but the terms alone — from Cold Dew to the Start of Winter all is the Xū month, and Frost's Descent is the Xū month's middle term, the very boundary of deep autumn: from here the Metal qi gathers in and the Water qi stirs toward birth, and when the Start of Winter arrives the Hǎi month's water-rule begins. Today the favored column swings wide, with marriage, business openings and burial all provided for — the very picture of "harvest in autumn, store away for winter": Frost's Descent is the term of gathering in. Collect what should be collected, store what should be stored, and wait with composure for the Start of Winter.

On a Rén Chén day, Rén is yang water — the rushing water of rivers, lakes and seas; Chén is the reservoir of water — within the Three Bureau of Shēn–Zǐ–Chén, Chén is precisely water's grave-store, and Rén water sits atop its own storehouse, in the month of Hài when water reigns in season: the water is at full flood, yet with somewhere to return. The day god arrives at Sīmìng 司命, the Director of Destinies — a Yellow Path auspicious day: Sīmìng is the god who holds the span of human lives, governing peace and clear fortune. Yet today the two lists stand as yesterday's mirror reversed: four auspicious spirits face five inauspicious — the Yellow Path's light is present, but it cannot scatter a full column of "avoid": today favors stillness over motion, the small over the great. First the auspicious: Yángdé 阳德, Yang Virtue, guards the merit of the daylight world, and offerings to gods and ancestors stand exactly in place; Jiěshén 解神, the Resolving Spirit, masters untying grudges, dissolving calamity and mending illness — whence the "clearing away ill fortune" in the favorable list: removing bad luck and closing out old affairs are in their very hour. Bùjiàng 不将 held its post yesterday and holds it again today, yet marriage still falls into the avoid column — because Fùrì 复日, the Day of Return, stands in the way: the Day of Return is a day of repetition, and the old folk rule holds that great matters done on it may "come round again" — a name that marriage cares not to hear; so though No-Clash stands present, a kindly spirit cannot carry the single word "repeat." Then the inauspicious: Wǔmù 五墓, the Five Burials, heads the list — Chén is the grave-store of the element, and with the Five Burials on duty the storehouse closes: great undertakings and building work all run counter to closing and storing, so breaking ground, construction, raising the ridgebeam, moving into a new home, relocation, burial and breaking the earth fall into the avoid column together. Tǔfú 土符, the Earth Talisman, is the ban-seal of the earth gods — breaking ground and piercing the earth carry a second prohibition; digging wells and opening ponds pierce the earth for its springs, building boats sets water-work in motion, and building the stove is earth-work and fire-work — all within the ban. Xiǎohào 小耗, the Lesser Depletion, wastes wealth, and Tiānzéi 天贼, Heaven's Thief, steals it — so opening a market is avoided: no opening of shops today, and large outlays, easy trust in side-door ventures — let them wait; travel too is avoided: journeys abroad risk loss and theft — let distant plans take a slower step. The favorable list numbers six, all small matters of "care and repair": hairdressing and grooming, acupuncture and moxibustion, trimming the nails of hands and feet — all small work upon oneself; admitting new members to the household — a child joining the family, extra hands being hired — stands favorable; and sacrificial offerings, with the Director of Destinies on duty, stand exactly in place. The Na Yin is Chángliúshuǐ 长流水, Long Flowing Water: a slender stream that flows on without break — today's way lies in the single word "nourish": the great avoided, the small favored, still water runs deep, and strength gathers for its hour. Clash the Dog, the Sha lies South: Chén and Xū stand opposed — keep clear of contention and partnership with Dog people, and let southern journeys take a slower step.

Zodiac Fortune · Dragon 龙

Today's earthly branch is Chén, which corresponds to the Dragon — making the Dragon the protagonist of the day: the Dragon of Chén keeps its watch, and Chén is the reservoir of water; with the month of Hài in flood and Rén water seated on its storehouse, the dragon that has water lies hidden in the deep — today's dragon is a dragon entered into the abyss. The Five Burials stand on duty and the dragon enters the storehouse: favor diving over leaping — Dragon folk today should sheathe their edge and keep to stillness, letting great matters wait; breaking ground on projects, moving house or home, journeys afar — none of these for today. What today favors is "clearing and care": with the Resolving Spirit on duty, untying old obstructions, closing out old affairs and setting old accounts straight are exactly in season; hairdressing and grooming, trimming nails — small repairs upon oneself, nourishing the great by way of the small. In wealth, the Lesser Depletion and Heaven's Thief stand double in the list and opening a market is avoided: no opening of shops today, no large outlays, and investments and signatures take a step back; count the day's accounts before dark and keep watch on wallet and papers — the thief's harm is guarded against in small particulars. In love, marriages are avoided and the Day of Return stands in the list: betrothals and registrations take a slower step — the old rule says matters done on a day of repetition may "come round again," and good things need not rush today; for the unwed Dragon, deepening acquaintance is well, grand gestures not yet. Admitting new members stands favorable: welcoming a new member to the family or hiring extra hands is exactly in season. In the body, acupuncture and moxibustion stand favorable — tending chronic ailments and opening the meridians are in their hour; Chén answers the stomach, and in the Chén hours, seven to nine in the morning, the Stomach meridian keeps watch — breakfast warm and hot, for one good morning meal is the Dragon's most practical homework today; Rén water answers the kidneys, and with the month of Hài in flood, after the Start of Winter: nourish the kidneys and store essence, lie early and rise late. The Yellow Path of the Director of Destinies is today's ground color: the great avoided, the small all at ease — "peace and clear fortune" is the Dragon's fortune in four words. Clash the Dog, Sha South: with Dog people today, keep clear of contention and go slow on partnership; southern routes take a slower step.

Metaphysics Insight

Yesterday the rabbit pounded its elixir; today the Dragon of Chén keeps the watch — the one creature in the twelve that exists only in imagination; so let us tell of the dragon. First the character 辰 itself: the Shuowen Jiezi 说文解字, the Explanations of Graphs, says: "Chén means to shake. In the third month the yang qi stirs, thunder rolls and cracks, the people's farming season arrives, and the ten thousand things come forth." The root sense of Chén is thunder and stirring, yang on the move — the dragon is the essence of thunder and rain, riding the clouds and spreading the rain, the very image of this. Then look at the Qian hexagram, first of all sixty-four in the Yijing, every one of its six lines imaged by the dragon: the first line, "the hidden dragon — do not act," a dragon lurking in the deep, no motion advisable; the second, "the dragon appears in the field"; the fourth, "perhaps it leaps from the deep"; the fifth, "the flying dragon is in the sky"; the top line, "the arrogant dragon has regrets" — the dragon's whole life is a textbook of knowing the hour and the moment: before the time comes, lie hidden; when the time comes, leap; when force peaks, regret. Today the Dragon of Chén keeps the watch, the Five Burials stand on duty, fourteen matters stand avoided, stillness over motion — this is the almanac's footnote to those four characters, "the hidden dragon — do not act": not that the dragon lacks power, but that the hour has not come; to hide is not to retreat, but to gather strength. And look at today's day god Sīmìng: the Jiuge 九歌, the Nine Songs of the Songs of Chu, carries both a "Greater Sīmìng" and a "Lesser Sīmìng" — the Greater holds the span of human lives: "The lot of man's life has its fixity; who can force meetings and partings?"; the Lesser left the line "No sorrow so deep as parting while alive; no joy so great as a new friend" — hence Sīmìng became the god who governs fate, and the folk later honored the Kitchen God as "Sīmìng of the Eastern Kitchen," who keeps the ledger of one whole household's fortunes. And Chén is the reservoir of water — in the Bureau of Shēn–Zǐ–Chén, Chén is precisely water's grave-store: the dragon governs rainfall, but when the water returns to the storehouse, the rain does not fall — that wells, ponds and boats stand avoided today is the very image of "water in store, dragon in the deep": the slender, long-flowing water of Rén is gathered into the Chén store to be nursed slowly, until next year's spring thunder rolls and the yang qi stirs — then comes the flight. What stands favorable today is hairdressing and nail-trimming, acupuncture and clearing away ill fortune — while the hidden dragon lies in the deep is precisely the day for combing one's scales.
